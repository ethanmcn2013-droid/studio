## Goal

Have every graphic the launch needs designed, rendered at the right pixel
sizes, and held in one place the founder can browse — so launch week is a
matter of posting, not producing.

## Offer

One hundred and fifty assets drawn from the master-suite redesign, in
seventeen series: launch day, the three products, the product in use, plain
English, the switch, beliefs and numbers, founder and proof, profile and
link, six carousels, feature moments, asked-and-answered, stories, more
beliefs, link cards, launch week, proof scaffolding, and Home. They sit
beside the existing social system, venue kit, identity, cards, ambassador
kit and explorations in the Gallery — 341 assets, read from the files
themselves.

## Current blocker

Three founder decisions gate the rest:

1. **The launch date.** The five-card countdown (Q01–Q05) is rendered but
   cannot be scheduled, and it sets when everything else goes out.
2. **The lane sentences.** Thirteen assets — the whole D series and the
   J·2 carousel — show every board column carrying its own plain-English
   sentence. That is the master-suite review's recommendation, not what
   `/app/tasks` renders today. They hold until the port lands.
3. **Pricing.** No link card exists for `signalstudio.ie/pricing` because
   there is no price.

## Notes

Nothing in the R series or G05 posts until the words are real and the
person named has agreed — every one carries a visible SPECIMEN mark.

Dark-theme product frames were deliberately excluded: in the live app the
dark remap drops the lane counts, the card notes and the "of … done" words
from the board head, so a dark capture would misrepresent the product.
Recorded as a defect against the app, not a design choice here.

Source and gates live in the collateral repo (`launch-set/`): every asset
is generated from one grammar, and two gates run over the set — accent
spend per asset, and the app's own first-contact vocabulary applied to
social copy.
