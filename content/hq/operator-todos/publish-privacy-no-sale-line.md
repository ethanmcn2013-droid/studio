## The decision

The policy's "What we don't collect" covers ad trackers, fingerprinting,
session replay, marketing pixels, behavioural profiling and model training —
but makes no representation about selling or sharing personal data. The
student page's claim was narrowed on 2026-08-12 to what the policy backs.

If the commitment is real, one line in the policy restores the stronger
marketing sentence everywhere, e.g.: "We do not sell personal data, and we
do not share it with third parties for their own purposes. Subprocessors
process it only on our instructions."

## Steps

1. Confirm the commitment holds for every current subprocessor.
2. Add the line to /privacy (the legal pair lives in the studio repo) and
   bump the updated date.
3. Restore the no-sale sentence on /students rung 05 in the same PR.
