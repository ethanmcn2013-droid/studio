## What changed

The Certificate of Incorporation (CRO register number 823488, Monday 17
August 2026, LTD, Companies Act 2014) is on file. On 8 September the footer
on every public page gained the line "Signal Studio Limited. Registered in
Ireland, company number 823488." and the HQ company record moved to
incorporated.

## Steps

1. Confirm with the solicitor or accountant the operating entity for the
   Terms and the Privacy policy, and the effective date of the change.
2. Revise `/terms` ("operated by Ethan McNamara, a sole trader registered
   in Ireland") and `/privacy` to name Signal Studio Limited, its registered
   office as filed, and the CRO number.
3. Decide whether the registered office joins the footer particulars.
   Companies Act 2014 §151 lists the name, legal form, registered office,
   place of registration and number for company websites; the footer today
   carries the name, place and number.
4. Record the revision in the dispatch and mark this item done.
