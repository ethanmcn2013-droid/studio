## Decision

Review and approve the customer-facing privacy wording for files in Google
Drive. No privacy-policy wording has been changed as part of this work.
It must plainly state that files live in the named storage owner's personal
Google Drive, count against that account's quota, are visible to that owner,
and remain provider-owned when Signal Studio rows are deleted.

This is a legal and product promise, so an agent may draft options but must not
publish or silently change the policy without the founder's explicit approval.
