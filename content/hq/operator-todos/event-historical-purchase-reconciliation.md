## Scope and authority

The principal has adopted the owner-controlled Event policy under delegated authority. No further routine product selection is requested. This item concerns authoritative historical/provider records and unresolved record identity, not approval to continue engineering. It blocks historical designation and the evidence required to reopen Event, not the rest of January implementation.

An authorized operator must identify the correct provider account/environment and supply or inspect the verified historical records. An agent may perform the reconciliation once the evidence and read authority are available. Keep personal and payment records in their authorized private system, not this repository. No provider or production inventory has been performed for this item; record counts are unknown, not zero.

## Required record for each reference

1. Canonical provider environment, immutable checkout/payment reference and exact purchaser identity. Match the recorded App project and customer binding; surface duplicates, conflicts, missing references and unbound records without guessing.
2. Verified settlement instant and original positive term end. Distinguish natural expiry from refund/revocation: local epoch zero and shared revoked state never establish archive eligibility. Preserve the actual refund state and pending mirror/recovery work.
3. Evidence of the purchaser's primary ownership at checkout and settlement, and any explicit project designation. Today's owner, membership alone, an email address, plan selection or an active shared mirror is insufficient. Leave unavailable historical evidence classified **unknown**; do not manufacture a new designation from it.
4. Independent applicable grants: a current primary owner's valid account coverage or explicit valid project coverage; any separate unrefunded completed designated Event term. A member's personal grant cannot upgrade the whole project, and one refund cannot erase another independent qualifying term.
5. Bound Timeline/public artifacts and the authorized owner recovery paths. Record the project and publication identifiers without copying private content. Inventory already-created checkout sessions and other provider sales entry points separately; a candidate code switch does not prove they are disabled.

## Completion and exclusions

Retain a private receipt of the account/environment inspected, records classified, discrepancies and unresolved cases, operator/date and source references. Link only a non-sensitive receipt identifier here. Close this item only when the inventory and unresolved-record dispositions are recorded; completion alone does not authorize applying locks, changing production data or reopening sales.

Unknown or historical designation retains its existing access treatment pending explicit reconciliation. Do not retroactively lock Free projects, infer a paid archive, grant membership, conflate epoch-zero revocation with expiry, or change standalone Notes. Preserve owner export/deletion and narrow security-revocation authority when implementing the new policy. Production/provider mutation and any external communication remain separately held.
