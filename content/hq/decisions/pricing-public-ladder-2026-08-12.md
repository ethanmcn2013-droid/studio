## Decision

The umbrella pricing page presents exactly four public choices: Free, Student,
Pro and Enterprise. Free, Student and Pro are waitlist-first and use only the
ratified prices and limits in the commercial contract. Enterprise has no
published price or capability promise; it opens a founder-led conversation so
the working group, start and terms can be understood before an offer is made.

Event remains in the commercial contract and entitlement vocabulary, but it is
not part of the umbrella public ladder. This decision does not retire existing
Event semantics or authorise a replacement Enterprise entitlement.

## Reason

The public page should help a person choose by the work in front of them while
keeping every unresolved term out of the promise. The four choices reflect the
current public commercial direction without pretending paid access or
Enterprise infrastructure is ready.

## Alternatives considered

Keep Event as the fourth public plan. Present Enterprise as a priced tier.
Publish a four-card feature matrix. Open checkout before the shared commercial
and operational gates are complete.

## Risks

Enterprise can be mistaken for a shipped entitlement tier. Pro editing-guest
limits and Student verification mechanics remain unresolved. Event can drift
if future work assumes its absence from this page means commercial retirement.

## Notes

Free is €0 with one workspace and three editing guests. Student is €9.99 per
year with three workspaces, three editing guests and annual re-verification.
Pro is €12 per month or €120 per year with unlimited workspaces; its editing
guest limit is shown before purchase. All prices are inclusive of VAT at the
prevailing rate. Joining the waitlist never charges the visitor. Pricing stays
available to signed-in readers who are choosing or changing access.
