## Decision

Venue Edition is **€1,500 per venue, per year, prepaid**. The first **25 founding
agreements** are **€1,000 per venue, per year, prepaid**. Both numbers are
**inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate**. The couple never sees a price.

The venue-facing line is: **€500 a year less, for as long as you stay.**

This supersedes `venue-edition-fixed-price-2026-07-11`, which held one price for
every venue and argued against a founding discount. That decision stands as
dated history. It is no longer the offer.

### What changed, precisely

| | 2026-07-11 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Standard annual price | €1,500 | €1,500, VAT-inclusive |
| Founding places | 15 | 25 |
| Founding rate | €1,500, locked | €1,000, held |
| Founding number | not defined | 01/25 to 25/25, assigned on payment |
| Entitlement | no seat count | every booked couple, no number in the terms |

### The founding rate, and what holds it

The €1,000 rate covers the base annual Venue Edition agreement. It holds for as
long as that agreement renews continuously, without lapse. It is not a trial
price and there is no clause that expires it into a higher number.

**Never write "for life" or "forever" anywhere.** Not in copy, not in a
contract, not in a film line, not in a summary. The commercial substance is a
lock with no expiry clause. The wording says that without promising the service
outlives everything.

The rate follows the property, not the company. It survives a rebrand and a
change of ownership while the agreement runs continuously. It does not travel to
additional properties a new owner brings with them.

### The Founding 25 programme

Twenty-five places. Each carries a number, 01/25 through 25/25, assigned **on
payment**, not on signature. Payment is the only event that cannot be walked
back, which is what makes a number mean something.

A place is held for **14 days from the proposal**, once. Ethan can extend a hold
personally. Outreach runs in cohorts of 25, released one after another until 25
venues have signed and paid.

### What a founding venue gets beyond the price

One 30-minute call per venue per year, plus a named email route. That is the
boundary, and it is a real benefit rather than a standing arrangement.

Founding requests are logged and shape what gets built. **Nothing is built for
one venue.** This is written into the Benefits Charter as a stated boundary, not
held back as a refusal for later.

### Entitlement

A venue with a current paid licence can open a workspace for any couple with a
signed booking. **No number appears in the commercial terms.** No seat count, no
per-couple arithmetic, nothing for a coordinator to track.

Fair use notifies Signal Studio and keeps issuing. It never blocks. A numeric
pause must not appear in a document that also says unlimited.

Any workspace a couple has already redeemed keeps its full term and its Keepsake,
whatever happens to the venue's licence. Only new issuance stops. This is said
first, unprompted, in the outreach email and the agreement.

A venue's annual wedding count is collected **after signature**, as an onboarding
field. It sets an internal issuance ceiling. It never sets the price and it never
changes at renewal.

## Reason

A venue owner doing arithmetic in euro understands €500 a year better than a
percentage. The 2026-07-11 position asked a founding venue to take a position
before there was a case study or a referral, and offered certainty as the reward.
Certainty is worth something. It is worth less than €500.

Twenty-five is the number of venues the programme needs, so it is the number of
places on offer. Fifteen was a smaller cohort attached to a different promise.

Assigning the number on payment rather than signature is what stops 01/25 being a
courtesy. Bounding founder access at one call a year is what stops twenty-five
relationships becoming a full-time job.

## Commercial effect

Founding revenue drops by €500 per venue per year against the previous model.
Twenty-five founding venues at €1,000 is €25,000 of prepaid annual revenue, where
the same 25 at €1,500 would have been €37,500. Every forecast, lender pack, pitch
deck, structured-data offer and financial model carrying founding revenue changes.

VAT-inclusive pricing is a second, separate reduction. If Signal Studio is or
becomes an accountable person, €1,000 inclusive nets about €813 at the 23%
standard rate. Across 25 founding venues that is roughly €4,675 a year absorbed,
and the price lock means it cannot be corrected later. A future rate rise is
absorbed too.

Both effects were put to the founder in full and accepted as commercial
judgement. They are tracked as R-021 and R-022 in the VEF-2026 register.

## Risk

The written Revenue position is not yet in hand. A MyEnquiries submission is
drafted and costs nothing to file, and it should be filed before any agreement is
signed. Until then, "inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate" is the wording that
keeps the disclosure honest.

Nothing in this decision has been reviewed by a solicitor or an accountant, and
no surface may state or imply otherwise.
