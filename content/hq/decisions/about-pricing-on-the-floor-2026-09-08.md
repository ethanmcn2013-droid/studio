## Decision

The founder accepted direction A, the floor and the sheet, as the home page
on 3 September (S·169) and on 8 September asked for About and Pricing to
match it. Both pages now use the same geometry: an ink floor, white sheets
with a 14px radius lifted off it, the mono kicker, the display and title
type from the floor stylesheet, and the inverted footer on the floor.

About is deliberately simple. One sheet carries the claim, the turn and the
company facts. The three products sit on the floor as three small sheets.
The founder's note is written on a ruled page: 32px rules, an indigo margin
line, the highlighter on the one sentence that matters, a signature, and a
turned corner at the top right. The refusals sit on the floor. Contact stays
the last sheet with the same subject-prefilled mailto. The translation table
from the six-movement brief retired with this cut.

Pricing keeps its plan model, ledger, keyboard behaviour, comparison and
answers unchanged, on sheets. The Notes to Tasks to Timeline proof moves onto
the floor as white panes, the same object grammar as the hero relay.

The home page carries the launch notice, January 2027, above the first
sheet, and the hero's status line agrees with it.

## Reason

The front door set the register; two of the three pages in the site
navigation still read as the paper estate that came before it. A visitor
moving from the home page to Pricing or About should stay in one building.
The founder's note earns its page because it is the one place on the site
where a person speaks in the first person; a ruled sheet says "written",
not "generated".

## Supersession

The six-movement About that cleared its 9.5 verdict gate on 2026-08-23
(S·168) is superseded as the shipped page. Its gate record remains history
under design-reviews. The About and Pricing browser specs were rewritten to
the new structure; the pricing contract test is untouched and still passes.
