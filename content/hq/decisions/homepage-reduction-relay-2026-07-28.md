## Decision

The Signal Studio homepage now demonstrates the suite through one fixed
wedding-planning handoff: a private Notes line becomes an approved Tasks
commitment, a confirmed commitment becomes a public Timeline milestone, and
Signal returns the open risk with its source.

The old four-row product summary is retired from the homepage. Each product is
now a chapter around its current interface, not a second marketing hero. The
product pages and consolidated app routes stay unchanged.

## Hero

Keep the canonical headline, with `80%` carrying the indigo emphasis. The
wordmark settles once in 1.45 seconds and then rests. The proposition is
immediately readable, the typewriter and scroll prompt are gone, and the first
view offers two quiet actions: see the system at work or join the waitlist.

## Proof fixture

The deterministic sample workspace is Mara and Finn at The Orchard on 30 July.
The handoff begins with “Venue can open the side room after six”, becomes “Ask
the venue to hold the side room after six”, and publishes “Side room held after
six” when confirmed. The sample does not write to a live workspace.

## Motion and access

The umbrella wordmark is the only automatic homepage motion. It plays once and
rests. The embedded Tasks board is static, the Signal ping is disabled, and
reduced motion renders the settled state. The Products disclosure follows DOM
focus order, returns focus on Escape, and keeps its calls to action visible on
touch and keyboard focus.

## Market path

The wedding path follows the suite proof rather than interrupting the hero.
Public conversion remains waitlist-first; the venue and events path stays
available in the close.
