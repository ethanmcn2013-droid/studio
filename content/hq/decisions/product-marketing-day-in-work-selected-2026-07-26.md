## Decision

The canonical product marketing direction for Notes, Tasks, Timeline, and
Signal is **A day in the work**. Each page tells three small moments from a
recognisable day and pairs them with a truthful, interactive product scene.

The page structure is:

1. product name, position, promise, and proof-first hero gesture;
2. three selectable moments from the day;
3. one real product interaction beside the active moment;
4. the product boundary and one route into the consolidated app.

## Product proof

- Notes lets a visitor write a private example line and deliberately send that
  line to Tasks.
- Tasks lets a visitor resolve and reopen plain-English commitments.
- Timeline renders the signed Mara and Finn artifact, including its real
  progress lens, countdown, line, milestones, Today marker, and details.
- Signal lets a visitor open briefing readings and see their source.

All examples are deterministic marketing fixtures. No scene reaches a live
workspace or suggests that a local interaction persisted to a real account.

## Motion

The hero gestures remain Notes caret, Tasks resolve pulse, Timeline line
travel, and Signal reading tick. They prove the product mechanism, use one
indigo, respect reduced motion, and stop short of logo theatre.

## Timeline parity

`timeline.signalstudio.ie` refuses framing through `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and
`frame-ancestors 'none'`. Marketing must not weaken those headers. The Studio
implementation therefore mirrors the signed artifact component and public
DTO shape under an executable contract test.

## Mobile footer

The shared footer keeps its five desktop columns. On mobile the brand block
spans the width and Product, Company, Resources, and Suite form a two-by-two
grid. Four long vertical link stacks are not the mobile contract.
