## Decision

Three founder decisions, all made on 2 October 2026. They are recorded here
from the founder's instructions in that day's working session, passed to the
build by the delivery coordinator. The pull request that carries this file is
where the founder confirms them: nothing is published until he merges it.

### a. The direction

The founder picked **"A, One Friday, with parts of B and C"** for the public
home page.

- A is the base: one Friday at The Orchard, a wedding venue in Kinsale, and
  one task, Chase florist deposit, followed from Home to the board, the list,
  the calendar and the Overview.
- From C: the working Home in the hero. A visitor ticks a task, the counts
  move, Undo puts it back, and a nudge goes to the florist. Nothing is saved
  or sent.
- From B: "Words you will not find here", fourteen software words struck
  through.
- The header reads Projects, Tasks, Timeline, Files, Analytics, Whiteboard,
  and each has its own section. Notes and the daily briefing are not named.

The page is **dark first, with a light switch, on the home page only**. It
opens dark, follows the device setting when that is light, and the visitor
can change it. The choice is not stored. Every other public page stays light.

### b. Publishing now

The founder's explicit instruction is to publish this home page now, ahead of
the 21 January 2027 date. The instruction is limited to the home page.

It does not open access. It does not send any email. It does not start
outreach. It does not approve a charge. The waitlist stays the only
conversion on the page, and the page says access opens in stages.

The 21 January 2027 date and its release gates stand for everything else.

### c. The review

A blind panel reviewed the page before the port, round 1. It raised 27
findings. 24 were confirmed and fixed. 3 were refuted. The evidence is in the
remote-redesign lab, `work/2026-10-01-landing-v3-2026-10/panel/round-1/`. The
lock, with the founder's objections in his own words, is `lock.md` in the same
folder.

## What this supersedes

- `homepage-reduction-relay-2026-07-28.md`: superseded. The home page no
  longer proves the suite through the Notes to Tasks to Timeline handoff.
- `about-pricing-on-the-floor-2026-09-08.md`: superseded for the home page
  only. "Floor and sheet" is no longer the home page. About and Pricing keep
  the floor and the sheet unchanged, and that decision still governs them.
- `dark-mode-ships-d013-2026-08-12.md`: amended for `/` only. That decision
  kept marketing and every public surface light. The home page is now the one
  public page with a dark surface. The signed-in app's theme setting is
  untouched and the two do not share a preference.

The floor-and-sheet home components are kept, unbuilt, in
`archive/home-floor-and-sheet/`.

## How it was built

Everything below is a delegated decision, made in the port.

- The global site nav hides itself on `/` for signed-out visitors and the page
  renders its own header. Pricing and About sit in that header as quiet links
  on wide screens and in the phone menu, and both stay in the footer.
- The locked site footer is unchanged, company registration line included. The
  dot run sits directly above it.
- The waitlist form at the close uses the same action as `/waitlist`, with
  source `home_close`.
- Two lines were brought back to what the site already says. The hero stamp
  reads "Wedding venues in private preview", not "Venues". The venue card
  reads "In private preview with wedding venues." and does not name the Venue
  Edition, which left the public estate with `/venues` on 11 September.
- Product captures ship as WebP at the sizes they are shown, from the lab
  originals. Two new phone captures, the board and the list, replace cropped
  desktop captures in steps 02 and 03.

## Round 2 (2 October 2026)

Six blind reviews of the live page came back the same day: visual, UX,
accessibility, responsive, QA and interaction. The founder's instruction was
to address every finding and bring the page to the standard of the best
studios working today. The fix plan and the six reports are in the
remote-redesign lab, `work/2026-10-01-landing-v3-2026-10/panel/round-2/`. The
home page's share shipped as S·184; the site-wide share (share image, footer,
manifest, redirects, 404, caching) is S·183.

### Founder decisions

Made by the founder on 2 October 2026 and passed to the build by the delivery
coordinator. The pull request is where he confirms them.

- Prices may be stated on the home page. The note under the form reads
  "There is a free plan for one project, and Pro is €12 a month." The Pro
  price is read from the commercial terms, as Pricing reads it.
- Venues can ask to try it now. The venue card has a second way in, "Run a
  venue? Ask to try it now", an email to hello@signalstudio.ie, beside
  "Everyone else, join the waitlist." The published claim stands: in private
  preview with wedding venues. No venue is named or counted.

### Delegated decisions

Made in the fix plan and the build, under the programme's delegation. None is
a founder pick.

1. The header keeps its six section links in the founder's order, then
   Pricing and About, then the one filled button. The page is re-ordered to
   match the header.
2. Page order: the working sample; who it is for; Projects; the Friday story;
   Timeline; Files; the same thing in your words; Analytics; Whiteboard;
   words you will not find; venues; the form; the dot run; the footer.
3. The Friday story is three steps: Home, Calendar, and one "same task" step
   on the board. The "One task" section is one sentence inside the last step.
4. Page copy says "a Friday at The Orchard" and "8 days" without day numbers
   where it can. The captures keep their dates.
5. The hero's hint is a label on the sample's top edge, "This one is live.
   Tick a task." The second button reads "Try the sample" and goes to it.
6. Analytics leads with the question, "Ask if you will make the date." The
   whiteboard's headline is "Throw it all on the wall. Tidy it later."
7. The struck list holds only words that appear nowhere on the page or in a
   capture, and the page adds "And a board and a calendar. They are just
   called that." A browser test keeps it honest.
8. Projects tabs read Covers, List, One project.
9. One filled button in the hero, one quiet prompt after Files, and the form.
   The note under the form uses facts the site already publishes, plus the
   founder's two decisions above. An optional "What do you run?" fills the
   form's `audience` field and the nearest of the waitlist's own use cases.
10. The venue card is two lines and two ways in.
11. The theme the visitor chooses is kept on their device
    (`signal-home-theme`) and painted first. This replaces "The choice is not
    stored" in a. above. Other pages stay light.
12. One motion system: 150, 250 and 450 ms and the design system's two
    easings. The headline is painted in the first frame.
13. The header is 92% opaque, with a blur where the browser has one.
14. Every product frame sits on the text grid, with one window radius (16),
    one control radius (12) and one small radius (8). A frame shows the
    product at one source pixel to one CSS pixel, or is sampled down from the
    2x file.
15. The spotlight veil is 0.28.
16. The footer's mascot film is off on this page. The dot run above it plays
    once and is the page's one closing motion. The film's loop costs a third
    of the main thread while it is on screen and competed with the dot run;
    it can come back when the shared component is fixed.
17. Out of this round, recorded so it is not lost: Pricing, About and
    Waitlist in the home page's shell and one-product story; a site-wide dark
    theme; serving `/` from cache, which needs the layout to stop reading the
    signed-in header for every route.

### Open, held for the founder

Neutral wording is on the page until these are answered.

- "People on the list get in first, in the order they joined."
- What "Ask your files" does under the hood, and making About, Principles and
  Privacy agree.
- How a nudge reaches a supplier, and what "make the day fit" suggests.
- "Works on your phone and your laptop. Nothing to install."
- One person or a small team; the founder's name and place near the form.
- Which social handles are real.
- The free plan's limits in words. The founder's wording was one workspace,
  one project, two editing guests. Pricing publishes one workspace and three
  editing guests, and no project limit. The home page says only "a free plan
  for one project" until the two agree.

## Risks

- The page shows the product's sample content at Friday 25 September. Pricing
  still shows the earlier sample day, 16 July. Both are sample content and
  both say so, but they are not the same day.
- The page title and description in the layout still say Notes, Tasks and
  Timeline. The page itself names Projects, Tasks, Timeline, Files, Analytics
  and Whiteboard. The words were left alone on purpose; they need a founder
  call before they change.
- The browser's own bar stays white on phones, because the theme colour is
  set once for the whole site.
