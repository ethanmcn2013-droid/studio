## Decision

Three priorities govern every front-facing decision across the suite —
the four product modules and the marketing surface alike — in this
order:

1. **Experience.** Using the product should feel considered end to end
   and, in the right moments, delightful. Delight is deliberate:
   candidate moments enter the app repo's `docs/DELIGHT_CATALOG.md` and
   receive an animate-or-restrained verdict — never sprinkled ad hoc.
2. **Design.** Everything front-facing ships at the standard of the
   best studios working today — spacing, type, motion, empty, loading,
   and error states, microcopy, all deliberate, nothing default.
3. **Utility.** Someone who has never used a project-management tool
   must be able to pick the product up and understand it unaided — the
   first-contact test. No jargon, no technical lock-out; a surface that
   needs explaining is not done.

When the three pull against each other, that order decides.

No new machinery ships with this decision; the priorities bind to what
already exists. Experience runs through the delight catalog and its
review gate. Design is held by the design register (A1 + A1.1 —
creativity and emotion outrank restraint stands beneath this ordering,
untouched) and proven by the 9.5 quality council gate under the
experience quality operating system. Utility is named by the
first-contact test and reviewed through the same 13-dimension rubric.

The ambient copies live where sessions read them — the app repo's
`AGENTS.md` §North star and this repo's `AGENTS.md` §North star, worded
to match so they cannot drift. This record is canonical.

## Why

The enforcement machinery predates the direction: the register said how
to design, the 9.5 gate said how to prove finish, the delight catalog
gated micro-interactions — but nothing above them said what the product
is optimizing for, or in which order, when they compete for the same
cycle. The operator set that ordering on 2026-08-01 as part of a
standing practice of re-deriving direction roughly every six months.

## Revisit when

The `reviewDate` falls due (~every six months; next 2027-02), or the

Refinements edit this record in place — the derivation history lives in
