## Decision

The founder directed on 30 October 2026 that customer-facing copy stops
naming Signal Notes, Signal Tasks and Signal Timeline. They are legacy
names and do not describe the current app.

Signal Studio is described as one app, by what it does: a board for what
needs doing and who has it, a timeline for every date in order, a plain
read on whether the work is on pace, files kept with the job, and a chat
that changes the plan only when the person approves it. Home stays the
front door. Lower-case words such as "tasks" or "timeline" are fine when
they describe the work rather than name a product.

## Applied

Home page pricing, About, Terms, Privacy, Press, the gift-code page and
the access, dispatch, student, venue and school emails were rewritten on
30 October 2026.

## Still to do

The pricing page (held behind the launch boundary) still shows the
earlier Free, Student, Pro and Enterprise model and a Notes to Tasks to
Timeline illustration; it needs rebuilding to the Pro and Organisations
model rather than a wording pass. Brand-kit files and wordmarks under
`public/brand/` keep the old product marks as historical assets.
