# Instrument opportunities after launch

Status: accepted delegated January no-build decision, recorded 2026-09-04.
Guests & Seating is excluded from January by the
[launch decision](../../../content/hq/decisions/january-2027-launch.md).
This decision does not await another routine founder checkpoint. The accepted
16 October instruments allocation can review evidence without reopening scope.
These are hypotheses from the retained product and venue strategy, not new
customer research or evidence of demand.

| Opportunity | Useful outcome | Reuse / cost | Evidence that would justify a review |
|---|---|---|---|
| Improve the existing Notes → Tasks → Timeline handoff | A coordinator captures a decision, assigns the work and shares the date | Existing products; lowest extra operating surface | Real first sessions fail at the handoff, or repeated support shows avoidable re-entry. |
| Improve venue adoption reporting | The buyer can see whether sponsored access leads to useful work | Existing event and aggregate contracts; requires trustworthy attribution | Paid venues ask for renewal evidence and qualified usage data is sufficiently complete. |
| Improve briefing relevance | People know what needs attention today | Existing Home; depends on useful underlying work | Returning users have real work but cannot identify the next action without help. |
| Guests & Seating | A couple or coordinator reconciles guest details and a seating plan | New data, privacy, import/export, permissions and layout surface; highest January scope cost | Independent postlaunch demand, a repeated concrete workflow, and willingness to pay or renew because of it. |

The January priority is completing and measuring the existing journey. Do not
add guest records, seating UI, routes, schemas or marketing promises under this
comparison. “Guests” in the commercial access-role policy is not a guest-list
product commitment.

## Proposed postlaunch review trigger

After first outreach on or after 21 January, collect specific accounts from at
least three independent venues or coordinators describing the same unmet job.
Retain the current workaround, frequency, time/cost, decision-maker, and whether
existing Notes, Tasks or Timeline can solve it. At least one account should be
from a paying venue with observed useful work, rather than a demo reaction.
Treat these thresholds as a proposed research filter, not a ratified sales or
product target.

Compare the opportunity against fixing activation, retention and support in
the existing products. If payment exists but useful work does not, investigate
that journey before widening scope. Record evidence, privacy implications,
support burden, a bounded experiment and a stop condition. The founder then
decides whether to commission discovery. Only a later explicit scope decision
can authorise a build. No calendar, request count or revenue number opens it
automatically.
