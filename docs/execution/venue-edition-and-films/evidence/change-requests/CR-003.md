# CR-003 — Launch scope after the WP-01 packet decisions

**Raised:** 2026-08-03 by Claude Code (WP-01)
**Decided:** 2026-08-03 by Ethan McNamara — **approved**, recorded as D-027
**Status:** approved, actioned in the same session it was raised

Change control is required because points 2, 3 and 4 change product scope
(WORKFLOWS §5). Point 1 is a risk with a target, not a scope change, and is
recorded as R-023 rather than here.

---

## Requested change

Three changes to product scope, arising from findings in the WP-01 audit.

### 1. ADD — a real founding-number field

`sponsors` gains a founding-number column with a unique constraint, assigned on
cleared payment.

### 2. NARROW — venue branding at launch is the venue's name only

No logo and no venue-written welcome message inside the couple's workspace for
the 1 September release.

### 3. NARROW — the Venue Portal at launch is invitation administration only

Aggregate adoption evidence moves to after 1 September.

---

## Reason

**The founding number.** D-009 point 6 and E02.10 both ratify numbers 01/25 to
25/25 assigned on cleared payment. E02.10 is Done and correctly so — it was a
policy task and its evidence is the programme mechanics document and
`commercial-terms.v2.json`. Neither it nor anything else creates a place to
*store* the number. The only occurrence of `01/25` in the codebase is marketing
copy on `/venues`. Two founding venues both being told they hold 07/25 is
unrecoverable with exactly the people whose reference value is the reason they
were recruited, and a unique constraint makes it impossible rather than
unlikely.

**Branding.** The audit found three venue-identity representations, of which the
one the couple's product actually reads is an untyped JSON blob in
`comp_codes.notes`. `brandMeta`, the column that would hold a logo, has no
schema, no type and no consumer anywhere in either repository. What inherits
into a sponsored workspace today is the venue's name, rendered as "Compliments
of <venue>", and nothing else. Building logo upload, storage, rendering and a
moderation position for venue-written copy is real work competing directly with
the films in the 29 days remaining.

**Portal scope.** `projectSponsorActivation` has no caller outside its own test.
`sponsorConsentGrants` has no writer anywhere. `listSponsorActivationDTOs` has no
caller. The consent layer is designed, schema'd, unit-tested and idle, and the
portal cannot show a venue aggregate evidence until it is wired. That wiring is
E07 and it was not going to fit.

## Affected dependencies

E07's launch-blocking set shrinks. Nothing else moves earlier as a result — the
time is absorbed by the films and by E05, both of which are behind the freeze
dates in D-008.

## Affected copy and contracts

**This is the part that must not be missed.** The name-only position is a
standing constraint on every venue-facing surface, not a one-off edit. A
proposal page, agreement, venue pack, outreach email or film line implying a
venue's logo appears in the couple's workspace is now a claim the product will
not meet, delivered to 25 businesses at once. Recorded as R-024 with a
copy-freeze target of 2026-08-21.

## Affected film or product assets

E13.09 and E14.12 must not show a venue logo inside a couple's workspace in any
frame. Worth confirming with the motion lane before render, not after.

## Schedule impact

Net negative, which is the point. Two narrowings remove more work than the one
addition adds, inside the 29 days before release.

## Commercial impact

None on price, terms, entitlement or the founding rate. The offer is unchanged.
What changes is what a venue receives at launch, and D-027 point 3 makes that
explicit rather than leaving it to be discovered.

## Risk impact

Opens R-023 (severity high, target before Cohort 1) and R-024 (severity medium,
target copy-freeze). Reduces delivery risk against R-001 by removing the two
largest pieces of unbuilt launch work.

**One exposure created deliberately and worth stating plainly.** Point 3 narrows
D-001 point 5, which describes the Venue Portal as "an aggregate
adoption-evidence layer". At launch it is not one. The charter is not rewritten;
this records that launch scope is smaller than the charter, on purpose, and that
the charter remains the destination.

## Recommendation

Approve. Both narrowings are honest reductions of work that was not going to
land, and the one addition closes a promise with no data behind it.
