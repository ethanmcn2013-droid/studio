**Raised:** 2026-08-03 · **Raised by:** Claude Code (WP-03, task E01.10)
**Status: APPROVED 2026-08-03** by Ethan McNamara, in one word: *"approved"*.
Recorded as **D-025** and actioned in full the same day.

This changes the supporting-epic set of two launch gates, which `PROJECT.md` §20
puts under change control. It is raised rather than applied.

---

## Requested change

1. Add **E15** to the **sales readiness** gate's supporting epics, with one new
   exit criterion: one venue configuration rehearsed end to end against a
   rehearsal account before Cohort 1 is contacted, with a written list of what
   broke.
2. Add **E01** to the **commercial** gate's supporting epics, with one new exit
   criterion: every freeze date in D-008 either observed on its date or moved by
   a numbered change request. No freeze passes silently.
3. On approval, empty the `KNOWN_UNGATED` list in `project-control.test.mjs` so
   the coverage test goes back to asserting full coverage.

## Reason

Mapping the six gates against the fifteen epics: commercial covers E02, legal
E03, product E04 to E07, data E08 and E09, creative E13 and E14, sales readiness
E10 to E12.

**E01 and E15 are supporting epics of no gate.**

The consequence is not theoretical. The six gates certify that the product works
and that the sale can be made. **Nothing certifies that what was sold can be
delivered**, because onboarding lives in E15, and `PROJECT.md` §4 and §22 both
close the project on venues being "configured, onboarded and capable of issuing
functioning couple invitations". The first venue's onboarding would be
improvised, by one founder, with no support team. That is R-024.

Separately, E01 holds the machinery R-006's mitigation depends on — the weekly
operating review, the founder-review queue, and the six freeze dates. Until this
session no gate criterion checked that any freeze had actually happened. A freeze
that nobody checks is a date in a document.

## What was already covered without a gate change

Three of the uncovered exposures were closed inside existing gates while writing
the criteria, because they belonged to an epic a gate already owned:

| Exposure | Where it landed |
|---|---|
| The launch-day deploy steps in `open-signal-studio-2026-09-01.md` (DEP-005, R-023) | Data gate, criterion 12 — E08.12 is already a data-gate epic |
| The freeze dates being observed at all (E01.11) | Commercial gate, criterion 12 — as part of the offer freeze |
| A-002, whether the film price sequence is genuinely parameterised | Creative gate, criterion 12 |

Those needed no change request. The two remaining holes do, because they need an
epic added to a gate rather than a criterion added to an epic a gate already has.

## Affected tasks

| Task | Effect |
|---|---|
| E15.09, E15.10, E15.11 | Come inside the sales-readiness gate. No change to their titles or scope |
| E01.11 | Its dates become checkable at the commercial gate rather than only recorded |
| E15.01 | Unchanged. Its own preconditions are a separate question, see below |

## Affected dependencies

None. This adds no task edges. Gate membership is tracked as gate state, not as
dependencies, exactly as `BASELINE_REVIEW.md` §4 item 6 established.

## Schedule impact

Neutral. The rehearsal in item 1 is work E15.10 and E15.11 already require; the
change makes it a gate condition rather than new work. Item 2 is a check, not a
task.

## Commercial impact

None directly. Indirectly it protects the completion condition: 25 paid venues
that cannot be onboarded is not project completion under §22, and today nothing
would catch that before it happened.

## Risk impact

Closes the structural half of I-008. Gives R-024 a home. Does **not** address
R-025 — that all six gates could pass with release-blocking tasks still in
Backlog — because the fix for R-025 is a precondition on E15.01, the go/no-go,
and E15.01's preconditions are the founder's to set, not a gate's.

## Recommendation

**Approve.** The alternative is a gate system with a documented hole, which is
worse than one with an undocumented hole: the hole is now written down, so
passing all six gates in late August would read as certainty it has not earned.

If you would rather not expand a gate this close to release, the honest fallback
is to reject this and instead record the two conditions as explicit E15.01
preconditions. That keeps the gates untouched and still means someone has to
answer the question before go/no-go. It is a worse mechanism, because E15.01 is
one meeting and a gate is a standing check, but it is not dishonest.

What is not acceptable is leaving it as it stands.
