# Change request CR-001 — Rewrite the legal release gate, defer E03.12

**Raised:** 2026-08-03 · **Raised by:** Claude Code · **Status: APPROVED 2026-08-03** (D-023)
**Approved under the founder standing instruction, not by a named approval — flagged in D-023 and reversible on request.** One amendment on adoption: criterion 6 required VAT-exclusive pricing; D-021 supersedes it with VAT-inclusive. This touches a launch
gate, which is on the change-control list in `PROJECT.md` §20.

---

## Requested change

1. Replace the legal release gate's exit criteria. They currently require
   *"Documented Irish legal and accounting review obtained"*, which D-016 made
   impossible.
2. Move task **E03.12** from Backlog to **Deferred**, clear its critical-path
   flag, and attach an un-defer trigger.
3. Add one conditional document to the E03 set: an Article 26 joint-controller
   arrangement, if the role map produces one.

## Reason

D-016 settled that there is no solicitor and no accountant. The gate as written
can then never pass, which means E15.01 can never pass, which means Cohort 1 can
never be contacted. The gate has to become achievable without becoming
dishonest.

## Why defer E03.12 rather than amend or cancel it

**Amend is unavailable by rule.** Imported titles are never rewritten. Amending
its scope to mean "AI review" would leave a task in the record whose title says
a solicitor reviewed the contracts, satisfied by evidence that no solicitor did.
Anyone reading the file in a year — including you — would be misled by your own
project record.

**Cancel asserts more than you decided.** You decided there is no budget today.
D-016 and R-013 both say to reopen at first revenue. Cancelling deletes the
anchor that trigger points at, and a fixed-fee review of six documents that
already exist is a much cheaper instruction than commissioning drafting from
nothing. The drafting being done now is what makes a later review affordable,
not what replaces it.

**Defer keeps the record honest.** The task still says a solicitor should look
at this. It is simply, and visibly, not happening yet.

## Proposed new legal gate exit criteria

1. All six documents drafted, each recording on its face the named, dated,
   publicly available Irish or EU source position it was built from — or
   recording explicitly that it is bespoke.
2. A written GDPR role map exists and is founder-ratified (E03.01), covering
   every data category including **Article 9 categories and children's data**,
   with the controller/processor/joint-controller position and lawful basis for
   each. Every later document is consistent with it.
3. Each document passed adversarial review **by the lane that did not draft it**
   — Claude drafts, Codex reviews, or the reverse — across four fixed lenses:
   GDPR completeness, consumer contract fairness, commercial enforceability,
   hostile reading. Each lens completes a named per-item checklist with an
   explicit verdict per item.
4. Every finding disposed of in writing: fixed, or accepted by the founder with
   his stated reason. Nothing flagged and silently shipped.
5. No statutory reference, case citation or legal proposition appears in any
   published document unless verified against primary source text, with source
   and retrieval date in a citation register.
6. A written VAT and revenue-recognition position exists, sourced to current
   Revenue guidance with retrieval dates, answering **in this order**: (a) is
   Signal Studio already an accountable person by receipt of foreign B2B
   services under the reverse charge, (b) the tax point on annual prepayment,
   (c) threshold arithmetic across all revenue lines together, (d) OSS. **A
   Revenue MyEnquiries submission is filed and its written reply attached as
   evidence** — this is free.
7. The contracting entity is correct and stated on every agreement (registered
   name and CRO number), or the founder has decided in writing to contract
   personally with the personal-liability exposure recorded as accepted.
8. GDPR operational paperwork current: Article 30 record, subprocessor register
   with each vendor DPA on file, documented transfer basis per non-EEA
   processor, and a written Article 35 DPIA threshold assessment reaching a
   reasoned conclusion either way.
9. An automated string check plus founder review confirms no published
   document, page, portal string, email, film line or proposal states or implies
   legal approval, solicitor review, accountant verification, or unqualified
   GDPR compliance. Pre-approved answers exist for "are you GDPR compliant",
   "has a solicitor reviewed this", "are you insured", "where is our data held".
10. A signed founder statement names what has **not** been done: no solicitor,
    no accountant, no professional indemnity, no privilege over review records.
    R-013 and R-014 open and accepted.
11. The gate pass is recorded as **"founder-accepted without professional
    review"** with the date and the founder's own words. Never as "legal review
    obtained" or "legally approved" in any generated report.
12. **Conditional:** if the role map produces joint controllership on venue
    adoption analytics, an Article 26 arrangement and its public essence summary
    exist — or the founder has recorded a product decision that removes the
    trigger by restricting venue-facing analytics to aggregates.

## Schedule impact

Neutral to positive. The gate becomes achievable, so the 1 September go/no-go
becomes possible rather than definitionally blocked. E03.01 becoming a hard gate

two to three days, against a gate that could not have passed at all.

## Commercial impact

The VAT-exclusive drafting rule protects roughly **19% of every founding
contract** — about €187 per venue per year, €4,675 across the founding 25 —
against the case where registration turns out to be required. The
top-end eligibility screen in the entitlement recommendation protects the price
lock from its one real loss case.

## Risk impact

rule), R-016 ("unlimited" unrepresentable in code), R-017 (Article 9 guest data),
R-018 (possible existing VAT registration obligation). R-013 and R-014 stay open
and accepted.

## Recommendation

**Approve.** The alternative is a gate that cannot pass, which would surface as
a go/no-go failure in late August with no time to fix it. Approving this makes
the legal position honest and achievable in the same move, and the VAT-exclusive
drafting rule alone justifies it.
