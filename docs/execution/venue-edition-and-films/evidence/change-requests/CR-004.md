Change control is required because `E09.10-copy-hierarchy.md` (`venue-copy.v1`)

---

## Requested change

**From.** E09.10 line 4 declares that it blocks *"offer, founding-rate, privacy,
Keepsake, CTA, objection and FAQ copy"*. That is **seven** blocks. The E09.11
title names **eight**. A search for `collaborat` across the whole of E09.10
returns zero hits: no terminology row in §1, no rung in §3, no prohibition in §5,
no tone rule in §4.

**To.** Collaboration becomes the eighth governed block. Its contract is
`E09.11-copy-system.md` §6, which is incorporated into `venue-copy.v1` by this
amendment and carries the same authority as a section written inside E09.10.

**What §6 supplies, and what it therefore adds to E09.10:**

| E09.10 section | What CR-004 adds to it |
|---|---|
| §1 Terminology | E09.11 §6.2, five rows: the people a couple brings in, the act, what they get, the people who see a published Timeline, and the venue's relationship to any of it |
| §3 Copy hierarchy | E09.11 §6.3, four audience variants: couple-facing in product, couple-facing privacy line, venue-facing, coordinator-facing |
| §5 Standing prohibitions | E09.11 §6.4, four refusals, each grounded in verified product behaviour rather than in preference |
| §6 The pre-approved answers | E09.11 §6.3's objection form, *"So we can see the plan if the couple lets us?"* |

**The determination the block rests on**, which is the part that is new policy
rather than new wording:

> A couple sharing their plan is a couple's act, and the venue is not a party to
> it.

There is no venue-side sharing control, no venue view of who a couple shared
with, and no route by which a venue becomes a participant in a couple's
workspace by any action the venue can take. The copy states that positively
rather than leaving it to be inferred.

**What is not changed.** No line of E09.10 is edited, moved or deleted. Its
version string `venue-copy.v1` is unchanged. Its §2 master never-list, its four
pre-approved answers in §6 and its fifteen prohibitions in §5 all stand exactly
as approved.

---

## Reason

**Why now.** E09.11's §2 audit found this while checking each of the eight named
blocks against what already existed. Seven had a contract. Collaboration had
none.

**Why it matters more than a missing heading.** Of the eight blocks, the one with
no words is the one sitting closest to a privacy boundary. Collaboration copy is
where a warm sentence most easily promises something the product does not do:
that a shared link is secure, that a couple can publish part of a plan rather
than all of it, that a published page is unlisted, or that a dietary list reaches
the venue through the workspace. Each of those four is false against verified
code, and each is recorded with its verification in E09.11 §6.4.

**Why it is a change request and not an edit.** E09.10 is Done and
founder-approved. The programme's rule is that approval means something, and a
session that edits an approved document to add a section it thinks is missing has
removed the founder from a decision he already made. Recording it here costs one
file and keeps the approval record honest.

## Risk impact

**Reduces R-020.** R-020 is the risk that untrained venues describe this product
to couples in words that become part of the venue's own promise. Sharing is one
of the four subjects the copy pack covers, and until now the pack's sharing
answers rested on no governed source.

**Closes a silent gap rather than opening one.** The exposure being closed is
that a collaboration string could have shipped through the copy freeze with no
contract to check it against, because the freeze checks strings against the
blocks E09.10 names and collaboration was not one of them.

No `RAID.md` entry changes severity. No new risk is created.
