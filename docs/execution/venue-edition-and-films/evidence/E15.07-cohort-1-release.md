## 3. The log shape

Two logs, both flat, both durable.

**Touch log** — one row per contact: venue account id, date, channel, asset
sent, who sent it, and the exact link set. One row per contact, never one row
per venue, so a second touch is visible.

**Response log** — one row per response: venue, date, **the venue's own words**,
the classification, the next action, the next action's date. A summary loses the
objection, and the objection is the thing E11.12 was built to answer.

**Hold register** — any founding place held: venue, proposal date, expiry
fourteen days later, extended by Ethan personally or not. D-009 point 7 gives one
hold, extendable by the founder alone.
