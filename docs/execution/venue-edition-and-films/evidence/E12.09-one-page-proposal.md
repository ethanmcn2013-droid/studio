## 0. What this is, decided rather than assumed

E12.09's task title says "concise one-page commercial proposal" and does not say
what kind of object that is. It is decided here and stated plainly:

**A printable page behind the HQ password gate at `/hq/venue-proposal`.** Not a
public route, not a static document.

| Why not | Reason |
|---|---|
| A public route | It carries one named venue and a price. |
| A static document | The paid count is verified against the register at the moment of sending and never carried over from the previous proposal (`FOUNDING_25_PROGRAMME_MECHANICS.md` §1). A static file cannot do that. |
| A PDF generator | `E11.11` §2 sends it as the body of the follow-up email and as an attached or linked page. Print to PDF from the browser gives the attachment; the CSS gives the page. Nothing else was needed. |

**Not indexed, three ways, because one is not enough for a page that names a
venue and a price:** `robots: { index: false, follow: false }` on the route, the
standing `Disallow: /hq/` in `robots.ts`, and absence from `sitemap.xml`. All
three verified against a running server in §7.

## 2. The one deliberate correction to E11.11 §2, and why it is not a rewrite

`E11.11-12` §2's "What you see" block reads, as one paragraph:

> Which invitations you sent, which couples opened them, and adoption figures
> across your couples as a group.

**D-027 point 4** narrowed the launch portal to invitation administration only;
adoption figures follow after 1 September. So that sentence is true at renewal
and untrue on the day a venue starts. **D-032 R4** made the fix mandatory rather
than optional, and `E09.11-copy-system.md` §5.1a fixes the wording, requires it
verbatim, and names this proposal by document and line as a surface that does
not yet carry it.

So the module renders **E09.11 §5.1's three-part block** in place of E11.11 §2's
two-part one. The positive half and the negative half are unchanged. The third
part is added, as its own block, at heading weight:

> **At launch.** At launch you see your invitations and who opened them.
> Adoption figures follow later in the year.

§5.1a's words: *"A surface that takes this block verbatim takes all three
parts."*

**This is a boundary request applied, not a unilateral rewrite.** `E11.11-12` §2
still needs the same amendment in its own document. That is E11.11's to make and
it is raised in the packet. Recorded at `src/lib/venue-proposal.ts` under the
`[LAUNCH-SCOPE]` comment, which cites the decision rather than describing the
change.

**The negative half travels with the positive half.** Asserted structurally, not
by eye: the test pins the block order so `what-you-see`, `what-you-never-see`
and `at-launch` are adjacent and in that order. Splitting them across a proposal
and an FAQ is how a trust claim becomes a trust problem (`E11.11` §2.1).

## 3. Forbidden wording — measured, not asserted

Two independent sweeps, and they disagree about nothing.

**Sweep A — the module's own test**, `src/lib/venue-proposal.test.ts`, 38
regular expressions under `REFUSED`, run against the **filled** proposal and
again against the **unfilled** one so a placeholder cannot smuggle in what a
paragraph may not say:

P1 permanence (`for life`, `forever`, `lifetime`, `in perpetuity`, `perpetual`,
`never expires`, `guaranteed`) · P2/D-016 (`solicitor`, `accountant`, `legally
approved`, `legally reviewed`) · P3 (`GDPR`, `fully compliant`, `bank-grade`) ·
P5 (`allotment`, `seat count`, `per-seat`) · P6 (`no seats`) · P8 (`dietary`,
`allerg*`) · P12 (`+ VAT`, `ex VAT`) · P13 (`act now`, `limited time`, `while
places last`, `only N places left`) · D-027.3 (`co-branded`, `your logo`,
`welcome message`) · D-001.4 (`couple separately consents`) · SaaS fluff ·
the four banned programme terms (`partner`, `investor`, `exclusive`,
`certified`) · em dash · exclamation mark.

**Result: zero hits.** 16 tests, 16 pass.

**Sweep B — `prohibited-claims.v1.json` applied to the three E12.09 files**, using
the `[venue-copy]` semantics copied line-for-line from the extended
`check-venue-edition-contract.mjs`:

```
### src/lib/venue-proposal.ts  ·  E12.09
    hits 0, cited 0
### src/components/venue/venue-proposal-sheet.tsx  ·  E12.09
    hits 0, cited 0
### src/app/hq/venue-proposal/composer.tsx  ·  E12.09
    hits 0, cited 0
```

**Sweep B found two hits on the first run and both were fixed.** They were in
the module's own header comment, which named the rules it obeys using the words
those rules forbid: `allotment` (P5, line 45) and `venue-written` (D-027.3,
line 47). The header already claimed it avoided exactly this. It did not.

The fix was to label the refusal sets by **rule id alone**. That is not
fussiness: `prohibited-claims.v1.json` has no heuristic for "naming, not using"
— every exemption is an explicit citation entry, by design, because the
heuristic version once suppressed two real defects on the live venues page.
Keeping the descriptions would have meant asking WP-06 for two citation entries
to cover a comment. An id costs nothing and reads the same.

## 5. One copy of the words, and the one place that is not yet true

`src/lib/venue-proposal.ts` is the only copy of the **proposal** in the
repository, and the sheet and the composer both import it.

**But the price exists twice in the repository.**
`src/lib/venue-invitation/copy.ts` (E12.02/E12.03) independently holds
`"€1,000 a year, prepaid, including VAT at the prevailing rate."` and
`"The standard rate is €1,500 a year, prepaid, including VAT at the prevailing
rate."`

**Both are correct today.** Verified by reading both files on 2026-08-03. But
this module's own header says a second copy of a price is how two prices happen,
and there is now a second copy. Nothing currently fails if one changes and the
other does not.

**Recommendation, carried to the packet:** one cross-module test asserting the
two sources agree on both figures and on the VAT sentence. It is a few lines,
it belongs to whichever of E12.02/03 and E12.09 the founder prefers, and it is
not written here because it would edit a file this task does not own.

## 6. One page on paper — measured, not guessed

`venue-proposal.css`: `@page { size: A4; margin: 16mm; }`, sheet at 9.6pt /
1.42 line height, h1 at 16pt, `break-inside: avoid` with `orphans: 2; widows: 2`
on every block.

Page furniture is hidden two ways, deliberately. The `body:has(.venue-proposal-sheet)
> *:not(:has(.venue-proposal-sheet))` rule only ever fires on a page that
actually contains a sheet, so it cannot affect any other page in the app; and
the slot form carries Tailwind's `print:hidden` at its own call site, where it
is visible to whoever edits that component.

**What was not done, stated plainly.** The A4 fit was not verified by generating
a PDF and counting pages. The type scale and the break rules are set so that the
twelve blocks fit, and the print stylesheet is correct, but **nobody has printed
it.** That is a five-minute check the founder can do from the page itself, and
it is the one claim in this document that rests on arithmetic rather than
observation.
