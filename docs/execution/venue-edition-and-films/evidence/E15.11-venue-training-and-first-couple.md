# E15.11 — venue training and the first real couple: the executable script

**Task:** E15.11 · **Written:** 2026-08-04 by Claude Code (WP-22)
**Status:** a runnable script. Nothing was run against a real venue or couple.

---

## 1. What only a human can do

Train a person, watch a couple redeem, and hear what the venue found hard. All of
it.

## 2. The two things this task actually protects

**The term the couple receives.** Not the term on the screen. Read the database.
See E15.03 step 4 to step 7 for the exact queries and the three date cases.

**What the venue says to couples.** R-020 is the exposure with the widest blast
radius in the programme: enormous care is taken over Signal Studio's own words,
and then the story is handed to twenty-five businesses with no obligation
attached. A coordinator saying "yours forever" at a show-around is a commercial
practice under the Consumer Protection Act 2007, it folds into the venue's own
contract with the couple, and a Signal Studio discontinuation becomes the venue's
consumer problem and then the venue's claim against Signal Studio.

## 3. The script

### Step 1 — Hand over the copy pack and the prohibited-claims sheet, first

**Only the founder can do this.**

- **Do:** give the venue the approved copy pack (`evidence/E09.11-venue-copy-pack.md`)
  and a one-page prohibited-claims sheet, **before** the venue speaks to a couple.
- **The prohibited list, verbatim:** no "forever", no "guaranteed", no permanence
  or storage promise, no "GDPR compliant", no claim that legal review was
  obtained.
- **Fail:** training that starts with the product and gets to the words later.
  The words are the part that creates liability.

### Step 2 — Train the right person

- **Do:** train whoever speaks to couples, which is usually the coordinator and
  usually not the person who signed.
- **Fail:** training the signer only.

### Step 3 — The venue requests its first invitation

**Not run.**

- **Do:** the venue asks for an invitation through the route it actually has.
- **Expect:** the request reaches Signal HQ reliably and is acknowledged.
- **Fail:** a route that depends on the founder happening to see an email.

### Step 4 — Issue and watch the redemption

**Not run. Needs a real couple.**

- **Do:** issue the code, watch the couple redeem, and stay available for the
  first hour.
- **Then read the database**, per E15.03:

  ```sql
  SELECT id, workspace_id, started_at, expires_at, notes
  FROM entitlements WHERE notes = 'comp:<code>';
  ```

- **Expect:** `expires_at` = `max(redemption + 548 days, wedding date + 90 days)`.
- **Fail:** if the app integration branch is not deployed, the couple receives a
  flat 548 days regardless of their wedding date. **That is the current
  production behaviour.** Verified 2026-08-04: the fix and its nine passing tests
  are in `_wt-integrate` and are not in production.

### Step 5 — The answer to the question every couple asks

- **The question:** "how long do we keep this?"
- **The answer the venue is given, and it must match the agreement:** eighteen
  months from when you set it up, or three months past your wedding, whichever is
  later. After that it stays as a read-only keepsake for as long as the service
  exists, and you can export everything at any time.
- **Never:** forever, for life, in perpetuity, or any storage guarantee.
- **Note honestly:** the Keepsake state described in the second sentence has no
  product surface today (see `evidence/E15.05-shared-timeline-and-keepsake-test.md`).
  The venue is being trained to describe something that is promised and not yet
  built. That is a founder decision to take before this training happens, not
  after.

### Step 6 — Record the friction

- **Do:** write down every point of friction in the venue's own words, including
  the ones that are the venue's confusion rather than a defect. The confusions
  are the ones that repeat across twenty-five venues.
- **Log:** every support request in the first week, with its resolution and how
  long it took.

## 4. Results recorded 2026-08-04

| Step | State |
|---|---|
| 1 to 6 | **NOT RUN** — no venue, no couple |
| Term correctness in production | **FAILS** — R-015 fix undeployed |
| Keepsake answer in step 5 | **describes a promise with no product behind it** |

## 5. What I would tell the founder

Do not run this task before the app integration branch is deployed. Training a
venue to tell couples "or three months past your wedding, whichever is later"
while production writes a flat 548 days puts the venue in the position of having
said something untrue, which is precisely the situation R-020 is about, except
that we caused it.

And settle the Keepsake sentence before anyone is trained to say it.
