# E09.01 — Venue Edition event taxonomy and metric data dictionary

**Dictionary version: `account-metrics.v2`**
Instrumentation version: `instrumentation.v1`
Calendar: `Europe/Dublin` (venue-local), configurable per account
Prepared 2026-08-03 · Evidence for E09.01, input to E07.04, E07.11, E09.03

> **This document is the dictionary.** It supersedes
> `studio/docs/venue-portal/METRIC_DICTIONARY.md` (`venue-metrics.v1`), which was
> written for the retired 15-venue code-allotment model and is stale against
> both D-020 and shipped code. The definitions of the six adoption metrics live
> in the companion document, `E09.02-metric-definitions.md`. This one defines the
> events those metrics are computed from and the fields that may never travel.

---

## 0. The version-name decision, made once

Three shipped files pin `account-metrics.v2`
(`studio/src/lib/account/fixtures.ts`, `live/project-venue-access.ts`,
`live/project-venue-usage.ts`). One pins `venue-metrics.v1`
(`studio/src/lib/account/schema.ts:658`). No document defined what
`account-metrics.v2` contained, and the coverage envelope requires a dictionary
version on every response, so the ambiguity was load-bearing.

**Determination.** `account-metrics.v2` is the canonical identifier.
`venue-metrics.v1` is retired as a name. `schema.ts:658` is corrected to
`account-metrics.v2` as part of E09.03. There is one dictionary and it is this
one.

---

## 1. What a venue is being sold, and what that means for measurement

A venue pays an annual licence and gifts a private planning workspace to every
couple who books with it. **The couple owns the workspace.** The venue sees three
things and no fourth: invitation administration, activation evidence, and
privacy-safe aggregate adoption evidence.

Two consequences bind everything below.

**Aggregate is not a presentation choice, it is the product.** There is no
biggest user, no per-couple row, no person ranking, no leaderboard. A metric that
resolves to one couple is not suppressed, it is not built.

**Signal Studio must never learn which couples hold real bookings.** The venue
states that a couple has a signed booking; Signal issues the invitation and never
audits the claim. Every denominator in this dictionary is therefore drawn from
codes Signal itself issued and redemptions Signal itself recorded. Defining a
denominator as "couples with a signed booking at this venue" would convert Signal
Studio from a processor of pseudonymous aggregates into a processor of couples'
contractual booking status with their venue, and would re-open the
forbidden-payload tests. It is prohibited, permanently, and the prohibition is
enforced by the never-list in §6.

---

## 2. Three tiers, deliberately kept apart

Everything measurable about Venue Edition falls into one of three tiers. They
have different privacy postures, different retention, different suppression, and
they must never be merged into one stream.

| Tier | What it is | Carrier | Identity | Retention | Suppression |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — Product meaningful action** | A couple committed a real action in one of the four products. | `venue-meaningful-action.v1` event stream | Salted hashes only | 35 days raw, projection survives | Behavioural: 3 / rates: 5 |
| **2 — Commercial lifecycle fact** | An invitation was issued, redeemed, expired, released; a term started or ended. | Durable rows in `license_codes`, `redemptions`, `sponsors`, `entitlements` | Commercial record, no behavioural content | Per existing access policy | **None.** Contract record, not observation. |
| **3 — Audience aggregate** | Someone looked at a public Timeline. | Timeline publication counters, session-hash dedup | No identity retained at all | Receipts expire at 30 min; counters persist | Behavioural: 3 |

Tier 2 is **not events**. It is state, already durable, already reconciled, and
computing it from an event stream would introduce drift against the contract
record for no gain. This is a determination of fact and closes the E09.03
question of what an "invitation event" is: there isn't one, and there should not
be one.

## 3. Tier 1 — the product meaningful-action event

### 3.1 The shape, unchanged

`venue-meaningful-action.v1`, frozen 2026-07-27, implemented at
`app/src/lib/account/instrumentation/event-schema.ts` and byte-mirrored in
`studio/`. Exactly seven fields. An event carrying any other key is **rejected,
not trimmed** — a silent trim would let a future field leak by accident.

```ts
type VenueMeaningfulActionV1 = {
  eventId: string;                     // idempotency key, day-bucketed
  instrumentationVersion: "instrumentation.v1";
  product: "notes" | "tasks" | "timeline" | "signal";
  kind: string;                        // allowlisted, must match its product
  occurredAt: number;                  // ms since epoch, UTC
  subjectIdHash: string;               // sha256(salt + ":" + clerkUserId), 32 hex
  workspaceIdHash: string;             // sha256(salt + ":" + workspaceId), 32 hex
};
```

The raw `workspaceId` and `subjectId` are forbidden precisely because their
hashed forms are the supported carrier. If the hash is unavailable, the event is
not emitted at all.

### 3.2 When an event fires

Three rules, all already enforced in code and all non-negotiable.

1. **After commit, never before, never inside.** A rolled-back action produces no
   event. `recordSponsoredUse(input, committed)` returns immediately on
   `!committed` (`app/src/lib/account/instrumentation/call-site.ts`).
2. **A page load is not an action.** Route loads, refreshes, heartbeats, focus
   events, prefetches, opening Settings, switching products, viewing an empty
   surface, autosave retries that change nothing, system seed/migration/demo
   actions, and anything performed by a Signal operator in support view-as are
   all excluded. None of them may satisfy any metric in this dictionary.
3. **One object, one kind, one venue-local day, one event.** The `eventId` is
   `sha256(product, kind, objectKey, subjectId, venueLocalDate)`, truncated to 32
   hex. A hundred autosaves of one note collapse to one
   `note_materially_edited`; a dropped response replayed produces the same id and
   lands on the same row.

> **Determination of fact, and it changes a metric name.** Because the event id is
> day-bucketed, a count of Tier 1 rows is a count of **action-days**, not raw
> actions. Two different tasks completed today is 2. The same task completed,
> reopened and completed again today is 2, not 3, because `task_completed`
> collapses. Any surface calling this "meaningful actions" overstates precision.
> The dictionary name is **meaningful action-days**, and the venue-facing word is
> "days of activity", never a raw action total.

### 3.3 The allowlist

A kind must belong to its product. `task_created` emitted from `notes` is
rejected.

| Product | Kind | Fires when | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Notes | `note_created` | A note insert committed. | **Live** — `notes/server/actions/notes.ts:1252` |
| Notes | `note_materially_edited` | A note update committed and changed persisted content, via the compare-and-swap. | **Live** — `notes.ts:1313` |
| Tasks | `task_created` | A task insert committed. | **Live** — `server/actions/tasks.ts:522` |
| Tasks | `task_completed` | A lane move committed into `done` from a non-done lane. | **Live** — `tasks.ts:123/162/189` |
| Tasks | `task_reopened` | A lane move committed out of `done`. | **Live** — same call sites |
| Tasks | `task_status_changed` | A lane move committed between two non-done lanes. A no-op move emits nothing. | **Live** — same call sites |
| Tasks | `task_reassigned` | An assignee change committed, diffed against a pre-read. | **Live** — `tasks.ts:429` |
| Tasks | `task_rescheduled` | A due-date change committed, diffed against a pre-read. | **Live** — `tasks.ts:429` |
| Timeline | `timeline_curated` | A milestone overlay upsert committed. | **Live** — `timeline/server/actions/workspaces.ts:419` |
| Timeline | `timeline_published` | A publish committed with at least one visible node. | **Live** — `workspaces.ts:613` |
| Timeline | `timeline_visibility_changed` | **An unpublish committed.** See the trap below. | **Live** — `workspaces.ts:650` |
| Timeline | `timeline_created` | A Timeline workspace or its first project committed. | **Proposed, E09.03** |
| Signal | `briefing_deliberately_opened` | A user explicitly opened a generated briefing. One per briefing per subject per day. Demo mode excluded. | **Live** — `signal/app/signal-ledger-actions.ts:80` |
| Signal | `briefing_acknowledged` | A user acknowledged a briefing. | **Allowlisted, no call site anywhere.** See §3.5. |

### 3.4 Trap: `timeline_visibility_changed` is not a share

It fires on **unpublish** and only on unpublish. Publishing emits
`timeline_published`. Any metric that reads `timeline_visibility_changed` as
evidence of sharing counts a couple taking their Timeline *down* as a couple
putting it *up*. This is recorded here because the kind's name invites exactly
that misreading, and the misreading produces a number that is wrong in the most
embarrassing possible direction.

**E09.03 correction:** rename to `timeline_unpublished` and reserve
`timeline_visibility_changed` for nothing. Until renamed, the definition in
E09.02 reads `timeline_published` only.

### 3.5 Trap: `briefing_acknowledged` is structurally zero

It is allowlisted and has no caller anywhere in `app/src`. A Signal engagement
metric built on acknowledgement reports zero forever, and — worse — a
product-reach figure that treats Signal as covered will understate Signal reach
while looking complete.

**Determination.** Until it has a call site, `briefing_acknowledged` is marked
`unwired` in the allowlist and Signal's coverage mask reports **one of two kinds
instrumented**, not complete. Whether to remove it from the allowlist entirely is
a founder choice, listed in E09.02.

### 3.6 Trap: two identity spaces

The contract states `subjectIdSource = "clerk-user-id"`, and the ingest join
closes on `redemptions.user_clerk_id` and nothing else
(`studio/src/lib/account/instrumentation/ingest.ts`). Notes, Timeline and Signal
pass the raw Clerk id. **Tasks passes `getCurrentUser()`**, which resolves
`users.id` via a lookup on `users.clerk_id`. For rows created after the Phase-A
consolidation these coincide. Any surviving legacy row where they differ produces
two subject hashes for one person and splits that person's counts between Tasks
and everything else.

**E09.03 must verify this against production before the sink is switched on**, and
the verification is an acceptance criterion, not a nice-to-have. Splitting subject
identity silently understates every distinct-workspace count.

### 3.7 Trap: two null holes that look like inactivity

`recordSponsoredUse` returns early when `workspaceId` is absent. Two paths hit
this legitimately:

- **Unfiled Notes.** `notes.workspace_id` is nullable by design — Unfiled is a
  normal, durable state. Every note captured before filing is unattributable.
- **Unlinked Timeline.** `timeline_workspaces.suite_workspace_id` is nullable
  while legacy Timeline workspaces remain connected. Such a workspace can curate,
  publish and unpublish without producing one attributable event.

Both are correct privacy behaviour and both are indistinguishable from "they did
nothing". They are therefore **coverage facts, not zeros**, and the coverage mask
in §7 must carry them.

### 3.8 Trap: salt rotation is a coverage break

`subjectIdHash` and `workspaceIdHash` are salted with `SPONSOR_USAGE_HASH_SALT`.
Rotating it intentionally breaks continuity: counts before and after a rotation
must not be compared, and the rollup must treat a rotation as a coverage break
rather than a drop in activity. The ingest layer stamps `hash_salt_epoch` on every
row precisely so a rotation is detectable. **Nothing currently detects or records
a rotation at report time** — E09.03 closes this.

## 4. Tier 2 — commercial lifecycle facts

Not events. Rows. Read directly, never projected through the pseudonymous stream,
and **never suppressed**, because they are the direct contract record between
Signal Studio and the venue rather than behavioural observation of a couple.

| Fact | Definition | Read from | Venue-visible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Invitation issued** | A canonical code row minted against the venue's active licence. Revoked and redeemed rows still count as issued. | `license_codes` | Yes, as a count and as a masked code list to owner/manager |
| **Invitation redeemed** | A distinct canonical code row with one successful redemption. A same-user retry stays one redemption; cross-user reuse is rejected. | `redemptions` × `license_codes` | Yes |
| **Invitation outstanding** | Issued minus redeemed minus revoked minus expired. | Derived | Yes |
| **Last redemption** | Most recent successful redemption, rendered as a venue-local **date**, never a timestamp, never with a person. | `redemptions` | Yes |
| **Access term start** | The redemption date. | `redemptions` | Aggregate only |
| **Access term end** | `max(redemption + 18 months, wedding date + 3 months)` per D-010. | Derived — **not computable today**, see below | Aggregate only |
| **Workspace released** | The venue released a workspace under the D-020 release rule. Branding off within 24 hours. | `workspace_sponsorships.status = 'revoked'` | Yes, as a count |
| **Keepsake entered** | Access term end has passed; the workspace is free, read-only, exportable. | Derived from term end | Aggregate count only |

### 4.1 What D-020 deleted from the old dictionary

The retired `venue-metrics.v1` built its whole access section on a cap:
*licences allotted*, *codes remaining* = `max(0, allotted − issued)`, *codes
issued ... because issue consumed allotment*, and a required test reading *"two
concurrent mint requests cannot exceed allotment"*.

**Under D-020 there is no allotment in the commercial terms and nothing to
reconcile.** The shipped code has already been corrected — `MetricValue` carries
`{ state: "unlimited" }`, `format.ts` renders *"unlimited. Every couple who books
is covered"*, refuses a rate against an unlimited denominator, and
`project-venue-access.ts` suppresses every headroom count and low-allotment
warning via `isUnlimitedSponsor()`. **The code is right and the old dictionary is
wrong.** This is the reverse of the usual drift and must not be "fixed" by
touching the code.

Retired here, permanently: *Licences allotted*, *Codes remaining*, *Redemption
rate as headroom*, any *low allotment* attention item, and the phrase "codes
remaining" in any venue-facing surface.

**Retained:** *Invitations issued* stays, and stays as the redemption denominator.
It is a count of things Signal did, not a budget the venue manages, and it is the
only denominator available that does not require knowing who holds a booking.

### 4.2 The access-term gap, stated plainly

D-010 ratified "18 months from redemption, or 3 months past the wedding date,
whichever is later". **It cannot be computed today.** `redeemCompCodeAction` sets
`expires_at = now + duration_days`, a flat number, and nothing reads the wedding
date. `venue-edition.ts` pins `VENUE_EDITION_COUPLE_ACCESS_DAYS = 548` and the
mint throws on any Venue Edition code whose duration is not exactly 548 (R-015).

So *Access term end*, *Keepsake entered*, and any "couples approaching Keepsake"
population are **defined here and not yet measurable**. They render `unavailable`
with reason `not_instrumented`, never as zero and never as an 18-month
approximation dressed as the ratified rule.

## 5. Tier 3 — audience aggregate

Applies only to a Timeline the couple chose to publish. Feeds E06.07.

| Fact | Definition | Read from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Qualified view** | One view of a published Timeline, deduplicated by a browser-generated session hash whose receipt expires after 30 minutes. No IP, referrer or user-agent is retained. | `timeline_publications.qualified_view_count`, `audience_view_receipts` |
| **Currently published** | `timeline_publications.published_at IS NOT NULL` at snapshot time. | Publication row |

**Venue visibility of Tier 3 is a founder decision, not a technical one.** The
aggregate is computable and privacy-clean. Whether a venue should be told how many
strangers looked at couples' wedding pages is a judgement about what the product
is. It is listed in E09.02's founder choices. Default in this dictionary:
**founder-only until ratified.**

`session_hash` and `token_hash` are internal-only and appear in §6.

## 6. Fields that may NEVER appear in a venue-facing payload

Rejected at any nesting depth, in events, projections, DTOs, CSV, PDF, API
responses, email, accessibility text, tooltips, and support view-as. Rejected, not
stripped.

### 6.1 The frozen list, enforced today

`title`, `name`, `body`, `content`, `text`, `description`, `note`, `comment`,
`email`, `emailAddress`, `phone`, `url`, `href`, `path`, `slug`, `filename`,
`attachment`, `clerkId`, `userId`, `workspaceId`, `subjectId`, `code`,
`accessCode`, `token`, `secret`, `ip`, `userAgent`.

### 6.2 Additions required by Venue Edition

**Booking and relationship status — the D-020 boundary.** Signal Studio does not
hold, process, infer or report whether a couple has a signed booking with a venue.

`bookingStatus`, `bookingReference`, `bookingConfirmed`, `contractSigned`,
`depositPaid`, `isBookedCouple`, `venueContractRef`.

**Couple and wedding identity.**

`coupleName`, `partnerName`, `partner1`, `partner2`, `weddingDate`, `primaryDate`,
`ceremonyDetail`, `venueOfCeremony`, `guestCount`, `guestList`, `dietary`,
`allergy`, `rsvp`.

**Private planning content and structure.**

`projectName`, `projectSlug`, `taskTitle`, `taskId`, `noteId`, `noteTitle`,
`milestone`, `milestoneLabel`, `label`, `tag`, `checklist`, `budget`, `amount`,
`supplier`, `vendor`, `briefing`, `briefingText`, `summary`.

**Relationship data.**

`collaborators`, `collaboratorCount`, `members`, `memberList`, `invitees`,
`inviteEmail`, `assignee`, `assigneeName`.

**Internal keys that exist but must not travel.**

`sessionHash`, `tokenHash`, `publicationId`, `shareToken`, `publicUrl`,
`hashSalt`, `saltEpoch`, `subjectIdHash`, `workspaceIdHash`.

> `subjectIdHash` and `workspaceIdHash` are the *carriers inside* Tier 1 and are
> legitimate there. They are forbidden **in a venue-facing payload**, because a
> stable opaque key handed to a venue across two reports becomes a per-couple
> row, which is the exact thing the aggregate posture forbids. The projector
> emits counts. It never emits keys.

### 6.3 The two rules behind the list

1. **No venue-facing value may resolve to one couple.** Not by name, not by key,
   not by a suppression threshold that a determined reader can subtract around.
2. **Consent cannot override the private-content boundary.** A couple consenting
   to share a note with their venue is a feature the product may one day build.
   It is not this measurement path, and it never travels on it.

## 7. The coverage envelope

Ten fields, carried with every usage response. **No usage metric may render
without this envelope.**

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `metric_dictionary_version` | `account-metrics.v2` |
| `instrumentation_version` | `instrumentation.v1` |
| `window_start`, `window_end` | Venue-local inclusive bounds |
| `data_through` | Last venue-local date with a closed rollup |
| `coverage_state` | `complete \| partial \| suppressed \| unavailable` |
| `covered_modules`, `missing_modules` | Which of the four products were instrumented for the window |
| `covered_days`, `expected_days` | Rollup completeness |
| `suppression_reason` | `small_group \| incomplete_telemetry \| not_instrumented \| none` |

**Missing is never zero.** `assertNoZeroForAbsent()` in
`studio/src/lib/account/instrumentation/suppression.ts` throws
`"absent metric rendered as a value"` if it is. A zero is a claim that nothing
happened; these states say the opposite, that we do not know. Per-product daily
counters are nullable for the same reason: **null means not instrumented, zero
means instrumented and quiet.**

Partial coverage presents as a `lower_bound` with its denominator, phrased "at
least N". Rates, changes, comparisons and rankings are withheld entirely when
coverage is partial.

## 8. Suppression

Enforced in the projector, never in the interface. Ratified in D-011 point 3 and
already implemented at the named thresholds.

- Behavioural values are withheld below **3** eligible sponsored workspaces.
- Rates, medians and cohort comparisons are withheld below **5**.
- Suppression applies to screen, CSV, PDF, API, email and support view-as.
- The response carries a **suppression reason, never the hidden number**, and the
  number does not leak through tooltips, exports or accessibility text.
- Tier 2 commercial facts are **not** suppressed. They are the contract record.

## 9. Retention

| Data | Retention | End action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 raw pseudonymous event | **35 days** | Hard delete after rollup and late-arrival repair |
| Daily sponsor usage aggregate | 24 months after period end | Delete or fold into annual totals |
| Frozen cohort counts | 24 months | As above |
| Report snapshot and export | 24 months, or earlier on venue request | Delete object and payload; keep audit proof only |
| Tier 3 audience view receipt | 30 minutes | Expire; the counter survives, the receipt does not |

**Hard deadline, worth stating separately.** Day-30 cohort bands seal from raw
events. If the nightly sealing job falls more than 20 days behind, a band closes
over days whose raw events were already swept at 35, and seals permanently
`indeterminate`. That is unrecoverable. The cron secret being unset is therefore
not a minor operator to-do; it is a clock running against data that cannot be
rebuilt.

## 10. Honest state of the system, recorded once

Every definition in this dictionary and in E09.02 has been proven against
fixtures and an in-memory SQLite engine. **None has ever been computed from a
real couple's action.**

- `app/src/lib/account/instrumentation/sink.ts` is `let emitted = 0`. All 13 live
  call sites terminate there. No table, no network, no file.
- Emission requires `SPONSOR_USAGE_EVENTS=1` and a `SPONSOR_USAGE_HASH_SALT` of
  at least 16 characters. Neither appears in `src/env.ts` or `.env.example`, so
  in production today even the in-memory counter does not tick.
- The `sponsor_usage_daily` migration is unapplied (operator to-do
  `apply-sponsor-requests-migration`).
- `/api/cron/sponsored-use` answers 401 until `CRON_SECRET` is set (operator
  to-do `schedule-sponsored-use-jobs`).

The only venue-adjacent number shipping today is `reachedBoard` from
`/api/internal/partner-stats`, sourced from `entitlements.reached_board_at`,
which is **stamped on a board page render**. It is a page load. Under this
dictionary it is disqualified as evidence of use and must not be presented as
activation to anyone.

## 11. What E09.03 has to build

1. A durable Tier 1 store and a real sink, replacing the no-op.
2. The eligibility join: event hashes → sponsored workspace set → venue. Requires
   the projector to hold the live salt.
3. A daily rollup writing `sponsor_usage_daily`, with the nullable per-product
   counters.
4. Salt-rotation detection at report time.
5. Subject-identity verification for Tasks (§3.6) before the sink goes live.
6. `timeline_created`; rename `timeline_visibility_changed` → `timeline_unpublished`.
7. Access-term computation per D-010, unblocking Keepsake entry (R-015).
8. A Keepsake export event. Keepsake does not exist in the product at all today.
9. `schema.ts:658` → `account-metrics.v2`.
10. Banner the four stale `venue-portal/` documents as historical.
