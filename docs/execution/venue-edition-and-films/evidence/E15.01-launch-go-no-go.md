A checklist that says "verify the gates" is worth nothing. This is the review as
a runnable sequence: a command or a click, the expected result, and a pass/fail
line with a stop rule. Where a step was run on 2026-08-04 the real output is
recorded under **Result**. Where it was not run, it says so and why.

- **Run:** `grep -n "noindex" "_wt-integrate/src/app/p/[slug]/page.tsx"` and
