## 1. The three things that change the plan

### 1.1 There is no venue-authenticated route anywhere in the product

D-027 point 4 makes the portal at launch "invitation administration only". Administration
implies an administrator. There is no venue login. Every Account surface sits behind `/hq`,
gated by **one shared password** (`studio/src/lib/hq/auth.ts:3,23-25`) that also opens
Today, Money, Company, Vault, Atlas, Decks, Founders Circle and the Data Room. The live
snapshot returns `members: []` unconditionally, "Invite member" only sets a notice string,
and support history is a hardcoded two-row array — so the four roles and the 13-row
capability matrix in `lib/account/roles.ts` govern nothing on the live path.

Twenty-nine days remain, and the UI freeze is in seventeen.

A lab ran and brought four options rather than picking:
`evidence/E07.16-venue-identity-lab.md`.

**Recommendation: Option C for 1 September** — Signal HQ administers on the venue's behalf
and the venue receives evidence rather than a login. It is the only option that already
passes its acceptance criteria rather than newly risking them, and it matches D-027 point 4
exactly. Hold **A′** (C plus a read-only venue link) as a two-day follow-on decided after
the first three venue conversations. Do not start **B** (a real venue account with members
and roles) before the freeze. One person administering 25 venues is bounded at roughly two
top-ups a working week and breaks somewhere past 60–80 venues — beyond the Founding 25 and
beyond this release, so it is an accepted bound rather than an open risk.

**Answerable with: approve C, or name a different option.**

### 1.2 The launch scope of E07 is smaller than E07 reads, and that is ratified

D-027 point 4 narrows D-001 point 5. The audit marked each task honestly:

- **Launch scope (invitation administration):** E07.01, .02, .03, .07, .08, .09, .10, .16,
  .17, .18.
- **Post-launch (the aggregate adoption-evidence layer):** E07.04, .05, .06, .11, .12, .13,
  .14, .15. Still specified to the same standard, still audited, still carrying evidence —
  but the gap is not built for 1 September.

**E07.08 was the largest unbuilt thing in the package and it is now built.** Invitation
administration did not exist in any form; `sponsor_activations` modelled the full lifecycle
at `entitlements-db/schema.ts:255-325` and **nothing read or wrote it**. Create, get-link,
mark-sent, resend, withdraw, replace and close-off now exist end to end, exercised in a real
browser against the real database, with server-enforced type-to-confirm, per-sponsor
authorisation and a chained audit row. **No email leaves the system** — "send" means marking
an invitation issued and producing the wording and the link for a person to send, and the UI
says so.

**Answerable with: approve the launch/post-launch split, or push back on which tasks sit
where.**

**Adversarial verification found four red gates that the build agents reported as green.**
That is the part worth knowing: the packages' own summaries were wrong in four places, an
independent pass measured rather than read, and all four are now closed.

1. **`studio pnpm test` was red** and died at step 5 of 15, so ten checks and 478 tests
   behind it — including two of this wave's own new check scripts — never executed at all.
   Cause: WP-05 rewrote two contract documents to add prohibition lists, and a prohibition
   list names the words it forbids. Closed by citing those lines explicitly through the
   gate's own citation mechanism, and **`METRIC_DICTIONARY.md` ratcheted from a pin of 5
   to 0** in the same change.
2. **The instrumentation contract check was red.** studio's `event-schema.ts` was CRLF, the
   app's was LF, content byte-identical after stripping carriage returns. The check exists
   precisely to stop a one-sided change to a duplicated contract, and it was failing over a
   line ending. Normalised, and `.gitattributes` added in **both** repos so it cannot recur.
3. **The retired `venue-metrics.v1` name had six pins, not three.** Two of them were SQL
   column DEFAULTs in `scripts/migrate-sponsor-usage.mjs`, wired to `npm run
   account:migrate-usage` — so any row inserted without an explicit version landed under
   the retired dictionary. The existing guard covered only the drizzle path and structurally
   could not see them. Corrected, and the guard extended to cover SQL defaults.
4. **`app pnpm test` could not be run as documented.** pnpm 11 aborts on its
   dependency-verification purge without a TTY and exits 1 having run nothing, which is
   indistinguishable from a real failure. Fixed in `app/.npmrc`.

### The map, measured rather than described

A verification agent rendered stills and decoded the pixels. It found two things no check
could see:

- **The recipient's name plate was sitting on four other venues' dots at 16x9** (two at each
  vertical format). Every assert was green because the only test asked whether the plate
  covered *the dot it names*. For a film whose whole argument is "a field of peers, and
  you", one in six peers was being deleted. **Fixed:** the placement search now takes the
  peer positions, slides along its edge to clear them, and the assertion is widened to *any*
  dot, over every real pin, at every legal name width. Fixing it exposed a second defect —
  the plate then slid onto the region caption and cut it in half — which is also fixed and
  also now asserted.
- **The River Shannon was drawn at a 1.099:1 contrast ratio** on a frame that was 97.6% pure
  white. Its geometry was correct and it would have disappeared entirely under the profile's
  own h264 4:2:0 CRF 18 encode. The choice was not subtle water against loud water, it was
  water against no water on the delivered file. Raised to the `inkGhost` DS token (about
  1.48:1) — a sanctioned token, so `check:ds` still passes. **The motion lane may take it
  further; it may not take it back below this.**

Both fixes were confirmed by re-rendering and looking: 25 dots visible, caption readable,
estuary legible with the Tarbert–Glin pinch and the Lough Derg arm in the right places, one
indigo dot, one name.

## 4. What was deliberately not done, and why

- **Nothing was committed, pushed, deployed or sent**, in any of the three repos. R-025
  blocks any studio deploy; `signal-motion/AGENTS.md:58` forbids committing without granted
  authority.
- **No task was marked Done.** All 30 are in Founder Review.
- **The entitlements migration was not applied.** It is inert until someone runs it, one of
  its defaults needs your ruling first, and migrating a shared database is a mutation nobody
  asked for.
- **No images were generated for E09.08.** I have no image-generation tool in this session.
  What was delivered instead: the provenance register (schema plus a check script that
  verifies every referenced file exists, hashes match, and no entry marked `containsFaces`
  reaches a surface R-011 forbids), an audit of every image on a demonstration surface across
  both repos, the shot list and prompt pack written to R-011's rule — *environments, details,
  hands, tables, flowers, light over faces; no close-up generated faces in either film or on
  the public Timeline demo; if a shot cannot be made to feel real, cut it* — and the QA gate
  as a runnable checklist. Generation itself needs a tool or a person.
- **E13.01, .02, .05–.14, .17, .18 were not touched.** Motion lane.
- **The motion, the water's tonal value and the river's cut ends** are handed to the motion
  lane with E13.05 as a defensible baseline, not an answer.
- **Three duplicate register IDs were recorded, not renumbered.** `DECISIONS.md:980` and
  `:1179` are both **D-028**; `RAID.md:430` and `:693` are both **R-025**; `RAID.md:443` and
  `:776` are both **I-007**. Renumbering is change-controlled and needs a cross-file sweep;
  a previous renumbering required repointing every reference across three files. `I-011`
  already records that markdown registers lose entries under concurrent sessions. **This is
  that failure mode continuing, and it will keep happening while Waves 3–5 run in parallel.**
- **`public/brand/assets/merchants/`** holds ten third-party trademarks (Apple, Booking.com,
  Irish Rail, Netflix, One4All, Ryanair, Aer Lingus, Starbucks, Three, Uber) with no licence
  and no recorded basis, publicly served, rendered by nothing. Recommended for deletion.
  Not deleted — that is your call, not mine.
- **`public/brand/collateral/venue/wedding-seed.md:19`** contains a synthetic guest health
  record ("3 coeliac, 2 vegan, 1 nut allergy (groom's side, table 9)") under `public/`, with
  an open P1 operator todo telling someone to type it into a real workspace. Flagged, not
  edited — it is exactly the artefact R-017 warns about.
