## Why a review and not a capture

`experience:capture` cannot produce the evidence this gate asks for, for these
surfaces. Established by running it:

- Capture only runs `capture-plan.json`'s **14-item pilot set**. Six of the
  seven are not in it, so there is no route to capture them at all.
- The seventh, `studio.page.design`, **is** in the pilot and captured cleanly
  (4/4). Its coverage stayed `partial`, because the registry requires 4 states ×
  4 breakpoints = **16 captures** and the plan provides one state = 4.

So `complete` coverage is unreachable through the current capture plan for every
surface, piloted or not. Expanding the plan is `approve-experience-golden-set`
territory and is a founder gate by design. That is the real fix and it is not
this document.

**What this review is:** rendered content and structure against the running dev
server for the public routes, the served HTML for the static artifacts, and the
source diff for the two authenticated HQ pages. **What it is not:** pixel
screenshots at four breakpoints in four states. Nobody should read the
re-baselined hashes as visual-regression evidence, because they are not.

## What I checked on `/venues`, against E12.04's own ten criteria

Read from the running page, not from source:

- **€1,500 standard and €1,000 founding both present**, and *"Both prices include
  VAT at the prevailing rate"* — D-021.
- **"The first twenty-five venues pay €1,000. That is €500 a year less, for as
  long as you stay."** — D-009's ratified wording, exactly.
- **"The rate holds while the agreement keeps renewing, and there is no clause
  that expires it"** — the lock stated as continuous-renewal. **No "for life",
  no "forever", no "in perpetuity"** anywhere. This is the R-008 trap and the
  page avoids it.
- **"fourteen days and assigned when payment clears"** — the 14-day hold and
  numbering-on-payment, both D-009.
- **"eighteen months, or three months past the wedding, whichever is later"** —
  D-010 plus the D-022 grace rule, stated twice.
- **"Every couple with a booking gets the full suite. No seats. No per-couple
  maths."** — D-020's unlimited entitlement, with no number anywhere.
- **"An activation register is in development"** — a capability that is not
  built, stated as not built. AC8.
- **"A couple's Notes, Tasks and unpublished Timeline remain inaccessible"** and
  *"Couple names, dates and work are absent by default"* — the privacy boundary
  stated in the venue's own terms.
- **No claim of legal, solicitor or accountant approval** anywhere. D-016.

The page is correct and it is a material improvement on what production served
until today, which was €1,500, "the first fifteen venues", no VAT line and a
flat eighteen months — four ratified decisions unrepresented on a live page.

## Finding — "founding partner" survives in the market-entry deck

`public/brand/market-entry-deck-2026.html` contains **"founding partner" four
times**, including *"The founding partner variant · presented at signing"*.

Two problems with it:

1. **"partner" is a banned programme term** on E12.04's own criteria list
   (partner, member, investor, exclusive, guaranteed, certified).
2. The ratified programme name is **"Founding 25"** (D-009). "Founding partner"
   is a different programme with different implications, and *"presented at
   signing"* means it is venue-facing.

**Not introduced by the VEF commit.** `06bc713` touched those lines only to fix
mojibake (`Â·` → `·`); the term predates it. `/design` carries it too, from
another lane's commit `233bd3a`.

**Not fixed here**, deliberately — it is outside E12.04's scope, it spans two
lanes' surfaces, and changing venue-facing deck copy is not something to do
inside a hash re-baseline. Recorded as R-042 and left open.
