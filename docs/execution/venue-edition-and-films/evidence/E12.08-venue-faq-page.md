## 3. The departure that matters: the GDPR answer

**This is a conflict between two ratified instruments and it is not reconciled
silently.**

**What §10.2 says.** "What exists is the paperwork: a written record of who is
responsible for what, a data processing agreement and a security schedule, and a
privacy notice written for the couple."

**Why that cannot be published today.** E09.10 §6 Answer 1 carries explicit
**conditions of use**: the answer may be given as written *"once E03.01 (role
map), E03.04 (couple privacy notice) and E03.05 (DPA and security schedule)
exist as drafts a venue could actually be sent, and the R-017 special-category
position is decided rather than open."*

Checked, not assumed:

| Artifact | State | Source |
|---|---|---|
| E03.01 role map | **exists** | `evidence/E03.01-gdpr-role-map.md` |
| E03.04 DPA, security schedule, subprocessor schedule | **backlog** | `BACKLOG.md:69` |
| E03.05 couple terms and privacy notice | **backlog** | `BACKLOG.md:70` |
| R-017 special-category position | **open** | `RAID.md:359` |

Three of the four conditions are unmet. Publishing §10.2 as written would put a
list of documents that do not exist in front of twenty-five businesses, on a page,
under the heading "Are you GDPR compliant?". That is the exact failure class P3
and D-016 exist to prevent, and it would be worse than the question being asked
on a call.

**What was published instead.** E09.10 §6 Answer 1's own supplied interim answer,
verbatim, plus the parts of §10.2 that are true today:

> Nobody can hand you a GDPR certificate, because there isn't one. The paperwork
> is being written now and you will get all of it before you sign. I would rather
> show you a finished document than describe one.
>
> What is already fixed is the shape of it. Reporting is aggregate only, with
> small numbers held back so a figure can never point at one couple. Nothing
> anyone at Signal Studio sends you describes what a couple is planning. If you
> have someone who reads this sort of thing, I would rather they read it now.

The second paragraph's claims were checked against the read path, not asserted:
every behavioural metric on `projectVenueAccessSnapshot` is `unavailable`, and
`privacyReceipt.neverIncludes` names "Note, task, or Timeline content".

**Recommendation.** Approve the interim form and swap it for §10.2's full text
the day E03.04 and E03.05 have drafts and R-017 is decided. The alternative —
publishing the full answer now — is not available.

## 5. Renewal, and where it actually lives

The task title names renewal. There is no separate renewal question in §10.2 and
none was invented. Renewal is answered inside answer 4 — *"That rate holds for as
long as the agreement renews continuously without lapse. It follows the property,
so it survives a sale or a rebrand"* — and inside answer 10, the survival
sentence. Both are the ratified positions (D-009.3, D-009.4, D-020.2).

**Recorded as a gap rather than filled:** a venue asking "what happens at the end
of year one" gets the rate answer but not a process answer, because no decision
describes the renewal process. Inventing one would be a commercial term written
by a build agent.

## 6. Two questions deliberately absent

E09.11 §10.3 keeps both off any page and the page respects it, and says so
plainly rather than leaving a hole:

- **Insurance.** No policy is recorded anywhere in this programme. E09.10 §6
  Answer 3 version B is the only permitted answer and §10.3 says it is given by a
  person, not read off a page without the conversation around it. **OQ-2 is still
  open** and this page does not close it.
- **Where is our data held.** P15 blocks any region, country or transfer claim
  until E03.05 verifies it. §10.3: "A written FAQ entry cannot carry that
  subtraction safely, so there is no written entry until E03.05 exists."

The page carries one short block naming both and saying they are answered by a
person. It makes no claim about either.
