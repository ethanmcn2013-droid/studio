## 1. The thing to understand before running anything

**There is no payment provider for venues.** Stripe carries the workspace,
studio, wedding and event tiers and has no venue price id. Venue money is
invoiced out of band, lands in a bank account, and the cleared payment is
recorded by hand through one script. That is the process, not a gap to be
apologised for, and the script says so in its own header.

Everything the record has to get right is therefore concentrated in one command.
It writes to an append-only table with triggers that refuse UPDATE and DELETE.
**A mistake here is permanent by design.** Rehearse first.

## 2. What only a human can do

- Send the invoice or order form and get it paid.
- Confirm the payment cleared, and the date it cleared. Nothing detects a
  cleared payment automatically. The operator todo
  `apply-venue-edition-terms-migration` records this in its own words: *"nothing
  detects a cleared payment automatically, so the first founding numbers need an
  operator action that does not exist until E08. The field is ready; the trigger
  is not."*
- File the Revenue MyEnquiries submission.
- Decide the VAT treatment to record, or decide to record none.

### Step 8 — File the Revenue submission, then invoice

**Only the founder can do this.**

- **Do:** file the submission drafted at
  `evidence/E02.07-revenue-myenquiries-submission.md` through Revenue
  MyEnquiries. Record the submission reference and date here.
- **Then:** issue the invoice or order form. It must state the price as
  **inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate** (D-021). It must not state or
  imply that an accountant has verified the treatment (D-016).
- **Stop rule:** R-018 and R-022 both direct that the submission is filed before
  any agreement is signed. It is free. If it is skipped, record that it was
  skipped and who decided, rather than leaving the record silent.

### Step 9 — Record the cleared payment against production

**Only the founder can do this, and only after money has actually cleared.**

- **Run:**
  ```bash
  cd studio
  pnpm tsx scripts/mark-venue-paid.ts <venue-slug> founding
  ```
- **Omit `--vat-rate` unless the Revenue reply has determined the treatment.**
  The rate is then recorded as "not determined", which is true. Recording zero
  is a claim.
- **Expect:** `recorded`, EUR 1,000, one founding number.
- **Paste the real output here.**
- **Fail:** `refused` for any reason, STOP and read the reason before retrying.
  `already_recorded` on a first run means the payment was already recorded by
  something else, which is itself a finding.

### Step 11 — Confirm the venue sees the right number

**Only the founder can do this.**

- **Fail:** any mismatch between the certificate and `sponsors.founding_number`
  is a stop. Two venues told the same number is the one failure D-027 point 2
  was created to prevent.

## 5. What I would tell the founder

The billing path is in better shape than anything else in E15. It has one
writer, append-only history, an idempotency guard, an audit line, a founding
number assigner that runs after payment rather than before, and an integrity
checker that catches every failure mode I could think of before I thought of it.

Two things are missing and neither is code.

1. **The Revenue submission is still unfiled.** It is free, it is drafted, and
   R-018's irreversible half turns on it. Filing it costs an evening.
2. **Nothing has ever run.** Every guarantee above is proven against test
   databases. The first production run is the test, and it is permanent. Do the
   rehearsal at step 5 first, on a disposable database, and do it before the
   first venue says yes rather than on the evening it does.
