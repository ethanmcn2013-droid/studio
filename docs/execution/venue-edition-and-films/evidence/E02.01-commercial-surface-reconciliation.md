## E · Escalations

**1. The send-ready outreach was aimed at the wrong region.**
`venue-edition-A1-staged.md` holds five ready-to-send emails naming Clontarf
Castle, Harvey's Point, The Montenotte, the Imperial and Waterford Castle. Those
are Dublin, Donegal, Cork and Waterford. VEF-2026 limits founding outreach to a
45-minute drive-time ring from Limerick city centre (D-012). Sending them would
offer a Greater Limerick founding place to five venues outside the region. Prices
and counts were corrected so nothing states a retired number; **a DO NOT SEND
banner was added rather than making wrong-geography emails look ready.** Re-target
against the E10 universe first. Owner: WP-11 and WP-02.

**2. The lender pack is externally distributed and its arithmetic is now wrong.**
`business-loan-pack-2026.html` underwrites on flat €1,500 venue revenue. At the
founding rate the first 25 venues produce €25,000 rather than €37,500, and
VAT-inclusive pricing takes roughly 19% off that again if Signal Studio is an
accountable person. Correcting a document that may already sit with a lender is a
founder call, not an agent's.

**3. Two gates were dead or blind, and both are now fixed.**
`check-suite-contract-consumers.mjs` looked for a `../tasks` directory that the
July infrastructure reset renamed to `app`. It had been reporting "missing
generated fixture" and then crashing on an undefined path, so all three of its
sections checked nothing. `check-venue-edition-contract.mjs` only forbade the
*pre*-July-11 price band, so it reported ok while ~140 files published the
July-11 position. It now fails on the retired cohort size and on any permanence
wording, with a narrow documented exemption for the two documents whose job is to
define the ban.

**4. Structured data publishes one price.** `src/app/layout.tsx` carries a
site-wide JSON-LD `Offer` at €1,500, and the contract check explicitly forbids
`priceSpecification`, `lowPrice` and `highPrice`. Publishing the standard price
is defensible and honest. Publishing the founding rate as well would need that
forbid list relaxed. Not changed unilaterally: site-wide structured data is
externally visible and E12.04 owns it.

**5. `revenue-model-datapack-2026-07-20.json` needs one confirmation** on
whether any `/hq` surface still reads it, which decides whether it is a live
contract or a dated snapshot.

**6. The loan pack promises an accountant who, by decision, does not exist.**
Three lines say VAT, tax, fees and timing "require accountant confirmation", that
the founder "completes incorporation, banking, VAT, and accountant review before
drawdown", and that an "accountant validates VAT, tax, fees, and timing". D-016
says there is no accountant and no solicitor. A lender pack describing a control
that will not happen is a different problem from a wrong number, and rewriting it
to say "no accountant" materially weakens the lender narrative. **Left untouched
deliberately.** Either D-016 gets an exception for this one engagement, or those
three lines change. Founder's call.

**7. The loan pack states ARR "ex VAT" while venue prices are now VAT-inclusive.**
The kicker on the revenue slide now says so explicitly, but the underlying figures
are the inclusive amounts. If Signal Studio is an accountable person, the venue
ex-VAT figures are roughly 19% lower than shown. The Revenue MyEnquiries
submission at `evidence/E02.07-revenue-myenquiries-submission.md` is what settles
it, and it is free.

**8. The approval sweep.** The command this packet originally suggested,
`approve-batch "note" --review`, approves everything in the founder-review queue
rather than one package. Run during a four-package wave, it moved 32 tasks across
E01, E02 and E04 to Done under the note `"your note"`; a second sweep took 11 more
across E09 and E11 under `"note"`. The tasks are approved; what is thin is the
record of why. The packet's command has been corrected to require explicit IDs.
