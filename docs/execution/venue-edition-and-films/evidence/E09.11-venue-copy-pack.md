# Venue Edition · the copy pack

**For the venue team. Print it. Pin it above the desk.**

Version `venue-copy-pack.v1` · 2026-08-03 · prepared by Signal Studio
Companion to `E09.11-copy-system.md`. The prohibited list in §4 is generated from
`copy/prohibited-claims.v1.json`, which is the same list the automated check
enforces.

---

## 0. Why this exists

Your couples will ask your team about the planning workspace, and your team will
answer in their own words. That is right, and nobody wants a script.

Two things follow from it. What a venue tells a couple about a product becomes
part of what the venue promised that couple. And a sentence that is warm and
slightly wrong is easier to say than a sentence that is exact.

So this is the short version: sentences that are true, and the specific ones that
are not. Say it your own way. Keep the facts.

---

## 1. The three sentences that matter most

If your team remembers nothing else.

> **They get a private place to plan the wedding. It is a gift from us, and it
> costs them nothing.**

> **We can see that they opened it. We cannot see what is in it.**

> **After the wedding it stays open, read-only, and they can download everything
> in one click.**

---

## 2. Answers your team will need

**"What is it?"**
> A private place to plan your wedding. Notes, the actual work laid out, a plan
> you can share with family and suppliers, and a short note in the morning when
> something needs attention. It opens ready to use.

**"Who is it from?"**
> Us. It is part of booking with us.

**"What does it cost me?"**
> Nothing. There is nothing to pay and nothing to sign up for.

**"How long do I have it?"**
> 18 months from when you open it, or 3 months past your wedding, whichever is
> later. If you move the date, it moves with you.

**"Can you see what I write in it?"**
> No. We can see that you opened it. We cannot see what is in it. If you want us
> to see something, you send it to us, the same as you would send it to anyone
> else.

**"Can I share it?"**
> Yes, with anyone you like. They can read it without an account. We do not see
> who you shared it with.

**"What happens after the wedding?"**
> It stays open, read-only, and you can download everything in one click.

**"Do I have to use it?"**
> No. It is there if it is useful.

**"Who do I ask if something goes wrong?"**
> Signal Studio support it, not us. There is a contact link inside it.

---

## 3. Two things to say carefully

**On how long it lasts.** Say *"18 months from when you open it, or 3 months past
the wedding, whichever is later."* Do not say *"18 months"* on its own. A couple
with a two-year engagement who hears the short version will believe they lose it
before the day, which is the one thing this term was designed to prevent.

**On what happens afterwards.** Say *"for as long as the service exists"* if you
are writing it down. Software companies do not get to promise permanence, and a
couple who was promised permanence has been promised something nobody can deliver.

---

## 4. Never say these

Each of these is a promise that cannot be kept, or a claim that is not ours to
make. If a couple asks something this list covers and you are not sure, "I'll
find out and tell you" is always a good answer.

### About how long anything lasts

- **forever** · **for life** · **lifetime** · **yours for life** · **permanent** ·
  **never expires** · **in perpetuity** · **always**
- **guaranteed** · **we'll always be here** · **we'll keep it safe**
- **unlimited storage** · **backed up forever**

*Say instead:*

> It stays open, read-only, for as long as the service exists, and you
> can download everything in one click.

### About data protection and security

- **GDPR compliant** · **fully compliant** · **GDPR certified** · **DPC approved**
- **audited** · **bank-grade security** · **military-grade encryption** ·
  **100% secure** · **your data is safe with us** · **end-to-end encrypted**
- **your data never leaves Ireland** · **EU only**

*Say instead:*

> It is private to you. I can put you in touch with Signal Studio if
> you want the detail.

### About legal and financial review

- **legally approved** · **solicitor-approved** · **reviewed by our legal team** ·
  **counsel has confirmed** · **accountant verified**

*Say instead:*

> I don't know, and I would rather find out than guess.

### About what the venue can see

- **we can see your plan** · **we get a copy** · **it feeds through to us** ·
  **we can check on your progress**
- **send us your dietary list through it** · anything that says guest dietary,
  allergy, accessibility or health information reaches us through the workspace

*Say instead:*

> We can see that you opened it. We cannot see what is in it.

### About price, to a couple

- **free** · **complimentary** · **worth €** · **normally €** · **we paid for
  this** · **included in your package** *(unless our own brochure says so)* ·
  **sponsored**

*Say instead:*

> It is part of booking with us.

### About the product

- **seamless** · **effortless** · **turnkey** · **revolutionary** ·
  **transforms your planning** · **best-in-class** · **AI-powered** ·
  **it decides for you** · **it knows what you need**
- **seats** · **licences** · **your allotment** · **codes remaining** ·
  **you have X of your 60**

*Say instead:*

> Every couple who books with us gets one.

### About sharing

- **secure link** · **private link** · **only the people you send it to can see
  it** · **unlisted**

*Say instead:*

> Anyone with the address can open it, so keep it to what you are
> happy for a guest to read.

> "I don't know, and I'd rather find out and tell you than guess."

Then ask us. That sentence is never a weak answer.
