## 5. DECISION POINT — recorded, not silently reconciled

`scripts/check-venue-edition-contract.mjs` used to **require**
`price: String(VENUE_EDITION_ANNUAL_PRICE_EUR)` in the root layout's
structured data ("one exact offer", guarding the retired price range). The
panel applied the ratified E11.11 rule — *a price never travels without its
seventeen conditions; a surface that cannot carry them does not carry the
number* — to structured data, where a search rich-result would surface a bare
€1,500 at the standard rate on the very page that states the founding offer.

This branch removes the price field from that one offer and **inverts the
check with equal strictness** (the offer must remain; the price must not).
The revert is one swap of two checks. **The founder ratifies whichever
stands, at merge.**
