# E09.02 — Adoption metric definitions

**Dictionary version: `account-metrics.v2`** · Companion to `E09.01-event-taxonomy.md`
Prepared 2026-08-03 · Evidence for E09.02 · **Proposed for founder ratification in E07.04**

Six definitions, written so that two people computing them independently get the
same number. Each carries: the exact rule, the event or table it is computed
from, whether a venue sees it, the suppression that applies, and the misreading
it has to head off.

**Read the last section first if you are ratifying.** §9 separates what the data
can support (determination of fact, not yours to overrule) from what is genuinely
a choice about where to draw a line (yours, and a reasonable person could pick
differently).

---

## 1. The denominator rule, before anything else

Every ratio below has a denominator drawn from **codes Signal Studio issued and
redemptions Signal Studio recorded**. Never from bookings.

**Why this is not negotiable.** The obvious denominator for "how many of our
couples are using it" is "couples with a signed booking at this venue". Adopting
it would require Signal Studio to know, per couple, whether a booking exists, is
signed, and is current. That converts Signal from a processor of pseudonymous
aggregates into a processor of couples' contractual status with their venue. It
re-opens the forbidden-payload tests, puts `bookingStatus` into the data model,
and hands the venue a per-couple reconciliation surface — which is a per-couple
row wearing a commercial hat.

**The rule.** "Signed booking" is a **venue-side eligibility statement that
Signal never audits per couple.** The venue asserts it when it invites. Signal
issues the invitation, counts invitations, counts redemptions, and stops.

So the two denominators in this document are:

- **Invitations issued** — for the redemption step only.
- **Invitations redeemed** — for everything after redemption, because a couple
  who never redeemed has no workspace and cannot be expected to act in one.

Both are Tier 2 commercial facts (E09.01 §4): unsuppressed, reconcilable, and
blind to booking status by construction.

**Corollary the venue must be told once, plainly.** A low adoption percentage can
mean couples are not using it, or it can mean the venue invited couples who
weren't ready. Signal cannot tell those apart and will not pretend to. The
denominator label always reads "invitations redeemed", never "your couples".

## 2. First useful action

### Rule

A sponsored workspace has taken its **first useful action** at the earliest
`occurredAt` of any Tier 1 meaningful-action event where all of the following
hold:

1. the event validates against `venue-meaningful-action.v1`;
2. its `workspaceIdHash` attributes to a workspace sponsored by this venue,
   through `redemptions → license_codes → sponsors`;
3. `occurredAt >= redemption timestamp` for that workspace;
4. the event is not excluded (system, seed, migration, demo, or operator
   view-as).

**Any one qualifying event of any kind in any of the four products is
sufficient.** The value is recorded once per sponsored workspace and is
**monotonic** — a later-arriving earlier event may move it earlier during the
35-day repair window; nothing may move it later, and nothing may unset it.

The **venue-visible metric** is the count of sponsored workspaces that have taken
a first useful action, against a denominator of invitations redeemed.

### Computed from

Tier 1 event stream → `sponsor_usage_daily` first-action projection.
Attribution join on `redemptions.user_clerk_id`. Not from any table today: this
metric does not exist until E09.03 replaces the no-op sink.

### Venue-visible

**Yes**, as a count with its denominator. Never as a date, never per couple.

### Suppression

Count withheld below **3** eligible sponsored workspaces. Any percentage form
withheld below **5**. Renders `unavailable` — never 0 — when telemetry coverage
for the period is absent.

### What it is NOT

- **Not a login, and not reaching the board.** `entitlements.reached_board_at` is
  stamped on a page render. It is the only activation signal shipping today and
  it is disqualified. Anything currently showing "reached board" as activation is
  showing a page load.
- **Not redemption.** Redeeming a code creates a workspace. It is not use.
- **Not "setup complete", not onboarding, not a checklist.**
- **Not a per-couple time-to-value.** The median hours-from-redemption figure that
  exists in the old dictionary resolves toward individuals in small cohorts and
  is founder-only.
- **Not reversible.** A couple who acts once and never returns has still taken a
  first useful action. That is what the metric means, and pairing it with recent
  use (§3) is how the picture stays honest.

## 3. Recent use

### Rule

A sponsored workspace is **in recent use** if it recorded at least one Tier 1
meaningful action on any of the **30 complete venue-local days ending at
`data_through`**.

- Complete days only. Today is never in the window, because today's rollup has
  not closed.
- `data_through` is the last venue-local date with a closed rollup, and it is
  displayed with the number every time.
- A workspace counts **once**, whatever its volume. Ten actions and one action
  are the same workspace.
- Denominator: sponsored workspaces **currently within their access term**
  (D-010). Workspaces that have entered Keepsake are excluded from both numerator
  and denominator — a read-only keepsake cannot record a meaningful action, and
  leaving them in the denominator would make every venue's number fall
  mechanically over time for no behavioural reason.

### Computed from

`sponsor_usage_daily`, distinct `workspaceIdHash` across the window. Denominator
from `redemptions` filtered by computed access-term end.

**Blocked:** the access-term end is not computable today (R-015 — the mint pins a
flat 548 days and nothing reads the wedding date). Until D-010's grace rule is
implemented, the denominator falls back to **all redeemed workspaces**, and the
coverage envelope carries `suppression_reason: not_instrumented` on the term
filter so the substitution is visible rather than silent.

### Venue-visible

**Yes**, as a count with its denominator and `data_through`.

### Suppression

Withheld below **3** eligible sponsored workspaces. Percentage form withheld below
**5**. Partial coverage renders "at least N", never a rate.

### What it is NOT

- **Not logged in recently.** Sessions are not measured. Only committed actions.
- **Not engagement intensity.** There is no volume in this number and there will
  not be. A venue asking "who is using it most" is asking for a person ranking.
- **Not a last-seen date, per couple or in aggregate.**
- **Not comparable across a salt rotation.** A rotation is a coverage break, not a
  drop in activity, and the report must say so rather than show a cliff.
- **Not zero when telemetry is missing.** Missing renders `unavailable`.

## 4. 30-day continuation

### Rule

For a **closed cohort** of sponsored workspaces grouped by the venue-local date of
their first useful action: the share that recorded at least one Tier 1 meaningful
action on **any venue-local day 25 through 35 after the day of first useful
action**, inclusive.

- Day 0 is the day of first useful action. The band is days 25–35, eleven days
  wide.
- A cohort is **not eligible** until day 35 has fully elapsed for every member.
- A workspace is **excluded from the cohort**, and the excluded count is shown,
  if any of the following touch its band: a coverage gap, a salt rotation, or a
  sealing run that closed the band after the 35-day raw-event sweep (which seals
  `indeterminate` and is unrecoverable).
- Percentages are recomputed from **frozen, versioned cohort counts**. No
  venue-readable row per person or workspace is ever retained.

### Computed from

Frozen cohort rollup, sealed nightly. Depends on the sealing job, which answers
401 today.

### Venue-visible

**Yes**, as a share with the denominator always shown next to it — "9 of 12" and
not "75%" alone.

### Suppression

Withheld below **5** eligible activations in the cohort. This is the rate
threshold, not the behavioural one, and it binds harder than anywhere else: a
venue with 20 weddings a year will rarely have 5 workspaces first-acting in the
same cohort. **Expect this metric to be legitimately unavailable for most venues
for most of year one.** That is a true statement about small venues, not a defect.

### What it is NOT

- **Not churn.** The complement is not "abandoned". A couple planning a wedding
  eighteen months out has genuinely quiet months, which is why the band is eleven
  days wide rather than a single calendar day.
- **Not "still active today".** That is recent use (§3).
- **Not a retention rate over the venue's whole book.** It is one cohort, and
  cohorts are not summed or averaged across for a headline figure.
- **Not comparable between venues.** Cohort composition differs. No league table,
  no benchmark, no "above average for your size".

## 5. Product reach

### Rule

For each of the four products independently — Notes, Tasks, Timeline, Signal —
the number of **distinct sponsored workspaces** with at least one Tier 1
meaningful action in that product within the reporting window.

- One workspace can count in up to four products, once per product.
- **The four numbers do not sum to anything.** They are four independent counts
  over overlapping sets.
- Each product's count carries its own coverage state. A product that is not
  instrumented for the window renders `null`, meaning not instrumented, which is
  categorically different from `0`, meaning instrumented and quiet.

### Computed from

Per-product distinct workspace counts in `sponsor_usage_daily`.

### Venue-visible

**Proposed: no — founder-only, with a single collapsed number shown to the
venue.** This is a founder choice (§9.5), and the argument against venue
visibility is E07.06's own thesis: "Timeline 6, Notes 8, Signal 5" is a
product-centric metric that invites a venue to reason about our feature adoption
rather than their couples' experience. The venue-facing form proposed here is
one sentence: *"Couples used Signal Studio across all four products."* or
*"Couples used three of the four products."*

### Suppression

Each product count suppressed **independently** at **3**. A product below the
floor is withheld while its neighbours show — and the withheld one must not be
inferable from a total, which is a second reason the four numbers never sum.

### What it is NOT

- **Not a ranking.** No "most used product", no ordering, no bar chart sorted by
  size.
- **Not a share of total.** There is no total.
- **Not intensity.** A workspace with one note counts identically to one with
  four hundred.
- **Not an honest read of Signal today.** Signal has two allowlisted kinds and
  only one has a call site (`briefing_acknowledged` is structurally zero).
  Signal's reach is therefore understated by an unknown amount and its coverage
  mask must say "one of two kinds instrumented" rather than "complete". See §9.8.

## 6. Timeline creation

### Rule

A sponsored workspace has **created a Timeline** when it has committed at least
one owner-initiated Timeline shaping action: today `timeline_curated`, and
`timeline_created` once E09.03 adds it.

Counted **once per workspace, ever**. Not windowed — creation is a state a
workspace reaches and keeps.

### Computed from

Tier 1 events, product `timeline`.

### Venue-visible

**Yes**, as a count against invitations redeemed.

### Suppression

Withheld below **3**. Percentage form withheld below **5**.

### What it is NOT — and this is the important one

- **Not "has a Timeline".** The app derives Timeline nodes from Tasks projects.
  Nearly every workspace *has* a Timeline the moment it has tasks. That fact is
  meaningless as a metric, and a venue told "24 couples have a Timeline" is being
  told nothing about couples. **Creation means the couple shaped it** — a
  committed milestone overlay, an owner decision — which is the only Timeline
  signal that distinguishes anyone from anyone.
- **Not publishing and not sharing.** A curated Timeline is private until
  published. Those are §7.
- **Not having a wedding date.**
- **Not attributable when the Timeline workspace is unlinked.**
  `suite_workspace_id` is nullable while legacy Timeline workspaces are connected,
  and an unlinked workspace can curate all day without emitting one attributable
  event. That is a coverage fact carried in the envelope, never a zero.

## 7. Timeline sharing

### Rule

Two distinct metrics. They are not the same number and must never be presented as
one.

**Ever shared.** A sponsored workspace has committed at least one
`timeline_published` event, with at least one visible node, at any time. Counted
once per workspace, ever. A later unpublish does not remove it.

**Currently shared.** A sponsored workspace whose Timeline publication row has
`published_at IS NOT NULL` at snapshot time. Computed from **state, not from the
event stream**, because the event stream after a 35-day sweep cannot answer a
"currently" question.

### Computed from

Ever shared: Tier 1 `timeline_published`.
Currently shared: `timeline_publications.published_at`, read at snapshot.

### Venue-visible

**Currently shared: yes.** **Ever shared: founder choice (§9.6)** — a couple who
published and then took it down made a private decision, and a venue counter that
remembers the publish forever arguably reports something the couple retracted.

### Suppression

Both withheld below **3**. Percentage forms withheld below **5**.

### What it is NOT

- **Not `timeline_visibility_changed`.** That kind fires on **unpublish** and only
  on unpublish. Reading it as sharing counts a couple taking their page down as a
  couple putting it up. E09.01 §3.4 renames it; until then, any implementation
  reading it for this metric is wrong.
- **Not viewer counts.** Whether anyone looked is Tier 3 and a separate decision
  (§9.7).
- **Not access.** The venue does not get the public URL, the share token, or a
  link. It gets a count.
- **Not endorsement.** A published Timeline is a couple sharing their plan with
  their guests. It is not a testimonial, not consent to be a case study, and not
  permission to use anything on it. D-010 point 1: couples are asked for nothing
  by default; any case-study use is a separate, specific, opt-in permission.
- **Not a marketing surface.** D-011 point 2 caps venue attribution on a public
  keepsake at one footer line. No logo, no badge, nothing in the viewport.

## 8. Sentences a venue may actually be shown

Derived from the six definitions above, replacing the allotment-era examples in
`VENUE_FACING_CLAIMS.md` which presume a finite issuance budget that D-020
abolished.

**Complete coverage.**

- "You have invited 24 couples. 18 have opened their workspace."
- "14 of those 18 have started planning."
- "11 used Signal Studio in the last 30 days. Data is complete through 24 July 2026."
- "9 of 12 couples in the March group came back around a month after they started."
- "6 couples have shaped their own timeline. 4 have a timeline live for their guests."

**Partial coverage.**

- "At least 8 couples used Signal Studio in this period. Usage data is partial for 3 of 30 days."
- "Invitations and redemptions are complete. Product use is unavailable for this period."

**Below the floor.**

- "Not enough couples yet to report usage safely."
- "Redemptions are visible. Usage appears once at least three couples are eligible."

**Still prohibited, unchanged.** "Your most active couple is…", "Alex and Sam used
Tasks 42 times", "This couple is disengaged", "Signal Studio saved 120 planning
hours", "Your Venue Edition generated €14,000 in value", "Usage is down" when
coverage or denominators changed, and any note, task, project, briefing, comment,
attachment, collaborator or private Timeline content.

**Also now prohibited under D-020:** "codes remaining", "licences allotted",
"low allotment", and any sentence containing "X of your 60".

## 9. For E07.04: fact versus choice

### 9.0 Determinations of fact — not open

These are what the data can and cannot support. Ratifying a different answer
would produce a number that is wrong, not a number that is different.

1. **A page load can never be activation.** `reached_board_at` is disqualified.
   The only venue-adjacent number shipping today is built on it.
2. **The denominator cannot be booking-attributed.** §1.
3. **A Tier 1 row count is action-*days*, not actions**, because the event id is
   day-bucketed. Any surface saying "meaningful actions" overstates precision.
4. **`timeline_visibility_changed` fires on unpublish.** It cannot mean sharing.
5. **`briefing_acknowledged` has no call site.** Any metric built on it is
   permanently zero.
6. **Unfiled Notes and unlinked Timelines are unattributable.** Coverage, never
   zero.
7. **Salt rotation breaks continuity.** Coverage break, never a drop.
8. **Access-term end and Keepsake entry are not computable today** (R-015).
   They render `unavailable`, not approximated.
9. **Suppression thresholds are 3 and 5**, ratified in D-011 point 3 and already
   implemented and tested. Not reopened here.
10. **Nothing in this document has ever been computed from a real couple's
    action.** The sink is a no-op counter and emission is off in production.
    Ratifying these definitions ratifies definitions, not measurements.

### Genuinely open — the founder's call

Each is a place where a reasonable person could draw the line elsewhere and still
be right. A recommendation is given, but the recommendation is not the decision.

**9.1 — The recent-use window: 30 complete days.**
Alternatives: 28 days (aligns to whole weeks, so the number stops wobbling with
the day of the month), or 14 days (catches disengagement sooner, but a couple
eighteen months out is legitimately quiet for a fortnight). *Recommend 30.* It
matches the continuation band and reads naturally as "this month".

**9.2 — The bar for a first useful action: one committed action.**
Alternative: a higher bar, such as an action in two different products, or three
actions. *Recommend one.* Higher bars make the metric better at predicting
retention and worse at answering the question the venue actually asked, which is
"did the gift land". One action is also the only bar that cannot be gamed by
onboarding design.

**9.3 — The bar for Timeline creation: one committed curation.**
Two live documents disagree today. This one says one curation.
`planning-period-analytics.md` says a wedding activates on a four-condition
bundle including at least three milestones. **Neither is marked authoritative and
E07.04 must pick one**, because leaving both live is how two teams report two
numbers for the same word. *Recommend one curation* for the venue-facing metric,
and keep the four-condition bundle as a separate founder-only product-learning
definition under a different name.

**9.4 — The continuation band: days 25–35.**
Alternative: exact day 30, or a wider 21–40. *Recommend 25–35.* A single calendar
day mislabels ordinary weekly use as churn; wider than eleven days starts
overlapping the recent-use window and measures the same thing twice.

**9.5 — Whether product reach is venue-visible per product.**
Proposed: no, collapse to one sentence for the venue and keep the four numbers
founder-only. Alternative: show all four, which is what the old dictionary did.
*Recommend collapsing.* E07.06 exists to replace product-centric metrics with
customer outcomes, and four product counts is the clearest example of the thing
it is meant to remove. The counter-argument is real: a venue that sees Timeline
adoption may sell the product harder to the next couple.

**9.6 — Whether "ever shared" is venue-visible, or only "currently shared".**
Proposed: currently shared only. A couple who published and then took their page
down made a private decision, and a counter that remembers the publish forever
reports something they retracted. Alternative: show both, since the venue gifted
the thing and ever-shared is the better measure of whether the gift was used.
*Recommend currently shared only.*

**9.7 — Whether public-Timeline viewer counts reach the venue at all.**
Tier 3 is computable and privacy-clean: session-hash dedup, 30-minute receipt
expiry, no IP or user agent retained. The question is not technical. It is whether
a venue should be told how many people looked at couples' wedding pages.
*Recommend founder-only* until E06.07 has a considered answer. A venue reading
audience numbers is one step from asking for the pages.

**9.8 — Whether `briefing_acknowledged` stays allowlisted while unwired.**
Leaving it in understates Signal reach while the coverage mask looks healthier
than it is. Removing it makes Signal's single-kind instrumentation honest but
requires re-adding it later. *Recommend removing it from the allowlist* until it
has a call site, and recording the removal so E09.03 restores it deliberately.

**9.9 — Whether continuation is shown at all before cohorts reach 5.**
Most venues will legitimately never reach five workspaces first-acting in the
same cohort during year one. Option A: show the row with "not enough couples
yet", which is honest and teaches the venue what will appear later. Option B: hide
the row entirely, which avoids drawing attention to an empty promise. *Recommend
A*, because a venue discovering a hidden metric later reads as concealment.

**9.10 — The Keepsake exclusion from the recent-use denominator.**
Proposed: exclude workspaces in Keepsake from both numerator and denominator,
because a read-only keepsake cannot record a meaningful action and leaving them
in makes every venue's number fall mechanically over time. Alternative: keep them
in, so the denominator matches the venue's lifetime invitation count and the
number is never flattered. *Recommend excluding*, with the excluded count shown.

## 10. Acceptance criteria this document supplies

For E09.02, and reusable for E07.11, E04.08 and E15.04, which currently have
empty criteria arrays:

1. Two independent implementers computing each of the six metrics from the same
   fixture set produce identical numbers.
2. Every metric renders with the full ten-field coverage envelope or does not
   render.
3. No metric renders `0` for an absent value; `assertNoZeroForAbsent()` throws on
   any attempt.
4. Behavioural counts below 3 and rates below 5 are absent from screen, CSV, PDF,
   API, email and support view-as, and the hidden number does not leak through
   tooltips or accessibility text.
5. No venue-facing payload contains any field in E09.01 §6, at any nesting depth.
6. No venue-facing value resolves to a single couple at any cohort size.
7. Product reach counts do not sum to a total, and a suppressed product is not
   inferable from its neighbours.
8. `timeline_visibility_changed` appears in no sharing computation.
9. A salt rotation across a reporting window produces a coverage break, not a
   changed number.
10. The redemption denominator query touches no booking, contract or wedding-date
    column, proven by a schema-level test.
