## 1. What only a human can do

Judge. A postmortem is a set of judgements about what went wrong and why, and it
is the founder's to make. What this document contributes is the structure, the
material that already exists, and the obligations list that nobody has written
down yet.

## 2. The postmortem structure

Four sections. The second is the one that matters.

1. **What was delivered.** Against M6: venues signed, venues paid, couples
   invited, films released, actual revenue, actual timeline.
2. **What went wrong.** Dated, with consequences. Not a lessons-learned list.
3. **What the record does not say.** D-026 already flagged this: the 55 bulk
   sign-off notes show that approval happened and not what was judged. The
   postmortem is where that is either repaired or accepted permanently.
4. **What continues.** Section 4 below.

## 3. Four candidates for section 2, visible already on 2026-08-04

Written now because they will be harder to see in December.

**Two implementations of one ratified rule.** D-022's access term exists twice in
this workspace: `src/lib/venue-access-term.ts` plus
`src/server/db/couple-access-term.ts` in the app integration branch, and an
untracked `src/lib/venue-edition-term.ts` plus
`src/server/db/sponsored-access-term.ts` in the dirty `app` working tree, wired
into different call sites. Both are competent. Only one can survive. That is a
coordination failure between lanes, not an engineering one.

**A migrated column nothing uses.** `entitlements.wedding_date` was applied to
the production entitlements database on 2026-08-03. The shipped term code derives
the wedding date from `workspaces.primary_date` instead, and the app schema has
no `wedding_date` at all. A production migration that no code reads is a trap for
whoever reads that column next.

**The branch-versus-production gap at the go/no-go.** On 2026-08-04, both
integration branches were green (742 and 1,263 tests, zero failures) while
production lacked the R-031 noindex fix and four of the five venue sub-pages
returned 404. Green branches read as readiness. They are not.

**Keepsake promised ahead of Keepsake built.** The library exists and passes its
tests. It has zero production callers. The promise is in the charter, the
decision record and the sales narrative.

## 4. The operations transfer, which is the part with no owner today

Everything below outlives M6. Each needs a named owner and a cadence, and "the
founder" is the true answer that should be written as a commitment rather than
left as a default.

| Obligation | Cadence | Runs until |
|---|---|---|
| Twenty-five annual renewals | annual, per venue | while each agreement renews |
| The founding rate lock at EUR 1,000 | permanent | while the agreement renews continuously without lapse |
| One 30-minute founder call per venue | annual | for the life of each agreement |
| Founding-request intake and triage | per request, monthly rollup | ongoing |
| Couple access terms | per couple | **into 2028 and beyond**, because the term is wedding-date relative with no upper cap |
| **The Keepsake tail** | none | **no end date** |
| Entitlements integrity checks | after every money event | ongoing |
| The venue permission register and revocations | on request | ongoing |
| VAT position, revisited | at the Revenue reply and at the threshold | ongoing |

**The Keepsake row is the one to look at twice.** Free, read-only, indefinite
access while the service exists, with an export the couple owns. That is a
storage and availability obligation with no end date, no owner, and no line in
any budget. It is a good promise and it is still an obligation.

## 5. The archive

- **Rule:** archiving is moving and indexing. Nothing is deleted.
- **Rule:** every superseded position stays traceable. What it was, what replaced
  it, when. `evidence/E01.01-superseded-ledger.md` is the existing instrument.
- **Test:** a person can find any programme artefact a year later, using the
  index alone, without asking anyone.

## 6. The honesty requirement

Two constraints carry into every updated document.

1. **Nothing states or implies legal approval, solicitor review, accountant
   verification or unqualified GDPR compliance** (D-016). This is permanent, not
   temporary.
2. **The business plan states the actual outcome**, including the conversion rate
   against the eligible pool. `venue-universe/counts-only.md` records 50 eligible
   accounts inside the ring against a target of 25 signed. Whatever the final
   number is, the denominator belongs in the plan.
