## 2. The timing fact, again, because it changes what is worth building

**Paid agreements today: 0 of 25.** A term is 365 days from the instant payment
clears. The earliest possible renewal is therefore roughly **September 2027**.

The whole renewal path will run in production about a year after launch, and
until then the tests are the only proof any of it works. That is a reason to get
the wording exactly right now, in the eleven days before the **2026-08-15 offer
freeze**, and a reason to build nothing.

Four separate claims, itemised because they fail differently.

1. **"Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel."** There is no

2. **"Cancelling stops the next renewal but does not refund the current

   nobody in this programme has ratified.

4. **"Refund what you paid for that period" within thirty days.** A thirty-day

None of these is necessarily wrong as a destination. All four are currently true
by accident rather than by decision, and one of them describes machinery that
does not exist. `/terms` needs either a Venue Edition carve-out or a correction,
and that is a decision in §8.

## 6. Term start: three options

### Option A. The term starts the day payment clears (recommended)

**For.** It is one indisputable date, it is the same event that assigns the
founding number, it is what the code already does, and it needs no judgement
from anyone.

**Against.** A venue that pays in September and is not onboarded until October
has paid for a month it could not use. With onboarding unrehearsed (R-024) that
gap could be longer than expected.

### Option B. The term starts when the venue can issue its first invitation

**For.** The venue pays for twelve usable months.

**Against.** "Onboarded" is an operational judgement by the person who also
benefits from calling it early, and R-024 records that the first onboarding is
improvised. It creates a disputable date on a document whose whole value is that
its dates are not disputable, and it delays the founding number, which D-009
ties to payment.

### Option C. All 25 founding terms share one anniversary

**For.** The cohort renews together, the founding programme has a clean birthday,
and one renewal season is one piece of work.

**Against.** Twenty-five invoices in one week, once a year, for one person, and
one bad week takes out the whole cohort at once. Cohort 1's payments will
already cluster within weeks of each other, so option A produces most of the
benefit without inventing a rule, and without the venue that pays in month four
being charged for a full year that started in month one.

## 7. Recommendation

**Option A, plus five mechanics.**

1. **The term starts the day the payment clears**, and runs 365 days. The
   renewal date is computed at that moment.
2. **The renewal date is told to the venue on the day it pays**, in the welcome
   message and on the record it receives. A venue that has known its renewal
   date for twelve months is a venue that does not miss it, and this is the
   cheapest missed-renewal prevention available.
3. **First invoice on receipt of the signed order form, payable in 14 days.**
   Already published, and 14 days matches the place hold so the two numbers do
   not disagree.
4. **Renewal invoice issued with the first renewal notice, payable in 30 days.**
   Thirty rather than fourteen because there is no place to protect at renewal
   and 30 days is ordinary practice for Irish B2B. If E02.04's recommended 60-day
   first notice is approved, the invoice is in the venue's hands two months
   before the money is due, which is what makes the notice regime work at all.
5. **Refunds, both directions.**
   - A venue that chooses not to renew simply does not pay. Nothing further is
     owed by either side.
   - A venue that ends its agreement part way through a paid term is not
     refunded for that term. It is annual prepaid, and the venue keeps the
     service to the end of what it paid for.
   - **If Signal Studio withdraws Venue Edition or stops operating during a paid
     term, the unused part of that term is refunded pro rata.** This is the one
     clause that makes taking €25,000 up front defensible, it costs nothing
     while nothing has been sold, and it is close to what `/terms` already
     promises consumers. It is also the honest counterweight to R-013.

### The wording, if this is approved

> **What you pay and when.** €1,000 a year for your venue, paid in advance,
> inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate. We invoice you when your signed order
> form reaches us, and it is payable within 14 days.
>
> **Your year.** Your twelve months start the day your payment clears. That is
> also the day your founding number is assigned. We will tell you your renewal
> date in writing on the same day, so you have it a year in advance.
>
> **Renewal.** We invoice you with your first renewal notice, and the renewal
> invoice is payable within 30 days.
>
> **If you decide not to continue.** Do nothing. Your agreement ends at the end
> of the year you paid for, and you keep the service until then. If you end it
> earlier, the year you have already paid for is not refunded.
>
> **If we stop.** If Signal Studio withdraws Venue Edition or stops operating
> while you are inside a year you have paid for, we refund the part you have not
> used. Every couple who has already opened a workspace keeps it and keeps their
> Keepsake.
