## 1. Two boundaries the venue will meet on this call

Both are ratified. Both are easier to state than to discover.

**Branding is the venue's name only.** D-027 point 3: no logo, no venue-written
welcome message. This matches what ships today ("Compliments of <venue>") and it
is now a stated position rather than an accident of `brandMeta` having no schema.

**There is no venue login.** Verified 2026-08-04:
`src/lib/account/live/project-venue-access.ts:330` returns `members: []`
unconditionally. D-027 point 4 makes the portal at launch invitation
administration only. Signal HQ administers; the venue receives evidence.

**The hard operational rule.** The HQ password is never given to a venue. It is
one shared password in front of Today, Money, Company, Vault, Atlas, Decks,
Founders Circle, the Data Room, Access and Account Review. The gate was hardened
on 2026-08-03 to a v2 HMAC token with a PBKDF2 key and server-side expiry, and
its own source states what it still is not: no per-person identity, no venue
account, no revocation of one holder without rotating for everyone.

## 3. The script

### Step 1 — Confirm the ceiling is computed, not defaulted

- **Check:** `src/app/hq/entitlements/OnboardVenueForm.tsx`. R-016 records the
  defect: the allotment field was required, `min={1}`, `defaultValue={10}`.
  Nobody decided ten.
- **Expect:** the ceiling comes from the venue's annual wedding count
  (D-026 R2 approved a fair-use ceiling at twice the annual wedding count, floor
  40), and unlimited is representable.
- **Fail:** if the form still defaults to a number, stop and fix before
  configuring a real venue. A venue sold "no seats" must never be shown a seat
  count.
- **Result 2026-08-04:** not verified by me in the current branch. R-016 is
  recorded as open with target "with the E02.12 decision", and D-020 states
  plainly that unlimited was not representable at the time it was ratified.
  **Check this before the first configuration.**

### Step 2 — Confirm no seat count reaches the venue

- **Check:** `project-venue-access.ts` no longer raises a "No remaining allotment
  headroom" attention item for an unlimited sponsor, and `availableCount` is
  suppressed for them.
- **Result 2026-08-04:** the integrity suite includes *"warns, without failing
  the run, when an unlimited venue still carries a seat count"*, which is the
  right shape: it detects the contradiction without blocking. Confirm the venue
  surface itself before showing it.

### Step 3 — Render the venue's name in a real couple workspace

**Not run. Needs a configured venue and a couple workspace.**

- **Do:** provision a rehearsal couple workspace under the venue and look at it.
- **Expect:** the venue's name, once, in one quiet line.
- **Fail:** a logo, a badge, a "powered by" in the viewport, or a venue-written
  message. D-011 point 2: if it reads as marketing on someone's wedding page, the
  thing being sold is gone.

### Step 4 — Check the unfurl

- **Run:** `curl -s <workspace public url> | grep -i "og:title\|og:description"`
- **Expect:** the venue name only (R-024).

### Step 5 — Show the venue and get a written answer

**Only the founder can do this.**

- **Do:** show the real render, not a mockup. Ask for approval in writing, with a
  date.
- **Fail:** approval given verbally and recorded as approved. This programme
  already has a decision (D-026) about approval records that show that approval
  happened and not what was judged. Do not add to that.

### Step 6 — Explain the invitation model in true words

**Only the founder can do this.**

- **Say:** you tell us the couple, we issue the invitation, the couple redeems
  it. You do not log in. You receive the evidence.
- **Fail:** any surface that offers the venue an "Invite member" control that
  sets a notice string and does nothing. Remove it or wire it before a venue sees
  it.

## 5. What I would tell the founder

The two launch narrowings in D-027 (name only, no venue login) buy back the two
largest pieces of unbuilt work before September, and both are things a venue will
ask about in the first configuration call. Say them plainly and early. A venue
told "your name appears, quietly, on your couples' pages, and we run the
invitations for you" hears restraint. The same venue discovering it after
expecting a logo hears a shortfall.

The one thing to check before the first configuration is the allotment default.
Ten was never anyone's decision and it is still recorded as open.
