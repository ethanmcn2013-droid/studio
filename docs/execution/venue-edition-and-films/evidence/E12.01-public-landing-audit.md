## 1. Why no source file was touched

Three separate mechanisms make an unrequested edit to `src/app/venues/page.tsx`
expensive, and they are independent of each other.

1. **E12.04 is Done and founder-approved over this exact file**, and the page is
   live. R-025 records why: price is a change-controlled item on a public
   surface, and publishing it is not Claude's.
2. **I-014.** `studio.page.venues` is a registered experience whose `source` is
   this file. Any edit moves its `materialityHash`, and once the hash moves the
   validator requires `complete` fixture, screenshot and accessibility coverage,
   which I-014 established is **structurally unreachable** through the current
   capture plan. An edit here turns a passing entry into a failing one.
3. **The WP-06 copy sweep.** `copy/prohibited-claims.v1.json` pins this file at
   `expected: 8`. The sweep stage that reads that pin is not on this branch yet:
   it exists as uncommitted work in another lane's tree. Any edit that changes the
   hit count collides with that merge.

So the honest deliverable is the audit and the words, and the sequencing
recommendation that makes the four corrections cost one hash movement instead of
four.

## 2. The organising idea, assessed

E12.01's brief is that the page's organising idea should be the sponsored couple
rather than the venue's feature list. **The page already does this in its first
screen**, which is worth saying plainly because it changes what the task is.

| Element | Whose experience |
|---|---|
| H1: "Stand behind every couple who plans with you." | The couple |
| Subhead: "Signal Studio gives every couple a clear place to plan their wedding…" | The couple |
| The hero mock, lines 177 to 266 | A couple's workspace, with the venue's name as one quiet line above it |
| "What your couples get · What your patronage gives them." | The couple |
| "The couple never sees a price. The venue pays so they never have to think about it." | The couple |

**Criterion 1 passes.** The reader meets the couple's experience first, and the
venue's name appears in the mock as one line above a couple's plan, which is
exactly the relationship D-027 point 3 describes.

**One structural observation, offered as a judgement rather than a defect.** The
activation register section sits between "What your couples get" and the price.
It is the most venue-administrative block on the page and it interrupts the run
from what the couple receives to what it costs. Moving it below the price would
run E09.10 §3.1's ladder without a detour. That is a design change on an approved
page and it is not made here.

### Criterion 2 — pass, with a note

The hero paragraph is written for this page rather than taken from E09.11 §3's
one-paragraph form. It says the same things and breaks no rule. E09.11's
instruction is "Three lengths. A surface picks one. It does not write a fourth."
Recorded as a drift risk rather than a defect: the page is right today, and the
next person to edit it has no single-source module to edit instead.
`src/lib/venue-invitation/copy.ts`, written for E12.02 and E12.03, is that module
if the founder wants the page to adopt it.

### Criterion 3 — fail. The OpenGraph description carries a bare term

Line 15:

> "A venue stands behind its couples' planning, **eighteen months of Signal Studio
> each**, under the venue name, paid once a year. Patronage, not software."

The body of the page is correct: line 87 and line 123 both carry "or three months
past the wedding, whichever is later". The metadata does not, and the metadata is
what appears in a link preview and a search result. P7 exists because a couple
with a two-year engagement who hears the short version believes they lose it
before the day, which is the one failure D-022 was ratified to prevent.

**Approved replacement**, from E09.11 §3's one-line form, which carries no term
at all and therefore cannot carry a bare one:

> A private place to plan, for every couple who books with you.

### Criterion 4 — fail. A published price with no VAT statement

Lines 10 and 11:

> "The Venue Edition is **€1,500** a year, prepaid, the venue's name in a quiet
> line, nothing for the team to run. The first twenty-five venues pay **€1,000**,
> held for as long as they stay."

D-021: "Every agreement, order form, invoice and page states the price as
inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate." The body of the page does this
correctly and twice over. The meta description states both numbers and does not.

**Approved replacement**, using E09.11 §4.1's permitted form:

> The Venue Edition is €1,500 a year, prepaid, including VAT at the prevailing
> rate. The first twenty-five venues pay €1,000, which is €500 a year less, for
> as long as you stay.

*A related defect is outside this file and is E12.14's: the JSON-LD
`SoftwareApplication` offer in `src/app/layout.tsx` publishes `price: "1500"` on
every studio route with no VAT statement. The concurrent session working this
worktree has already replaced the old `priceSpecification` ban with a required
VAT statement, so this one is being closed.*

### Criterion 6 — fail, and D-032 R4 makes it required rather than desirable

The page tells a venue what it sees, at line 118 and in the activation register
section. It does not carry the launch-scope sentence.

`E09.11-copy-system.md` §5.1a names this file by line and calls it **"worse than
silent"**. The rule above the list is the part that will not go stale: *any
surface that tells a venue what it sees must carry it.*

**Required, verbatim, no variant:**

> At launch you see your invitations and who opened them. Adoption figures follow
> later in the year.

### Criterion 7 — fail. A fourth visible thing, and an unwired mechanism

Line 118, inside the "The activation register, first" perk:

> "Private couple work never appears **unless the couple separately consents to a
> specific field**."

Two problems in one sentence. D-001 point 4 fixes the venue-visible list at
exactly three things, and this names a fourth. D-027 point 4 leaves the consent
layer unwired at launch, and E09.11 §5.1a says outright that no surface may
describe a couple consenting to share a specific field with a venue.

Line 447 repeats it: "A separate, field-level consent receipt is required before
any optional metadata can appear."

**Approved replacement**, E09.11 §5.1's second part:

> What any couple is planning never appears. There is no per-couple row, no
> ranking and no list of who is behind.

### Criterion 8 — fail, and this one is new

D-020 point 2 requires the survival sentence to be **stated unprompted** wherever
the end of the agreement is described, and E09.11 §3.1 says why: "A venue that
has to ask what happens to its couples if it stops paying has already decided we
would not have said. The sentence costs one line and it is the strongest thing in
the offer."

The page describes the end of the agreement twice, at line 102 ("for as long as
you stay") and line 476 ("The rate holds while the agreement keeps renewing").
The survival sentence appears nowhere. Neither does Keepsake: `grep` for
`Keepsake` over the file returns zero.

**Approved additions**, E09.11 §3.1 and §7.2, both verbatim:

> Any workspace a couple has already opened keeps its full term, and its
> Keepsake, whatever happens to your agreement. Only new invitations stop.

> When a couple's planning term ends, their workspace stays open in Keepsake
> mode. Read-only, for as long as the service exists, with a one-click export
> they own.

### The three that pass, verified rather than assumed

**Criterion 5.** Zero hits across the file for `for life`, `lifetime`, `forever`,
`in perpetuity`. Line 476 states the lock correctly: "The rate holds while the
agreement keeps renewing, and there is no clause that expires it into a higher
number."

**Criterion 9.** The OpenGraph description reads "under the venue name". Line 179
carries the rule as a source comment: "the venue name, one quiet line. Never a
logo (D-027 point 3)." No logo, colour or venue-written content is implied
anywhere. *One residue: line 176's source comment still reads "Co-branded
workspace mock", which is E09.11 §13 defect 5. It is a comment and reaches no
screen, but it is a pattern in `prohibited-claims.v1.json` and it will be a sweep
hit when WP-06's stage lands.*

**Criterion 10.** Zero hits for `partner`, `member`, `investor`, `exclusive`,
`guaranteed`, `certified`, `act now`, `limited time`, `only N places left`,
`solicitor`, `accountant`, `GDPR compliant`. "Twenty-five places" is a static
true statement with no counter that can go stale. E12.04's audit reached the same
result independently on 2026-08-03 and this audit confirms it.

## 5. The recommendation

**One decision, and everything else follows from it.**

> **Is E12.01 the same surface as E12.04?**

**Recommended: yes.** A second public Venue Edition landing page would put a
second price on the public internet, maintained by a different person on a
different day. That is the I-002 failure mode with a new URL, and I-002 is the
issue that took roughly 140 files to close once.

**If yes**, the work is one change to `src/app/venues/page.tsx` carrying four
corrections and two additions, all with approved text, all in §3 above. It should
be sequenced with WP-06's sweep landing so the pinned hit count and the
`materialityHash` each move once rather than twice, and it needs the same founder
review E12.04 got, because it touches a live commercial surface.

**If no**, the second page needs a stated job that the live page does not do, and
this audit did not find one.

**A third option worth naming.** Adopt `src/lib/venue-invitation/copy.ts` on
`/venues` at the same time. It already carries every block this audit quotes,
verbatim, in one place, and it is swept by
`check-venue-edition-contract.mjs`. That converts "four sentences are wrong on
this page" into "the page cannot state a claim that has not been approved", which
is the only version of this that does not recur.
