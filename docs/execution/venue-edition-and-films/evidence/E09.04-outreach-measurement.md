# E09.04 — Outreach measurement specification

**Stream version: `venue-outreach.v1`**
Prepared 2026-08-03 · Evidence for E09.04
Governed by D-013 point 3, D-016, D-020 · bound to `E09.01-event-taxonomy.md`,
`E09.02-metric-definitions.md`, `E11.01-02-crm-stages.md`,
`E11.05-07-outreach-email.md` · copy bound by `E09.10-copy-hierarchy.md`

> **What this document is.** The specification for measuring the six outreach
> subjects named in E09.04: personalised-film views, landing-page visits, booking
> actions, meetings, proposals and paid conversion. Every event, its properties,
> its trigger, where it must be emitted, its retention, and the privacy position.
>
> **What this document is not.** It is not the routes. Outreach measurement lands
> on studio's public commercial pages and on the private venue page, which belong
> to E12 and E11.07 respectively. This is the contract they build against. **No
> code was written for E09.04 in this package**, deliberately, because writing an
> emitter into a surface another package owns is how two packages ship two
> incompatible halves of one thing.
>
> **What this document is not, second.** It is not legal advice, and nothing in it
> has been reviewed by a solicitor (D-016). §7 describes an operating position,
> not a legal determination.

---

## 0. The determinations, made once

Five things that later sessions should not have to re-argue.

1. **Outreach measurement is a fourth stream and it never joins the other
   three.** E09.01 §2 defines three tiers and states they must never be merged.
   Outreach is none of them: it observes **a business that has been contacted**,
   not a couple who is planning a wedding. It uses no salt, no `subjectIdHash`,
   no `workspaceIdHash`, and it never touches the `venue-meaningful-action.v1`
   store. A join between the two would put a named business next to pseudonymous
   couple behaviour, which is the exact adjacency the whole privacy model exists
   to prevent.
2. **Nothing in this stream is ever venue-visible.** The venue sees three things
   and no fourth (D-001.4): invitation administration, activation evidence,
   aggregate adoption evidence. "How many times you opened your film" is a
   fourth thing. This stream is founder-only, permanently, and it is not a
   founder choice.
3. **Paid conversion is not an event.** It is a state, already durable in the
   sponsor ledger, and `isPaidVenue({ venuePlan, paidAt })` is the whole test
   (`src/lib/db/schema.ts`). E09.01 §2 made the same call for commercial
   lifecycle facts and the reasoning is identical: computing money from an event
   stream introduces drift against the contract record for no gain. **Do not
   build a `venue_paid` event.** §3.6.
4. **A click is not a stage.** E11.01 §0 point 4, restated here because this is
   the document that could most easily break it. Film link clicks inform who to
   follow up first. They never move a venue forward, they never appear in a
   funnel, and no counter in `PROJECT_STATE.json` is incremented by anything in
   this stream.
5. **The subject is the account, never the person.** `account_id` is the join
   key everywhere. A named individual at a venue exists in the CRM as a business
   contact and is never a property on an event.

## 1. Verified state of the system today

Checked against shipped code on 2026-08-03.

**None of the six subjects is instrumented.** There is no personalised film
route: `studio/src/app` contains `/venues`, `/venues/demo` and the HQ-gated
`/hq/demo-film`, and no `/v/*`. A film view has no surface to fire from.

**GA4 is the only landing-page measurement, and it has no consent gate.**
`src/components/analytics/google-tag.tsx` renders gtag.js from the root layout
(`src/app/layout.tsx:144`) on every page in production, with a comment that says
so in as many words: *"this is the raw gtag with no consent gate"*. GA4 pageviews
cannot be joined to a prospect, carry no `account_id`, and are not evidence of
anything in this specification.

**The tracking params are real and they already have a durable reader.** This
corrects the reconnaissance note that said no endpoint captures them and no row
stores them. `src/lib/tracking.ts` decorates outbound hrefs with `source`,
`campaign`, `audience`, `artifact`, `touch` and `venue`.
`src/app/contact/page.tsx` formats them into a mailto body, which is display
only. But **`src/app/waitlist/actions.ts` captures all five into
`waitlist_entries`** (`src/lib/db/schema.ts:328`), which stores `source`,
`campaign`, `audience`, `artifact`, `touch`, `path`, **`referrer` and
`user_agent`**. The last two are taken from request headers, are not on any
form, and are named in no notice. That is a live data-minimisation finding, not
a gap: §5.3.

**No CRM vocabulary exists for four of the six subjects.** `PROSPECT_STAGES`
(`src/lib/db/schema.ts:187-195`) ends at `pilot_active`. There is no meeting
stage that carries the E11.01 §4 qualification, no proposal stage, no signed,
paid, configured or onboarded stage. E11.01 §3 records this in full.

**The CRM has no link to a sponsor.** `prospectsTable` carries no `account_id`,
no sponsor id and no slug, so a paid venue in the ledger cannot be attributed
back to the prospect that converted. Every figure in §4 is therefore a count
today, not a rate, and stays that way until the column exists.

## 2. The shape

One stream, one table, seven fields. Modelled on `venue-meaningful-action.v1`
because the shape has already survived review, and deliberately **not** sharing
its store.

```ts
type VenueOutreachV1 = {
  eventId: string;        // idempotency key, see below
  streamVersion: "venue-outreach.v1";
  accountId: string;      // the venue account. Never a name, never a person.
  kind: OutreachKind;     // allowlisted, §3
  occurredAt: number;     // ms since epoch, UTC
  cohort: number;         // 1..4, the release cohort (D-017)
  touch: number;          // 1..4, the touch this event belongs to (D-013.4)
  properties: Record<string, string | number | boolean>; // per-kind, §3
};
```

**Rejected, not trimmed.** An event carrying a key outside its kind's declared
property set is rejected, exactly as `venue-meaningful-action.v1` is. A silent
trim is how a venue contact's email arrives in an analytics table one release
after somebody adds it "just for debugging".

**Idempotency.** `eventId = sha256(kind, accountId, dedupeKey, day)` truncated to
32 hex, where `dedupeKey` is per-kind (§3) and `day` is the Europe/Dublin
calendar date. A page refreshed eleven times in one afternoon is one
`venue_page_viewed` row for that day, and a **repeat-view counter** on the
account carries the strength of interest instead. E11.05-07 §7.4 calls repeat
views "the strongest signal available in this system"; day-bucketing keeps that
signal without turning a refresh into a data point.

**Store.** A new `venue_outreach_events` table in the studio database
(`STUDIO_DATABASE_URL`), alongside `prospects` and `sponsors`. Not the
entitlements database, which is couple-side. Not the Tier 1 store, which does
not exist yet and must not be given a second tenant when it does.

## 3. The six subjects

Each carries: the kind, when it fires, its dedupe key, its properties, where it
must be emitted, and what it is not.

### 3.1 Personalised film view

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | `venue_page_viewed` |
| **Fires** | Server-side, on a successful render of the private venue page, after the token resolves and before the response returns. |
| **Dedupe key** | `accountId` + venue-local date. One row per venue per day. |
| **Properties** | `state`: `"invitation" \| "proposal"` (the two states of one URL, E11.05-07 §7.2) · `firstView`: boolean · `repeatViewCount`: integer, the running count on the account |
| **Emitted from** | `studio/src/app/v/[token]/page.tsx`, owned by **E11.07 / E12.03**. Server component only. |
| **Retention** | §6 |

**What it is not.** Not an email open: there are no open pixels and there will
not be (D-013.3). Not a click on the link, which is unobservable without a
redirector, and a redirector is refused in E11.05-07 §7.1. The page render is the
click, and that is the whole reason the link points at a page rather than a file.
Not a play, not a watch-through: see the open question in §8.1.

**Never recorded on this surface.** No third-party script. No pixel. No session
recording. No heatmap. No scroll depth. No IP-derived location. No user agent. No
referrer. No cookie that requires a banner. E11.05-07 §7.2 already fixed this and
this specification does not reopen it.

### 3.2 Landing-page visit

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | `venue_landing_viewed` |
| **Fires** | Server-side, on a render of a public Venue Edition commercial page. |
| **Dedupe key** | `accountId` + `path` + venue-local date. |
| **Properties** | `path`: the route, from a fixed allowlist, never an arbitrary URL · `campaign`, `artifact`, `touch`: from `TrackingParams` when present |
| **Emitted from** | `studio/src/app/venues/page.tsx` and its siblings, owned by **E12.04 / WP-13**. |
| **Retention** | §6 |

**The attribution problem, stated rather than solved.** A public page has no
token, so there is no `accountId` unless the visitor arrived on a decorated link
carrying `venue=`. Two consequences, both of which the surface must state rather
than paper over:

- **An anonymous landing visit produces no event.** It is not attributable and it
  is not counted. It is not zero, it is absent, and any report of landing visits
  carries a coverage line saying how many were attributable. This is the same
  rule as E09.01 §3.7: unattributable is a coverage fact, never a zero.
- **`venue=` in a URL is a weak identifier that survives forwarding.** A venue
  owner forwarding the page to a partner produces a second attributed visit from
  a different person. That is acceptable, because the subject is the account. It
  is not acceptable to strengthen it with anything that identifies the reader.

**Blocked.** This kind must not ship until §5.1 is resolved. Adding a
first-party server-side event to a page that already loads an unconsented GA4 tag
makes the consent problem worse, not better, and does it on the surface a
prospect sees first.

### 3.3 Booking action

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | `venue_booking_action` |
| **Fires** | On a committed action by the venue that offers a conversation: a scheduling link opened or a slot taken, a call requested, a form submitted. After commit, never before. |
| **Dedupe key** | `accountId` + `action` + venue-local date. |
| **Properties** | `action`: `"time_taken" \| "call_requested" \| "form_submitted" \| "reply_logged"` · `surface`: `"venue_page" \| "landing" \| "email_reply"` |
| **Emitted from** | The CTA handlers on `/v/[token]` (**E11.07**) and the public pages (**E12.04**). `reply_logged` is operator-entered in the CRM. |
| **Retention** | §6 |

**What it is not.** Not a meeting. A booked slot is an intention; E11.01 §4 is
explicit that a rescheduled meeting stays at `responded` until it happens, and a
no-show is not a meeting. Not a stage change on its own. Not a reply: a reply is
stage 3 and is operator-logged with date, route and substance.

### 3.4 Meeting

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | `venue_meeting_held` |
| **Fires** | When the CRM records the stage-4 exit, which requires the meeting to have happened **and** four facts to be on the record (E11.01 §4). |
| **Dedupe key** | `accountId` + the CRM stage-transition id. One row per transition, not per day. |
| **Properties** | `qualified`: boolean, true only when all five exit tests are satisfied · `attended`: boolean · `discoveryRecorded`: boolean (E11.09) |
| **Emitted from** | The CRM stage-transition writer, owned by **E11.01**. Not from a calendar integration: no calendar is connected and connecting one would import attendee names. |
| **Retention** | §6 |

**What it is not.** Not a calendar entry. Not a scheduled meeting. Not a
conversation that ended without knowing who signs, which E11.01 §4 explicitly
excludes from `qualifiedMeetings`.

**This kind cannot be emitted today.** There is no meeting stage in
`PROSPECT_STAGES` and no exit-criteria record. §5.2.

### 3.5 Proposal

| | |
|---|---|
| **Kind** | `venue_proposal_issued` |
| **Fires** | When a written proposal is issued and `proposal_expires_on` is recorded on the account (E11.01 §4, stage 6). |
| **Dedupe key** | `accountId` + `proposalId`. A revised proposal is a new row, and the supersession is a property, not an edit. |
| **Properties** | `holdExpiresOn`: date · `revision`: integer · `holdExtended`: boolean, true only where Ethan extended it personally (D-009.7) |
| **Emitted from** | The CRM stage-transition writer (**E11.01**), and the proposal page (**E12.03**) where it is generated there. |
| **Retention** | §6 |

**What it is not.** Not a price quoted on a call. Not a proposal without an
expiry date, which does not exit the stage at all. The proposal **content** is
never a property: no price, no terms, no signature block. The proposal is a
document, the event is that it went out.

### 3.6 Paid conversion

**No event.** Read the sponsor ledger.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Test** | `isPaidVenue({ venuePlan, paidAt })` returns true. `venuePlan` in `founding \| paid`, `paidAt` not null, meaning cleared funds and not an invoice. |
| **Read from** | `sponsors` (`src/lib/db/schema.ts:85`), already read by `getTraction()`. |
| **Authority** | The ledger, always. E11.01 §5: where the tracker and the ledger disagree, the ledger wins and the tracker is corrected. |

**Why this is a determination and not a preference.** Money is already durable,
already reconciled, and already the trigger for the one irreversible act in the
whole sequence: the founding number is assigned on payment (D-009.6). An event
stream would give a second, lossier copy of a fact that is definitionally exact,
and the first time the two disagreed the argument would be about which one to
believe. It is also the only one of the six that is machine-checkable end to end
today.

**What must be built instead:** the join. §5.2.

## 4. What the six subjects are allowed to be turned into

Counts, per cohort, with the denominator named. Nothing else, until §5.2 lands.

| Figure | Numerator | Denominator | Available today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venue page reach | accounts with ≥1 `venue_page_viewed` | accounts invited in the cohort | No, no route |
| Repeat interest | accounts with ≥2 distinct view days | accounts with ≥1 view day | No |
| Booking actions | accounts with ≥1 `venue_booking_action` | accounts invited | No |
| Qualified meeting rate | accounts with `qualified: true` | accounts invited | No, no stage |
| Proposal rate | accounts with ≥1 proposal | qualified meetings | No, no stage |
| Paid conversion | `isPaidVenue` accounts | accounts invited | **Partly.** Numerator yes, denominator not attributable |

**Three rules on every one of these.**

1. **The denominator is named on the surface**, every time. E09.02 §1 fixed this
   for the couple-side metrics and the reasoning transfers exactly: a rate whose
   denominator is unstated is a rate somebody will quote back wrongly.
2. **A cohort of 25 is a small number.** Percentages over 25 accounts move four
   points per venue. Report `n of 25`, never a bare percentage, and never a
   comparison between cohorts of different sizes.
3. **No venue is named in any report** (`E11.01` §8.3, `01-GEOGRAPHY.md`).
   Counts and clusters only. `consent_public_naming` is `unknown` for all 219
   researched accounts.

## 5. What must change first

Three things, in this order. None of them is optional and none of them is mine
to change in this package.

### 5.1 The consent problem, and it is the blocker

**Verified.** `GoogleTag` renders gtag.js from the root layout on every
production page, with `gtag('config', ...)` fired immediately and no Consent Mode
default. There is no cookie banner. There is no consent state. The component's
own comment records the gap.

Three consequences that bear directly on E09.04:

- **The private venue page inherits it.** `/v/[token]` will live under the same
  root layout, so gtag.js will load on it the day it ships, unless something
  stops it. That directly contradicts E11.05-07 §7.2, which specifies *"no
  third-party script, no pixel"* and *"no cookies that require a banner"* on that
  exact page. **This is the single most important thing in this document.** The
  page whose entire subject is that the venue's couples are not being watched
  cannot ship with an unconsented third-party tag on it.
- **`/v/` is not disallowed in robots.** `src/app/robots.ts` disallows `/api/`,
  `/hq/`, `/redeem/` and `/review`. E11.05-07 §7.2 requires `/v/` disallowed and
  excluded from the sitemap. It is not there yet.
- **A new first-party event on a public page does not fix an existing
  third-party one.** Adding `venue_landing_viewed` while gtag runs unconsented
  leaves the page in a worse position, not a better one: two collectors, one of
  them still ungated.

**The order this must happen in.**

1. Exclude the private venue page from `GoogleTag` before `/v/*` ships. Whichever
   mechanism is chosen, the predicate must be **imported from one place**, not
   duplicated by hand. The app repo already carries that exact defect
   (`app/src/lib/bare-artifact-path.ts:2` and
   `app/src/instrumentation-client.ts:10` maintain the same predicate twice), and
   it is recorded here so studio does not copy it.
2. Add `/v/` to the robots disallow list and exclude it from the sitemap.
3. Decide the public-surface consent position (§8.2). Until it is decided, **no
   new tag, first-party or third-party, lands on a public Venue Edition page**,
   and `venue_landing_viewed` stays unbuilt.

**Related and out of scope here, recorded so it is not lost.**
`app/src/app/privacy/page.tsx:155-158` asserts there is never a third-party
tracker and names PostHog, while gtag.js loads on the same page. It is the
disclosure of record for E03.06 and E12.14. It belongs to the app tree and to
those tasks.

### 5.2 The CRM cannot hold four of the six subjects

E11.01 §10 already lists the ten items. Three of them gate this document:

- **Item 1, the stage vocabulary.** Without a meeting, proposal, signed, paid,
  configured and onboarded stage, `venue_meeting_held` and
  `venue_proposal_issued` have nothing to fire from.
- **Item 6, the exit-criteria record.** `qualified: true` is only meaningful if
  the five exit tests are recorded. A boolean an operator ticks is a claim, not
  evidence.
- **The missing sponsor link, which E11.01 §10 does not list and this document
  adds.** `prospectsTable` needs a nullable `account_id` (and, once configured, a
  sponsor slug) so the ledger's paid fact can be attributed back to the prospect
  that converted. Without it there is no conversion rate in this programme, only
  two counts that cannot be divided. **Recommended as an eleventh item on the
  E11.01 build list.**

### 5.3 The waitlist already stores more than it says

`joinWaitlistAction` writes `referrer` (from the `referer` header) and
`user_agent` into `waitlist_entries` on every submission. Neither is on the form,
neither is named in a notice, and neither has a stated purpose or retention.
`userAgent` and `ip` are both on E09.01 §6.1's forbidden list; that list governs
venue-facing payloads rather than internal storage, so this is not a breach of
it, but it is the same instinct pointing at the same data.

**Recommendation.** Stop writing `user_agent`. Keep `referrer` only if it has a
stated purpose, and name both in the privacy notice if they are kept. Owned by
E03.04 and E12.14; recorded here because E09.04 is the task that went looking.

## 6. Retention

Outreach records are commercial records about a business relationship. They are
not behavioural observation of a person and they are not couple data, so they do
not inherit the 35-day Tier 1 sweep. They still do not get to live forever.

| Data | Retention | End action |
|---|---|---|
| `venue_outreach_events`, converted account | Life of the agreement plus 24 months | Delete rows, keep the aggregate counts |
| `venue_outreach_events`, non-converted account | 12 months from the last touch, or immediately on an explicit objection | Hard delete |
| `venue_outreach_events`, suppressed account | **Delete on suppression**, except the suppression record itself | The suppression list is permanent; the observation of them is not |
| Repeat-view counter on the account | Same as the events it summarises | Delete with them |
| Aggregate cohort counts | 24 months | Fold into programme totals |
| CRM stage-transition record | Life of the relationship | Per the CRM's own policy |

**One rule under the table.** A venue that says stop has its address suppressed
permanently and its outreach observation deleted. Keeping a record of how many
times somebody who told you to go away looked at your page is the wrong side of
the line, and no reporting need justifies it.

## 7. Privacy position

Stated as an operating position. Not a legal determination, and no solicitor has
reviewed it (D-016, P2).

**What this stream holds.** An account identifier for a business, a cohort
number, a touch number, a timestamp, and a small fixed set of per-kind
properties. Business contact records live in the CRM and join on `account_id`.

**What it never holds.** A person's name. An email address. A phone number. An IP
address. A user agent. A referrer on the private page. A device or browser
fingerprint. A location derived from any of those. Any couple, guest, wedding or
planning data of any kind (E11.01 §8). Any special-category data, ever (R-017).

**What the venue is told.** The private venue page carries one quiet line saying
that Signal Studio records that the page was opened, and a route to object. That
is the whole disclosure and it is enough, because the whole record is "this
account opened this page on this date". A venue that objects is honoured and the
rows are deleted.

**What the venue is never shown.** Anything in this stream. §0 point 2.

**Deletion.** On request, and on suppression without a request.

**What is not claimed.** No GDPR compliance claim, no certification, no audit, no
legal review (P2, P3). No claim about where the data is held or transferred until
E03.05 has verified that specific claim (P15).

## 8. Open founder questions

### 8.1 Film play events, or page view only

E11.05-07 §7.4 raises this as **FD-B** and takes the conservative reading. It is
restated here because it is a measurement question and this is the measurement
document, and because the two documents must not answer it differently.

D-013 point 3 permits "film link clicks only". "Play started" and "watched to the
end" are film events rather than link clicks. They would be genuinely useful:
page view alone cannot distinguish a venue that opened the page and left from one
that watched the whole film, and that is the difference between a follow-up worth
making and one that is not.

Against: it is a player watching a reader, on a page whose subject is that
nobody is being watched. And a watch-through figure invites a second question,
"where did they stop", which is scroll depth wearing a different hat.

*Recommendation: page view only for Cohort 1.* Revisit with real data if the page
view turns out not to discriminate. **Answering differently here than in
E11.05-07 FD-B would leave two live answers, so answer once.**

### 8.2 The public-surface consent position

Three options, and this one has to be decided before anything in §3.2 is built.

- **A. Consent Mode v2, default denied, with a banner.** Standard, defensible,
  and it puts cookie theatre on a page whose pitch is restraint. E09.10 §3.4
  bans cookie theatre on the public Timeline; it says nothing about the venue
  landing page, so this is not currently a rule violation.
- **B. Remove GA4 from the Venue Edition surfaces entirely** and measure them
  server-side, first-party, with no cookie and no third party. Smallest surface,
  no banner, and it is the same posture the private page already takes.
- **C. Leave it.** Not recommended and recorded so the option is explicit rather
  than the default by inaction. It is currently the live state.

*Recommendation: B for `/venues`, `/v/*` and every Venue Edition commercial page,
and decide A separately for the rest of the site.* B is consistent with the
product being sold, needs no banner, and is the only one of the three that is
strictly smaller than what runs today.

### 8.3 Whether an anonymous landing visit is counted at all

Proposed: no, and the coverage line says how many were unattributable (§3.2).
Alternative: count anonymous visits as a separate, clearly-labelled total, which
is more informative and invites exactly the wrong comparison against the
attributed number. *Recommend not counting.*

## 9. Acceptance criteria

For E09.04. Written so an auditor who was not in the room can answer each yes or
no.

1. Every one of the six subjects has a named kind or a named state read, a
   trigger, a dedupe key, a declared property set, a named owning surface and a
   retention row.
2. No event in `venue-outreach.v1` carries a person's name, email, phone, IP,
   user agent or referrer, at any nesting depth, proven by a schema-level test
   that rejects rather than trims.
3. No outreach event, count or derived figure appears on any venue-facing
   surface: screen, CSV, PDF, API, email or support view-as.
4. The outreach store shares no table, no salt and no join key with the
   `venue-meaningful-action.v1` stream, proven by a test that asserts the two
   schemas have no common identifier column.
5. No open pixel, read receipt, session recording, heatmap, scroll-depth or
   IP-location mechanism exists in any Venue Edition surface, proven by a string
   sweep over `studio/src`.
6. The private venue page loads no third-party script and sets no cookie that
   requires consent, proven by a request-log assertion on a real page load.
7. `/v/` is disallowed in `robots.ts` and absent from the sitemap.
8. Paid conversion is read from `isPaidVenue` and no `venue_paid` event exists
   anywhere in the codebase.
9. Every reported rate names its denominator on the surface, and an
   unattributable visit renders as a coverage figure rather than as zero.
10. A suppression deletes that account's outreach rows, proven by a test.

## 10. Honest state, recorded once

- **Nothing in this document is instrumented.** No route, no table, no emitter,
  no test. E09.04 is a specification and the six subjects are unmeasured today.
- **Four of the six cannot be built until the CRM grows** (§5.2), and one of
  those, the sponsor link, is not yet on any build list.
- **One of the six should never be built as an event at all** (§3.6).
- **The one measurement that runs on a public Venue Edition surface today, GA4,
  is not evidence for anything here** and is itself the blocker in §5.1.
- **No venue has been contacted.** Every commercial counter in
  `PROJECT_STATE.json` is 0 except `researchedAccountUniverse`, which is 219. The
  first cohort will be the first test of whether any of this measures the right
  thing.
