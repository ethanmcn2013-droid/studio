# E15.14 — permissioned evidence, referrals and public names: the register and the script

**Task:** E15.14 · **Written:** 2026-08-04 by Claude Code (WP-22)
**Status:** a register design and a runnable script. No permission was sought or
recorded, because no venue has signed and no couple exists.

---

## 1. The position this task protects

**Venues are asked. Couples are asked for nothing by default.** D-010 point 1.
Any case-study use of couple material is a separate, specific, opt-in permission.

That asymmetry is deliberate and it is the trust story in one sentence. It is
also the thing most likely to be eroded by a good conversation: a couple says
something lovely on a call and it ends up on a page.

## 2. What only a human can do

Ask. Record the answer. Honour a revocation.

## 3. The permission register

One row per permission. Not one row per venue, and not one row per artefact.

| Column | Rule |
|---|---|
| Giver | the venue account id, or the couple's workspace id. Never a name typed by hand. |
| Exact use | the specific surface and the specific material. **"Marketing" is not a use.** |
| Date given | |
| Given how | email, signed schedule, in-product opt-in. A verbal yes is recorded as verbal, with who heard it. |
| Expiry or review | a date, or "until revoked" |
| Revocation route | something the giver can actually use without asking permission to revoke |
| Where it appears | every artefact carrying the material, so a revocation can be executed |
| Revoked on | filled when it happens |

**The last column is the one that makes the register real.** A permission record
with no list of where the material appears cannot be honoured when somebody
withdraws it.

## 4. The script

### Step 1 — Venue name and map

- **Do:** ask for the venue's name on the public map and in the founding
  programme, as a specific use, in writing.
- **Note:** `venue-universe/counts-only.md` is generated to never name a venue,
  and it carries its own reminder: *"Venue names are published as participants
  only with recorded consent (E10.14, E15.16)."* That discipline already exists
  in the tooling. The register is what extends it to the published surfaces.
- **Fail:** publishing a name because the venue "obviously would not mind".

### Step 2 — Venue quote

- **Do:** send back the exact words you want to use, in the exact form, and ask.
- **Fail:** using a sentence from a call. This is the permission most often taken
  casually and the one a venue is most likely to be surprised by.

### Step 3 — Couple material

- **Default:** do not use it. Couples are asked for nothing by default.
- **If asked at all:** a separate, specific, dated opt-in from that couple, for
  that material, on that surface. A general consent inside an account flow does
  not satisfy this.
- **Before asking, consider R-017.** A couple's workspace foreseeably contains
  special-category data about guests who consented to nothing: dietary and
  accessibility information reveals health, and routinely reveals religious
  belief. Guest lists routinely contain children's data. **The couple cannot
  consent on behalf of those people.** So the question is not only "may we use
  your material" but "what is in it".
- **Recommended:** no couple material is used publicly in year one. It costs the
  programme very little and it removes an entire class of problem.

### Step 4 — Referrals

- **Do:** ask the venue whether it is willing to be asked for referrals, and
  record the answer, before asking for one.
- **Fail:** treating a good relationship as a standing referral permission.

### Step 5 — The indexing precondition

- **Run:** `curl -s https://app.signalstudio.ie/robots.txt`
- **Expect:** `Disallow: /p`.
- **Result 2026-08-04:** **FAIL.** `/p` is not disallowed in production.
- **Why it belongs in this task:** a permission register governs what Signal
  Studio publishes deliberately. It does nothing about a couple's workspace being
  offered to search engines by default. Both have to be true for the position to
  hold.

## 5. The standing refusals

- No face detection, no auto-tagging, no face grouping. Both independent
  derivations of the role map reached this and it is recorded as a standing no.
- Nothing published implies a venue's logo or its own words appear in a couple's
  workspace (D-027 point 3).
- Nothing published states or implies legal approval or GDPR compliance (D-016).

## 7. What I would tell the founder

The register is ten minutes of setup and it is the difference between a
permission model and a memory. Set it up before the first venue says something
quotable, not after.

And take the year-one decision on couple material now, while it is free. Saying
"we do not use couples' material" is a strong sentence in a sales conversation
and it costs almost nothing to keep.
