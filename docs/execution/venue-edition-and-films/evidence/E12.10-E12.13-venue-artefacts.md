## 0. What this document is, and the one thing to read first

Four artefacts were built: the sales deck (E12.10), the founding certificate
(E12.11), the pre-booking venue kit (E12.12) and the couple welcome kit
(E12.13). Sections 1 to 3 are the audit and the build. Section 4 is the
honesty position, which is the part with commercial consequences. Section 5 is
the defect register. Section 6 is the onboarding sequence traced to code.
Section 7 is the verification. Sections 8 and 9 are what was deliberately not
done, and what is recommended next.

**The one thing to read first** is section 4. The Keepsake is promised in the
agreement, in the proposal, in the FAQ and in the approved venue copy pack, and
it does not exist in the product in any form. Four artefacts had to say
something about the end of a couple's term, and this is the first time that
question has been answered against the running code rather than against the
decision. The answer changed what three of the four artefacts say.

## 5. Defects found, owned elsewhere, recorded not fixed

Each is outside these four tasks' file column. None is edited here.

### C-1 · The founding card is presented at the wrong event

**Where:** `src/app/hq/partner-card/page.tsx`.
**What:** the page states *"The number on the front is still written by hand at
signing"*, *"the number field stays blank until a real venue signs"*, and
*"The number is still written by hand, in front of the venue, at signing."*
**Rule:** D-009 point 6 and `FOUNDING_25_PROGRAMME_MECHANICS.md` section 2. The
number is assigned when the first payment clears, never at signature.
**Why it matters:** a card handed over at signature either carries a number the
venue has not earned, or carries a blank that invites somebody to write one in.
A number written in by hand is a number that did not come from the register,
and two venues both holding 07/25 is the one failure in this programme that
cannot be corrected afterwards.
**Note:** the page's programme wording was corrected by a concurrent WP-16
stream on the same day. The assignment-event defect is a different thing and is
not addressed by that sweep.
**Owner:** E12.11 follow-on, or the R-042 stream if it is already in that file.
**Recommended wording:** presented when the payment clears, and the number
printed rather than written, because a printed number is one the register
produced.

### C-2 · The approved copy pack describes a Keepsake that is not built

**Where:** `evidence/E09.11-venue-copy-pack.md` section 1, third sentence.
**What:** it instructs a venue to tell a couple, today, *"After the wedding it
stays open, read-only, and they can download everything in one click."*
**Rule:** E09.11 section 7 sets the rule this breaks: Keepsake is described in
the future tense on any surface that describes the product as it is today, and
in the present tense only in the agreement, the proposal and the commercial
summary, which describe what is being bought. The copy pack is a
venue-to-couple surface describing the product as it is today.
**Why it matters:** this is R-020's exact shape. A venue repeats an approved
sentence to a couple, the sentence is not true yet, and what the venue told the
couple folds into the venue's own contract with them.
**Owner:** E09.11. **Not edited here**, because it is approved copy on another
task.
**Recommended replacement, used already in E12.12 and E12.13:**
*"It does not switch off the day after the wedding."*
Nothing further until a Keepsake surface exists.

### C-3 · The July venue deck is wrong on three counts and is still linked

**Where:** `public/brand/collateral/venue/venue-deck-screen.pdf`, surfaced at
`/hq/venue-kit`, and `pricing-explainer-*.pdf` beside it.
**What:** €1,500 as the founding price, the retired programme term, and a pilot
ask that the ratified offer does not contain. The `/hq/venue-kit` caption for
the pricing explainer still reads "€1,500 per venue, per year, prepaid" and
describes the founding guarantee as "permanence, not a discount", which is the
wording D-009 point 3 and D-021 replaced.
**Owner:** E12.14 or a founder call. Recommended: retire both PDFs and point
`/hq/venue-kit` at `/hq/venue-deck`, because a PDF cannot be kept current and
that is how these became wrong.

### C-4 · The internal asset system still carries the retired position

**Where:** `src/lib/hq/asset-command.ts`. It states the price as "the fixed

**Owner:** the R-042 stream, which was working in this file on the same day.

## 6. The onboarding sequence, traced to running code

E12.13's eight steps are a description of what happens, not a plan. Each was
read out of the two repositories.

| # | Step | State | Read from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The venue asks Signal Studio for a code | **by hand** | R-043; no venue-authenticated route exists |
| 2 | One code is issued for that couple | built | `src/lib/entitlements-db/codes.ts`, `/hq/entitlements` |
| 3 | The venue sends the email or hands over the card | built | this module; D-018 |
| 4 | The couple opens the code page and sees the venue name | built | `src/app/redeem/[code]/page.tsx`; E12.05 moment one |
| 5 | They create an account and the workspace opens | built | E12.05 moments two and three |
| 6 | They give the wedding date and the term widens | built | `app/src/server/actions/planning.ts:945` calls `recomputeSponsoredAccessTermQuietly` |
| 7 | The venue sees that the invitation was opened | **by hand** | D-027 point 4; R-043 |
| 8 | After the wedding | **not built** | section 4.1 |

**Step 6 corrects a natural assumption.** D-022 point 1 says "capture the
wedding date at redemption". The code does not: `recomputeSponsoredAccessTermQuietly`
is called on completing the **planning onboarding** inside the product, and its
own comment says so. The behaviour is right, because the rule only ever moves
access later and is safe in either order. But an artefact that told a venue the
date is asked for at the code page would be describing a flow that does not
exist, so E12.13 step 6 says "in the first run-through inside the product, not
at the code page".
