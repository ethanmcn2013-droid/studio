# E02.08 — Create the Founding Venue Benefits Charter covering founder access, feedback, early access, priority support and recognition.

**Task ID:** E02.08 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
Write the document that states what a founding venue gets and where each benefit stops.

## Business reason
Twenty-five unbounded relationships against one founder is the capacity failure this project cannot absorb (R-006). A benefit with a stated edge is a benefit. A benefit without one is a future argument.

## Intended outcome
`docs/strategy/FOUNDING_25_BENEFITS_CHARTER.md` exists, is venue-readable, and every benefit carries its boundary in the same paragraph.

## Deliverables
- [x] Six benefits, each with a "where it stops" clause: the rate, the number, founder access, first look, requests, recognition
- [x] A separate section for what every venue gets, so the charter is not read as the whole offer
- [x] What is asked of the venue in return: approved wording, and honesty on the call
- [x] A "what this charter is not" section

## Scope
Founding benefits and their boundaries. D-009 point 5 and D-014 point 2 are the source.

## Non-goals
The contract (WP-04). The venue pack layout (E12.12). Pricing (E02.01).

## Acceptance criteria
1. Founder access is stated as one 30-minute call per venue per year plus a named email route, and explicitly not standing access.
2. "Nothing is built for one venue" appears as a stated benefit boundary, not as a later refusal.
3. Every one of the six benefits carries an explicit boundary.
4. No permanence wording, and no statement or implication of legal or accounting review.
5. Brand voice: no em dashes, no exclamation marks, no banned register words.

## Validation plan
`node scripts/check-venue-edition-contract.mjs` (the charter is in the gated source list); brand-voice read-through; grep for em dashes and exclamation marks.

## Evidence required
The document, the check output, the voice scan.

## Risks
R-020: venues describing the product in their own words. The charter asks for the approved copy pack; the enforceable version is a clause WP-04 drafts.

## Decisions required
Whether "priority support" is an acceptable framing given there is one founder. Written honestly as a named email route answered within one working day, rather than as a support tier.
