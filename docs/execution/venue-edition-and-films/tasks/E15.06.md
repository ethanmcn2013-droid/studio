## Objective

Confirm, by request rather than by assertion, that every operational surface a
venue or a couple can reach on 1 September is actually reachable and says what
the programme says it says.

## Business reason

A privacy notice that 404s, a support address nobody watches, or an incident
procedure that exists only as a document are the failures that turn a small
problem into a public one. All six are cheap to check and expensive to discover.

## Intended outcome

Six confirmations, each with a real HTTP response, a real inbox, or a real alert
that fired, and a named owner for anything that is not live.

## Deliverables

- [ ] An HTTP status sweep of every venue-facing and couple-facing legal and
      commercial route.
- [ ] The support channel confirmed, with a stated response commitment.
- [ ] Monitoring confirmed by an alert that actually fired, not by configuration.
- [ ] The incident procedure confirmed by one rehearsal.
- [ ] An analytics disclosure that matches the running code.

## Scope

Contracts and order forms, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/security`, the venue-facing
sub-pages, the couple privacy notice, analytics on public surfaces, the support
address, Sentry and the health endpoint, and the incident runbook.

## Non-goals

- Drafting legal documents. That is E03.
- Any claim of legal approval, solicitor review, accountant verification or
  unqualified GDPR compliance (D-016). This task confirms that documents are
  reachable, not that they are correct in law.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every venue-facing and couple-facing route in the commercial and legal set
   returns 200 in production. Any 404 is named with an owner and a date.
2. The privacy notice a couple reaches describes the analytics that actually
   run on the surface they are on. If GA4 runs with no consent gate, the notice
   says so and the consent question is answered rather than left open (R-032).
3. The support address is live, monitored, and carries a stated response time
   the founder can actually meet alone.
4. Monitoring is confirmed by an error deliberately raised and observed arriving,
   not by the presence of a DSN.
5. The health endpoint returns 200 and its response is checked by something on a
   schedule, with an owner for a failure.
6. The incident procedure is rehearsed once end to end, and the rehearsal is
   recorded with what broke.
7. The rollback procedure is rehearsed once against a real deployment.
8. No document, page or reply states or implies legal approval, solicitor
   review, accountant verification or unqualified GDPR compliance.
9. The venue agreement carries a marketing-controls clause and a prohibited-
   claims list, and the venue pack contains the approved copy pack (R-020).

## Risks

- **R-020** — twenty-five untrained venues describing the product. The
  prohibited-claims list and the approved copy pack are the only controls, and
  they have to be in the agreement from version one.
- **R-032** — analytics with no consent gate on surfaces carrying other people's
  names.
- **R-013** — every document is AI-drafted with no professional indemnity behind
  it. Accepted by the founder. It must never be described as reviewed.

## Decisions required

1. Does GA4 run on couple-facing public artifacts at all? The strongest answer
   is that it does not, which removes the consent question rather than managing
   it.
2. What response time does the support address promise? One founder cannot
   promise four hours.
