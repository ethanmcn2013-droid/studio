## Objective

Produce the two documents that let a venue sign: the annual Venue Edition agreement and
the commercial order form that sits in front of it.

## Business reason

Nothing else in the programme moves until these exist. A venue cannot pay without an order
form, a number cannot be assigned without a cleared payment, and the founding schedule
(E03.03) has no parent document to attach to. The live `/venues/questions` page already
promises a venue that it gets all the paperwork before it signs, so this is on the critical
path to first contact even though the task is not flagged release-blocking.

## Intended outcome

A venue owner can read one agreement and one order form, understand exactly what is
promised, what is not, what it costs and what happens at renewal, and sign. Every
commercial term in them traces to a ratified decision or is visibly marked as an open
decision point rather than quietly chosen.

## Scope

- The annual agreement covering the standard €1,500 offer, term, renewal, exclusions,
  change of control, marketing controls, branding, founder access, liability and the
  general clauses.
- The commercial order form as a separate object: identity, price, place, term rule,
  attached documents and a signature block.
- A visible DRAFT status block under D-016 on both.
- Marked decision points wherever E02.04, E02.06 or E02.07 leaves a term open.

## Non-goals

- **No data-protection clause set.** Clause 13 points at the E03.04 instrument and stops.
  Restating it is how the two documents drift into disagreement.
- **No founding-rate terms.** Those are Schedule 1 and belong to E03.03.
- **No renewal notice period or grace window as numbers.**
  `src/lib/venue-billing.ts:98` refuses their publication in an agreement while
  `VENUE_RENEWAL_POLICY.ratified` is `false`.
- **No edits to `/terms`, `commercial-terms.v2.json`, `prohibited-claims.v1.json`, the
  Benefits Charter or the Programme Mechanics.** Each conflict is recorded and named, not
  silently reconciled.
- No couple-facing terms or privacy notice. That is E03.05.
- No solicitor review, and no wording that implies one happened.

## Implementation approach

1. Write the acceptance criteria first, then audit what already exists against them. Two
   venue-facing surfaces already promise invoice-on-receipt-of-the-signed-order-form, so
   that clause confirms what a venue has been told rather than deciding something new.
2. Carry every ratified term in the words the decision record uses, rather than restating
   it freshly. Fresh words are how a contract and a website come to disagree.
3. Where a term is open, draft to the reading that costs the venue less, mark it as a
   decision point, name the reading and give the reason. Where the code refuses a number,
   leave a marked blank instead.
4. Keep every data-protection question inside a pointer to E03.04.
5. Run the prohibited-claims rule engine over the result and classify every hit.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A DRAFT status block heads the deliverable, naming what it is, what it is not, and
   carrying the E09.10 Answer 2 sentence verbatim: "No solicitor has reviewed these
   documents, and no accountant has verified the financial or VAT position."
2. The price is €1,500 standard and €1,000 for the Founding 25, annually prepaid, both
   inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate (D-021). No formulation that puts VAT on top of
   the price appears anywhere.
3. The founding lock is expressed only as covering the base annual Venue Edition agreement,
   held for as long as it renews continuously without a lapse (D-009 point 3, E02.03). No
   permanence wording appears in the operative text of either document.
4. The initial term is one year, renewing on the same terms, matching the live
   `/venues/questions` promise, and the agreement does not silently make it a multi-year
   commitment (D-023).
5. Change of control follows the property, survives rebrand and ownership change while the
   agreement runs continuously, and does not extend to additional properties a new owner
   brings (D-009 point 4, E02.05).
6. Entitlement carries no number anywhere: every couple with a signed booking at the venue,
   while the licence is current (D-020). The survival sentence appears without being asked
   for.
7. The couple access term is stated as eighteen months from the day the couple opens the
   workspace, or three months past the wedding, whichever is later (D-022). `p7-bare-term`
   returns zero hits.
8. A marketing-controls clause exists with a named prohibited-claims list and a correction
   period (R-020).
9. Venue branding is the venue's name only: no logo, no venue-authored text, no shared
   branding (D-027 point 3).
10. Founder access is one 30-minute call per venue per year plus a named email route, stated
    not to be a retainer or an account manager (D-009 point 5, E02.09).
11. Every point the agreement cannot settle because E02.04, E02.06 and E02.07 are backlog is
    carried as a visibly marked decision point, drafted to the safer reading, with the
    reading named and its reason given. None is silently chosen. The renewal notice period
    and the grace window are marked blanks, not numbers, because
    `renewalPolicyPublicationRefusal()` refuses their publication.
12. Every data-protection reference points at the E03.04 instrument rather than restating
    it.
13. The order form is a separate object from the agreement: it carries identity, price,
    place number, term rule, attached documents and a signature block, and it references the
    agreement rather than repeating it.
14. The prohibited-claims rule engine returns no hit that is not a line whose function is to
    forbid the word it contains, and every such hit is classified in writing.

## Risks

- **R-013** — drafted without a solicitor, and this is the largest document produced under
  that decision. Clause 15, liability, is the clause most likely to be wrong and is flagged
  in the document itself.
- **R-020** — clause 12 is the mitigation. It is untested against a venue that does not
  follow it.
- **R-022** — the price is stated inclusive of VAT before Revenue has confirmed
  accountable-person status. The MyEnquiries submission is drafted and unfiled.
- **R-014** — annual prepayment with no deferred-income schedule yet.
- **New, recorded here:** `/terms` binds a venue on its face and contradicts this agreement
  in four places. Clause 18.2 answers it contractually. The page still needs a carve-out or
  a correction, and the automatic-renewal sentence is untrue for venues today.

## Decisions required

Eight decision points and six founder questions, all listed in the evidence document at
sections 3, 4 and in the marked blocks. The two that gate sending anything to a venue:

1. Approve the eight decision points at the drafted readings, or hold the agreement until
   E02.04, E02.06 and E02.07 are ratified.
2. `/terms`: Venue Edition carve-out, or correction.
