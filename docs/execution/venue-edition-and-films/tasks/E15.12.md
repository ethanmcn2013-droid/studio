# E15.12 — Run seven-day and 30-day venue reviews covering adoption, friction, support and next action.

**Task ID:** E15.12
**Epic:** E15 — Release, venue onboarding and completion of the Founding 25
**Status:** backlog
**Executor:** founder
**Approver:** Ethan McNamara
**Spec written:** 2026-08-04

---

## Objective

Run two short structured reviews per venue, at day seven and day thirty, and
turn what they surface into a fix, a roadmap item, or a stated no.

## Business reason

The founding relationship is as much the product as the software is. Two
scheduled conversations per venue is the smallest structure that catches a
problem before renewal and the largest one a sole founder can sustain across 25.

## Intended outcome

Every venue has two dated reviews on file covering adoption, friction, support
and a single next action, with nothing left without a disposition.

## Deliverables

- [ ] The review script, the same for every venue.
- [ ] Completed day-seven and day-thirty reviews.
- [ ] A cross-venue rollup of what more than one venue said.

## Scope

Adoption evidence within the suppression rules, friction in the venue's words,
support volume and resolution, and one next action.

## Non-goals

- Producing a venue-attributable count below the suppression floors. Withholding
  is the correct answer at small populations, and "fewer than three" is itself a
  disclosure.
- Committing to build anything for one venue (D-014 point 2).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Both reviews happen within three days of their due date, or the slip is
   recorded with a reason.
2. Adoption evidence shown to the venue passes the two-sided small-cell test and
   the rate floor. Any withheld metric is presented as withheld without
   disclosing the population.
3. Friction is recorded in the venue's words and every item gets a disposition:
   fixed, on the roadmap, or a stated no.
4. Support volume and resolution time are recorded per venue.
5. Exactly one next action leaves each review, with an owner and a date.
6. A cross-venue rollup exists once more than one venue has completed both
   reviews, and it names anything two or more venues raised.
7. Nothing in a review commits to building for one venue.
