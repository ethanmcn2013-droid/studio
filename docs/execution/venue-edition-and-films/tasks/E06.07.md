## Objective

Give the couple a count of how many people opened their shared Timeline, built
so that no number on any surface can be resolved to one identifiable person.

## Business reason

The venue pays EUR 1,000 to give couples something, and what is being sold is
the trust layer. A wedding page that reports "1 view, 3 August" tells the couple
which single guest read it and when. That is the exact failure the programme is
selling protection against, and it was shipped. E12.07 has to state in writing
what the couple and the venue can and cannot see; this task is what makes that
sentence true.

## Intended outcome

- No third-party analytics loads on `/p`, `/s`, `/share` or `/embed`.
- A view count exists, it is first-party and aggregate, and it is withheld
  entirely below a floor rather than rounded, hedged or captioned.
- No surface renders a count beside a day-precision timestamp.
- All three controls are asserted by a test that runs in CI.

## Implementation approach

The threshold is a property of the value, not of the caller. R-028's defect was
a floor implemented in a projector no production code called; the fix shape is a
function that returns a discriminated union and never a raw integer, so a
downstream formatter has nothing to reformat.

Suppression is two-sided in the sense this surface requires. R-027's two sides
are the value and its complement against a known population. Here there is no
population, and the second side is the timestamp: a count of 1 names a person,
and so does a precise date beside any small count. Both are withheld together,
and the timestamp is coarsened to a month even above the floor.

## Acceptance criteria

1. No third-party analytics loads on `/p`, `/s`, `/share` or `/embed`, enforced
   at the component seam and at the Content-Security-Policy, and asserted by a
   source-text contract test registered in the `pnpm test` chain. A Playwright
   assertion cannot carry this: the e2e suite runs with `VERCEL_ENV=preview` and
   `GoogleTag` renders only when `VERCEL_ENV === "production"`, so a browser
   assertion that GA is absent passes today and would keep passing if the
   exclusion were deleted.
2. The count is first-party and aggregate, with no IP, user-agent, referrer or
   raw token durably stored. The existing `/s` mechanism already satisfies this
   and is recorded as passing evidence rather than rebuilt.
3. A suppression rule lives in a library function with its own unit test: the
   threshold is a property of the value, not of the caller, and no formatter can
   build a displayed count from a raw integer.
4. Below the floor the surface says nothing about the data. It never renders
   "fewer than five" or any other bound, because a bound is itself a disclosure.
   Counts of 0, 1, 2, 3 and 4 produce byte-identical output.
5. `lastQualifiedViewAt` is removed from the surface at day precision and
   coarsened to a month, shown only above the same floor.
   `src/modules/timeline/app/audience/artifact-studio.tsx` no longer renders a
   count and a last-viewed timestamp side by side.
6. Every surface that renders the count goes through the same presenter,
   including the dense list row in `audience-manager.tsx`, so a list cannot
   become the leak the detail page closed.
7. The floor number is recorded as a founder decision and is explicitly not
   D-011's venue-cohort thresholds of 3 and 5.
8. If the couple's artifact is `/p` or `/share`, the absence of an equivalent
   first-party counter there is stated as not built, and
   `share_link_visits.user_agent_hint` is dealt with explicitly.

## Decisions required

1. **The floor.** Shipped at 5, chosen because a couple typically shares the
   link with a handful of people at once and a count of 3 inside that handful is
   still resolvable by the person who sent it. Needs ratification.
2. **The withheld state discloses that the count is below the floor.** That is
   unavoidable in any design that ever shows a number, and it is a much weaker
   disclosure than the count itself. Recorded as accepted unless the founder
   would rather no count ever appeared.
3. **`share_link_visits.user_agent_hint`** stores the first 60 characters of a
   visitor's user agent per visit, on the `/share` path. Nothing renders it
   today. Delete the column, or keep it and say so in the privacy notice.
