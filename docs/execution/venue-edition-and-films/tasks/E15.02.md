## Objective

Prove, once, against production, that money received from a venue is recorded at
the ratified founding rate, is stamped with a VAT basis that matches D-021, and
assigns exactly one founding number, with an audit line and no way to overwrite
the record afterwards.

## Business reason

The first EUR 1,000 is the moment the programme becomes real and the moment the
price lock starts. A payment recorded at the wrong amount, on the wrong basis,
or with a founding number that another venue also holds cannot be walked back:
D-009 point 6 makes the number the one promise that has no correction path.

## Intended outcome

One venue exists in the production entitlements database with a cleared payment,
a `sponsor_price_agreements` row at EUR 1,000 on a VAT-inclusive basis, a
founding number in 1 to 25 held by nobody else, an audit line naming the actor,
and a term window that the renewal worklist reads correctly.

## Deliverables

- [ ] A rehearsal run against a disposable database, recorded before any real
      money is touched.
- [ ] The production run, with the real output pasted.
- [ ] `entitlements:integrity` green after the run.
- [ ] The Revenue MyEnquiries submission filed, dated, with the acknowledgement

- [ ] A written statement of the VAT position taken and its basis.

## Scope

`scripts/mark-venue-paid.ts`, `recordAnnualPrepayment`, `assignFoundingNumber`,
`sponsor_price_agreements`, the audit ledger and its append-only triggers,
`scripts/check-entitlements-integrity.mjs`, and the invoice or order form the
venue actually pays against.

## Non-goals

- Building a venue payment provider. There is none and none is planned for
  1 September. Venue money is invoiced out of band by design.
- Renewal. No renewal falls due before 1 September or before 2027.
- Any recommendation on VAT registration. The position is the founder's and the
  Revenue reply is the source (D-016, R-014, R-018).

## Implementation approach

Rehearse the whole path against a disposable database first, then run it once
against production with real cleared money. The rehearsal is not optional: the
script writes to an append-only table, so a mistake in production is permanent
by design.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A rehearsal run against a disposable entitlements database records a founding
   prepayment, assigns a number, and a second identical run returns
   `already_recorded` rather than writing a second row or moving the term.
2. The production run records `amountReceivedCents = 100000` for a `founding`
   plan and the script refuses any other amount for that plan.
3. The written agreement row carries the VAT-inclusive basis string
   `D-009 · D-021 · contracts/commercial-terms.v2.json`, and
   `check-entitlements-integrity.mjs` reports no price agreement on a
   non-inclusive VAT basis.
4. Exactly one founding number is assigned, it is in 1 to 25, it is unique in
   `sponsors`, and its `founding_number_assigned_at` is not earlier than the
   cleared-payment timestamp.
5. `pnpm entitlements:integrity` exits 0 against production after the run.
6. An attempt to UPDATE or DELETE the recorded agreement row is refused by the
   append-only triggers, demonstrated rather than asserted.
7. The `--vat-rate` flag is omitted unless the Revenue reply has determined the
   treatment. A recorded rate of zero is a claim; "not determined" is the honest
   record.
8. The Revenue MyEnquiries submission is filed before the agreement the payment
   settles is signed, and the submission reference is recorded.
9. The venue's invoice or order form states the price as inclusive of VAT at the
   prevailing rate, and nowhere states or implies accountant verification
   (D-016).

## Risks

- **R-022** — EUR 1,000 VAT-inclusive nets about EUR 813 if Signal Studio is an
  accountable person. Accepted by the founder. The first payment is the trigger
  to rebuild the model on the net figure.
- **R-018** — receipt of foreign B2B services may already make Signal Studio an
  accountable person, with no turnover threshold. Filing the submission is free
  and converts the guess into a written position.
- Recording a founding number before payment clears is the one error with no
  correction path.

## Decisions required

1. Is the first payment taken from a real Cohort 1 venue, or from a rehearsal
   venue created for the purpose and then retired? A rehearsal venue leaves a
   permanent row in an append-only table and consumes a founding number unless
   it is recorded on the `paid` plan instead.
2. Does the founder file the Revenue submission before the first agreement is
   signed, as R-018 and R-022 both direct?
