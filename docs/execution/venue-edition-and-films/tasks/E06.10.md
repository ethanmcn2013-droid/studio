## Objective

Assess the shipped desktop editorial Timeline against written criteria, hold
its identity under CI, and report what it gets wrong rather than redesigning
what it gets right.

## Business reason

This is the layout in the film and in every screenshot a venue will be shown.
It is also the layout on the owner's own page, so it is what a couple sees
before they decide whether the link is worth sending.

## Intended outcome

The desktop layout's identity is asserted in CI; its accessibility and overflow
behaviour are evidenced with real numbers at four widths; and the two things
that are actually wrong with it are named with their exact locations.

component, not a change of standard.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The artifact has three tiers driven by container width — the base editorial
   rail, a mid tier at 980px and a widest tier above it — and the widest tier
   is the one that reveals collision-avoided labels.
2. The horizontal rail's own affordances belong only to the wide layout:
   horizontal scrolling with hidden scrollbars above the breakpoint, and never
   below it.
3. The owner surfaces embed the exact same artifact rather than a variant, and
   the owner's app shell suppresses the artifact's own wordmark so two
   wordmarks never stack.
4. Reduced motion is honoured, and the motion preference is hydration-safe:
   server and first client render must choose the same subtree.
5. Zero critical and zero serious axe violations at 390, 768, 1280 and 1440,
   and no horizontal document overflow.
6. The contract file asserting all of the above **runs in CI**.
7. Anything found that contradicts a ratified decision is reported with its
   file and line, whether or not it is in this task's scope to fix.

## Decisions required

1. **The wordmark above the couple's names.** `timeline-artifact.tsx:279-281`
   renders a `timeline` wordmark with an accent dot at the top of the shared
   page, above the couple's names. D-011 point 2 permits one line of
   attribution in the footer and forbids a badge in the viewport. The footer
   line already exists and is correct. `experience/tests/critical-experiences.spec.ts`
   asserts the wordmark's exact text, so a passing test currently locks in what
   the decision forbids. Removing it is a visible design change to an approved
   task's output (E06.08) and therefore a founder call, not a tidy-up.
2. **`detailNote`'s three generated sentences** (`timeline-artifact.tsx:320-324`)
   read "This milestone is part of the story so far", "This is the next point
   on the shared journey", "This milestone comes later on the shared journey".
   They are front-facing copy on a couple's wedding page, and "journey" as a
   noun is on the banned list. Either replace them or keep them deliberately.
