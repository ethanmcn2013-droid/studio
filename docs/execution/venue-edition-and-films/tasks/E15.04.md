## Objective

Verify the venue-facing evidence path as it actually exists at launch: Signal HQ
administers, the venue receives a report, and no venue holds a login.

## Business reason

A venue paying EUR 1,000 is buying, among other things, evidence that the thing
it gave its couples is being used. That evidence has to be true, has to withhold
anything that identifies one couple, and has to arrive in a form the venue can
open. If it is wrong once, the venue stops believing all of it.

## Intended outcome

One venue's Usage snapshot, CSV and report have been produced from live data,
checked against the suppression rules, and confirmed to contain nothing that
identifies an individual couple. What is delivered and what is not is written
down and matches what the venue was told.

- [ ] A live Usage snapshot for one venue, produced through the live loader.
- [ ] The CSV export, opened and checked.
- [ ] The report file, opened and checked. **It is HTML, not PDF** (see scope).
- [ ] A renewal-worklist read, with the honest note that no term is live yet.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The Usage snapshot for a venue with fewer than 3 eligible sponsored
   workspaces publishes no behavioural count at all, and the surface says
   nothing that reveals the population size.
2. A behavioural count is withheld when the count is below 3 **or when the
   complement is below 3**. A count of 39 of 40 is withheld for the same reason
   a count of 1 of 40 is.
3. No surface anywhere renders "fewer than 3" or any equivalent. That phrasing
   is itself the disclosure.
4. Every percentage shown to a venue comes from the rate value type and is
   withheld below 5 eligible activations. No formatter anywhere constructs a
   percentage from two loose numbers.
5. The CSV export opens in a spreadsheet, carries the same withheld states as
   the on-screen surface, and contains no couple name, email, workspace slug or
   identifier.
6. The report download opens. Its content type and file extension match what it
   actually is. Every venue-facing description of it uses the same word.
7. `members` is empty on the live venue snapshot, and no surface offers a venue
   an invite-a-member action that does nothing.
8. The renewal worklist runs without error and correctly reports that no venue
   has a live term.
9. The delivery model is written down and matches the venue pack: Signal HQ
   produces and sends; the venue does not log in; the HQ password is never given
   to a venue.
