## Objective

Publish the FAQ of record as a page a venue can be sent, read aloud from, or
paste into an email reply, with every answer either verbatim from an approved
source or explicitly marked as new copy needing its own sign-off.

## Business reason

The only FAQ artifact in the repository until 2026-08-03 was
`VENUE_FAQ_OBJECTIONS.md`, dated 2026-05-27, headed "internal use only", and
carrying two verified defects: "seamless" on the master never-list, and a
privacy answer naming a fourth visible thing. It is what got reached for on a
live call regardless of its header. D-032 R3 replaced it with E09.11 §10 and
bannered the old file. That replacement has no surface. This task gives it one.

## Intended outcome

There is one public page carrying the twelve answers, one venue reads the same
thing another venue reads, and the two questions nobody wants in writing are in
writing.

## Implementation approach

Reproduce the approved answers verbatim. Do not paraphrase approved copy.
Where an answer's stated conditions of use are unmet, substitute the interim
answer the same document supplies rather than publishing a claim that is not
true yet, and record the substitution as a conflict rather than reconciling it
silently. Where the task title requires an answer the FAQ of record does not
have, write it from a named decision and mark it as new copy needing its own
approval.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All twelve answers from E09.11 §10.2 appear, and each is byte-for-byte the approved text except where a departure is named in the evidence document.
2. Every departure from §10.2 is listed in the evidence document with the rule that forced it and a recommendation the founder can approve or push back on.
3. The price answer states €1,000 and €1,500 as including VAT at the prevailing rate, describes the founding rate as €500 a year less, and conditions the lock on continuous renewal with no permanence wording anywhere.
4. The entitlement answer carries no number, no seat, no allotment and no per-couple arithmetic the venue is asked to supply, and does not use the words "unlimited" or "no seats" while P6 stands.
5. The access-term answer carries the grace rule. A bare "18 months" appears nowhere.
6. The privacy answer opens with "No", carries the D-032 R4 launch-scope sentence verbatim, and names no fourth visible thing.
7. No answer states or implies legal approval, solicitor review, accountant verification or unqualified GDPR compliance, and no answer describes a document as existing when it does not.
8. The branding answer states the venue's name only — no logo, no colour, no venue-written message.
9. Insurance and data location do not appear as written answers, and the page says plainly that they are answered by a person.
10. No banned programme term, no manufactured urgency, no em dash, no exclamation mark anywhere in the file, and the route is in `sitemap.ts` and in the commercial contract sweep.
