# E02.11 — Define when a founding place is reserved, when it expires, when it becomes locked, and when the programme automatically closes.

**Task ID:** E02.11 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
Define the life of a founding place as a state machine with dated transitions.

## Business reason
Twenty-five places against cohorts of 25 emails means more than 25 venues are in play at once. Without a stated rule, two venues can both believe they hold 03/25.

## Intended outcome
`FOUNDING_25_PROGRAMME_MECHANICS.md` sections 3 to 6: the states, their durations, the close condition, and the decided edge cases.

## Deliverables
- [x] State diagram and table: Researched, Invited, Proposed, Held, Signed, Paid, Onboarded, Expired, Lapsed
- [x] Reserved at proposal, 14-day hold, one extension by Ethan, recorded when extended
- [x] Signature-to-payment handling, so nobody loses a place to a slow accounts department and nobody blocks one indefinitely with a signature and no money
- [x] Automatic close on the 25th cleared payment, with a four-step ordered consequence
- [x] Eleven edge cases decided, including the programme closing while holds are outstanding
- [x] The register requirements this creates, listed and handed on

## Scope
Reservation, expiry, locking, closing, and the register fields required to run it honestly.

## Non-goals
Building the register. E15.17, moving commercial surfaces to standard pricing at close, stays its own release-blocking task.

## Acceptance criteria
1. A place is reserved at proposal, not at expression of interest, and the proposal states the hold end date.
2. The hold is 14 days, once, extendable only by Ethan, and recorded when extended.
3. A place locks on cleared payment.
4. The programme closes on the 25th cleared payment, and outstanding holds are voided and the venues told the same day.
5. Every edge case in section 5 has one decided answer rather than a note to decide later.
6. The public counter is the paid count, so no true-at-send claim depends on a hold that can expire.

## Validation plan
Walk each of the eleven edge cases against the state table and confirm none has two possible outcomes.

## Evidence required
The mechanics document, sections 3 to 6.

## Risks
The close sequence assumes E15.17 executes as one deliberate action rather than twenty manual edits. Recorded in section 6.

## Decisions required
None for the mechanics. The register implementation is downstream work and is not decided here.
