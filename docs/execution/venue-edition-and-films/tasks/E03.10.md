## Objective

Write the lifecycle rules for the seven cases in the title, drafted against what the code
actually does, and present options rather than a rule for the two the role map records as
unanswerable.

## Business reason

Six of these seven cases will happen inside the first twenty-five venues. A wedding is
postponed, a wedding is cancelled, a couple moves venue, a venue does not renew, a couple
separates, one partner deletes their account. An edge case found by a founding venue costs
the reference. The last of them is not hypothetical: today, whichever partner holds
`ownerUserId` can delete their account and destroy the other partner's wedding record.

## Intended outcome

Nine cases, each with an expected behaviour, a state against code, and either a drafted rule
or a marked set of options. Nothing silently chosen, and nothing described as covered that
is not.

## Deliverables

- [x] `evidence/E03.10-lifecycle-cancellation-separation-and-unlinking.md` — nine cases, the
      register cross-referenced to ADR-008 §11, six decision points, a ten-item
      not-built list, seven open legal points and a verification table.
- [x] This specification.

## Scope

Cancellation. Postponement. Venue change mid-planning. Venue non-renewal and licence lapse.
Couple separation. Account ownership and account deletion (R-023). Venue-workspace
unlinking. Plus the three neighbouring cases ADR-008 §11 leaves uncovered and this task's
title touches: guest erasure, supplier objection, lost account access.

## Non-goals

- Building anything. No route, no migration, no writer.
- Deciding the separation rule or the R-023 answer. Both are presented as options with a
  recommendation, because the role map records the first as CANNOT ANSWER and D-027 point 1
  makes the second a founder decision tied to this task.
- Rewriting the ratified survival promise. D-020 point 2 is restated in the couple's words
  and not in new ones.
- The couple's terms and privacy notice, which carry the guest and supplier route. That is
  E03.05.

## Implementation approach

Harvest rather than invent, the same method E04.11 used. Read the state machine and its
test before drafting anything that touches access. Check every drafted rule against the
orthogonality invariant. Where the role map says CANNOT ANSWER, present options with what
each costs and what each risks, recommend one, and choose nothing.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A DRAFT status block heads the document with the E09.10 Answer 2 sentence verbatim and
   the date.
2. All seven cases in the title are covered, each with an expected behaviour and a state of
   covered, rule only, or undecided against the code.
3. Venue non-renewal restates the ratified survival rule and cites the invariant and the
   test that enforce it, not a promise in prose.
4. Couple separation presents options rather than a rule, states that the role map records
   it as CANNOT ANSWER, and names what the indexed-page correction does to the freeze
   proposal.
5. R-023 is stated as a verified live defect with its file and its actual deletion
   behaviour, and is answered with options and a recommendation, since D-027 point 1 ties it
   to this task.
6. Postponement states the ratified extension rule and the verified gap that no wedding date
   reaches the term computation for a sponsored couple today.
7. Every case ADR-008 §11 marks uncovered that falls inside this task is either resolved
   with a drafted rule or carried as a marked decision point. None is silently chosen.
8. No rule is invented for any question the role map records as unresolved, and nothing
   states or implies legal approval or GDPR compliance.
9. A single plain list of what is not built accompanies the rules, and every drafted rule is
   true regardless of which items on that list get built.

## Risks

- **R-023** is live and shippable today and this task does not fix it. It presents the
  options. D-027 point 1 dates the fix before Cohort 1.
- **R-015 residual:** the wedding-date limb of the access term cannot fire, so the
  postponement rule is an operations commitment rather than a computed one.
- **R-043:** there is no venue-authenticated route, so the release control the unlinking
  rule describes has nowhere to live.
- Two of the nine cases are deliberately undecided. If they stay undecided, the couple terms
  cannot carry the separation clause and E03.05 §3 clause 5 stays a pointer with a blank in
  it.

## Decisions required

Six, listed as DP-1 through DP-6 in §4 of the evidence document. The two that block other
documents:

1. **DP-4 (R-023).** Refuse deletion, delete the person and keep the workspace, or delete
   and warn. Recommended: delete the person and keep the workspace, with an ownership
   transfer. Due before Cohort 1.
2. **DP-5 (separation).** Freeze and dual export, unpublish first then freeze, owner of
   record wins, or no rule. Recommended: unpublish first, then freeze and dual export. The
   couple terms cannot be finished without it.
