## Objective

State the ownership and collaborator model that ships today, and record honestly that account recovery does not exist.

## Business reason

A couple is two people and one account. When one of them loses access, or they separate, the product has no answer today. Both are ordinary in weddings.

## Intended outcome

The gap is named, sized and put in front of the founder as a decision rather than discovered by the first couple it happens to.

## Deliverables

- [ ] studio/docs/architecture/ADR-008-venue-edition-lifecycle.md section 4, ownership, co-owner, collaborators and the recovery gap
- [ ] The four scenarios stated as questions the model must answer

## Scope

Documenting ownerUserId, workspaceMembers, pendingInvites and the erasure and export paths. Naming the recovery gap.

## Non-goals

Building recovery. Building ownership transfer. Answering the separation scenario, which is a founder decision and sits in E03.10.

3. The four scenarios are listed with the current answer or an explicit 'no answer'.
