# E01.08 — Create a decision log recording every ratified commercial, legal, product, design and film decision.

**Task ID:** E01.08 · **Epic:** E01 · **Status:** in_progress
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03

---

## Objective

A decision log that records every ratified decision across the five named
domains, and can be queried by domain rather than read end to end.

## Business reason

Twenty-four decisions were made in two days. They are the only record of why the
price, the term, the entitlement and the legal position are what they are. A log
nobody can navigate gets re-litigated, and re-litigating a settled price with 29
days left is the expensive kind of waste.

## Intended outcome

The log answers "show me the design decisions" without a full read, and its four
stale headers stop sending the founder back to settled questions.

## Deliverables

- [x] An index at the top of `DECISIONS.md`: by domain, and the four resolved-but-still-proposed entries
- [x] The change-request register
- [x] An honest count per domain, including the zeroes

## Scope

`DECISIONS.md` as the log, its navigability, and its coverage of the five named
domains.

## Non-goals

- **Not rewriting any entry.** The log is append-only and stays that way. The index is navigation, not revision.
- Not making the missing design decisions. That is a programme gap, recorded as I-009.
- Not building tooling over the log. Recorded honestly instead: nothing validates it.

## Dependencies

None recorded.

## Inputs

- `DECISIONS.md` D-001 to D-024
- `evidence/change-requests/CR-001.md`, `CR-002.md`
- `PROJECT.md` §15 — the source-of-truth order the log sits inside

## Implementation approach

Audit all twenty-four entries against the five domains the title names and count
them, including the zero. Add an index rather than edit entries. Record two
findings the audit produced: four entries still headed "proposed, founder
decision required" that were resolved by later decisions, and zero ratified
design decisions with UI freeze seventeen days away.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A single log records every ratified decision, append-only, with no entry rewritten by this task.
2. Every decision carries date, status, decision-maker, what it affects, the decision, and its rationale.
3. The log is navigable by the five domains the title names, without reading it end to end.
4. Coverage per domain is counted and stated honestly, including any domain with zero entries.
5. Entries whose headers are stale relative to a later decision are identified at the top so nobody acts on a resolved question as if it were open.
6. Superseding chains are explicit: a decision that changes another names it.
7. Change requests are registered alongside decisions, with their status.
8. Where the log's coverage reveals a gap in the programme rather than in the log, that is recorded as a RAID entry and not disguised as a documentation problem.

## Validation plan

- Count entries per domain by reading all 24 and classifying each
- Confirm no existing entry's text changed: the index is additive
- Confirm every entry carries the six required fields
- Confirm each of D-003 to D-006 is mapped to the decision that resolved it

## Evidence required

- `DECISIONS.md` and its index
- The per-domain counts
- `RAID.md` I-009, the zero-design-decisions finding

## Risks

The real risk is what the audit found rather than anything the task introduces:
**no design decision has ever been ratified** and UI freeze is 2026-08-20. A
document cannot fix that.

## Decisions required

None for the log. I-009 needs your attention, and it is not a governance task.
