# E15.17 — Close the founding offer at 25 paid agreements, switch all new commercial surfaces to €1,500 and prepare the first renewal cycle.

---

## Objective

Close the founding offer the moment the twenty-fifth agreement is paid, move
every forward-facing surface to EUR 1,500, and set up the first renewal cycle
without disturbing the twenty-five locked rates.

## Business reason

The founding rate is a permanent lock for the venues who hold it and a closed
door for everyone after. Leaving EUR 1,000 visible after the twenty-fifth
payment gives away EUR 500 a year, permanently, to a venue who was not promised
it.

## Intended outcome

Every new commercial surface states EUR 1,500. Every existing founding venue
still reads EUR 1,000 on every past and future term. The renewal cycle is
prepared before the first term ends.

## Deliverables

- [ ] The closure record: date, the twenty-fifth venue, the cleared payment.
- [ ] The commercial-surface sweep to EUR 1,500.
- [ ] Proof that founding venues are unaffected.
- [ ] The renewal preparation.

## Scope

Every venue-facing price statement, the machine contract, the enforcement
scripts, the certificate, the proposal, the decks, and the renewal worklist.

## Non-goals

- Changing the price a founding venue pays. The lock holds while the agreement
  renews continuously without lapse (D-009 point 3).
- Any wording change to the lock. Never "for life", never "forever", never "in
  perpetuity" (D-021 restates this explicitly).

## Acceptance criteria

1. The offer closes on the date the twenty-fifth **cleared payment** is
   recorded, not on the twenty-fifth signature.
2. Every forward-facing commercial surface states EUR 1,500 inclusive of VAT at
   the prevailing rate. The sweep uses the same inventory method that found
   roughly 140 files last time, not the sixteen-file method that missed them.
3. `check-venue-edition-contract.mjs` and `check-venue-copy.mjs` pass against
   the post-closure position.
4. Every existing founding venue's `sponsor_price_agreements` history still
   reads EUR 1,000 for every past term, and its next renewal is priced at
   EUR 1,000. Demonstrated by query, not asserted.
5. The rate lock survives a rebrand or ownership change of the property and does
   not transfer to additional properties a new owner brings (D-009 point 4).
6. The renewal worklist produces a correct list for the first venues approaching
   renewal, and each has an action.
7. No surface anywhere says the lock is forever, for life, or in perpetuity.
8. The closure is recorded as a dated decision, because the offer closing is a
   commercial fact other documents depend on.

## Risks

- **The sweep is the risk.** I-002 records that a full-workspace inventory found
  roughly 140 files carrying a retired position when the previous change had
  reached sixteen. A partial sweep at closure leaves EUR 1,000 live somewhere a
  venue can find it.
- **R-022** — every founding venue is locked at a VAT-inclusive EUR 1,000. If the
  Revenue answer makes Signal Studio an accountable person, the lock makes the
  haircut permanent and uncorrectable.
- No renewal falls due before 2027, so the renewal path stays untested for a
  year after this task completes.
