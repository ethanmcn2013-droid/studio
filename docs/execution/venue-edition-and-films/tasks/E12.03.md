## Objective

Build the proposal state of `/v/<token>`: the same private URL, after a
conversation, carrying the finalised proposal, the commercial summary, the venue's
place number and the fourteen-day hold, parameterised per venue.

## Business reason

It is the page a venue reads while deciding to pay €1,000. Every condition attached
to that number has to be on it, because a price that travels without its conditions
is how a venue and Signal Studio end up pointing at different documents a year later.

## Intended outcome

After a walkthrough, the venue returns to the link it already has and finds the
proposal there. It reads its own name, the person the walkthrough was with, the
date, what is taken, what number is next, when the hold ends, and every commercial
condition in one table. It never receives a second link.

## Deliverables

- [x] The `proposal` state in `src/app/v/[token]/states.tsx`
- [x] Per-venue slots validated at load: venue name, first name, walkthrough date,
      hold end date, paid count, next free number
- [x] The commercial summary, all seventeen rows, quoted from E11.11 section 3
- [x] Refusal of an incomplete proposal row, because E11.11 section 2 makes an
      unfilled slot a send defect
- [x] Tests covering every slot, the founding-number format and impossible dates

## Scope

The proposal state, the per-venue parameterisation, the commercial summary, the
number sentence, the hold sentence, and the order-form ask.

## Non-goals

- **A second URL.** E13.16 section 3 forbids it: "The venue never gets a second
  link." The state is a property of the row, not of the address.
- **The order form itself.** E11.11 section 4 holds its words; it is returned by
  email and there is no form, no sign-in and no card field at this stage.
- **A venue slug or a query parameter.** D-032 R13. The token is bare and the venue
  is resolved server-side, which is why this page cannot use the `venue` tracking
  parameter every other commercial link uses.
- **Invoice timing, renewal notice, failed payment, cancellation and refunds.**
  E11.11 section 3 records these as not ratified. They are not invented here.

## Implementation approach

The proposal is E11.11 section 2, quoted, with its six slots filled from the link
row. Every slot is validated when the registry loads rather than when the page
renders, so a missing walkthrough date is a refusal at start-up instead of a gap in
front of a venue.

One deliberate correction to E11.11 section 2, and it is the reason this page does
not simply concatenate that document. Its "What you see" block says "and adoption
figures across your couples as a group" in the present tense, which is untrue on
1 September. `E09.11-copy-system.md` section 5.1a records that as a boundary
request and requires the three-part privacy block instead. This page carries the
three-part block, including the launch-scope sentence verbatim.

The number sentence follows E11.11 section 2.3: state the paid count, state the next
free number, and say in the same sentence that numbers follow cleared payment and
other proposals are open. That satisfies the two mechanics rules that pull against
each other, and it is true at the moment of sending.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The page renders the venue's name and no other venue asset, from a bare token
   with no slug and no query parameter.
2. A proposal row missing any of the six slots is refused at load and never rendered
   with a gap.
3. The commercial summary appears in full, and every row matches E11.11 section 3.
4. Both prices are stated as including VAT at the prevailing rate; the founding
   difference is "€500 a year less"; the lock is continuous renewal without lapse;
   no permanence wording appears.
5. The number is stated as 01/25 through 25/25, assigned when payment clears and
   explicitly not on signature, with the fourteen-day hold stated once and no
   countdown.
6. The launch-scope sentence appears verbatim, and no fourth venue-visible thing is
   named.
7. The couple access term is never a bare eighteen months.
8. There is exactly one action: return the order form.
9. The page is `noindex, nofollow`, excluded from robots and the sitemap, and loads
   no third-party script.
10. Nothing states or implies legal, solicitor or accountant approval.

## Risks

- **The paid count goes stale.** Mechanics section 1 requires it to be verified
  against the register at the moment of sending. It is operator data in the runtime
  document, so a stale count is an operator error the page cannot catch. Recorded
  rather than engineered around, because the register is the truth and it is not
  reachable from this route.
- **R-043.** The page describes what a venue sees. It must not describe a portal the
  venue can log into, because there is no venue-authenticated route.

## Decisions required

1. Whether the paid count and next free number should be read live from the
   founding-number register instead of carried in the runtime document. Built as
   carried, because the register is in the entitlements database and a private page
   with no login should not open a database connection on a cold click.
