## Objective

Put the ratified commercial position on the public venues page, accurately, and
verify every line of it against the decision that authorises it.

## Business reason

This page is what a venue reads after the film. It carries a price, a founding
promise and a description of what the couple receives. Every claim on it reaches
25 businesses at once and is indexed. A claim the product cannot keep is
expensive with exactly the people whose reference value is why they were
recruited.

audit behind every line so a later session does not have to re-derive it.

## Implementation approach

Audit-first per D-015 Q2. The page rewrite already existed, uncommitted, with no
task over it. Rather than rewrite it, every claim was traced to the decision that
authorises it and the gaps corrected. A full-file search was run for each class
of forbidden wording rather than spot-checking.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Both prices appear, are stated as VAT-inclusive, and the founding rate is described as €500 a year less.
2. The founding lock is described as holding on continuous renewal, and the page contains no permanence wording anywhere.
3. The number is stated as 01/25 to 25/25, assigned on cleared payment and explicitly not on signature, with the fourteen-day hold stated.
4. Entitlement is every booked couple with no seat count anywhere on the page.
5. The couple access term is stated as eighteen months or three months past the wedding, whichever is later — which the product can now actually deliver.
6. No banned programme term appears: partner, member, investor, exclusive, guaranteed, certified, or manufactured urgency.
7. Nothing on the page implies legal, solicitor or accountant approval.
8. Venue branding is described as the venue's name only, including in the OpenGraph description.
9. Any capability not yet built is stated as not yet built.
10. Scarcity is a static true statement, never a countdown that can go stale.

authorising decision · `pnpm test` · `pnpm typecheck` · `pnpm build`.
