## Objective

Give the couple four per-milestone publication states, and prove each one's
effect on the serialised public payload rather than on the control that is
supposed to produce it.

## Business reason

The couple's page is shown to guests, parents and suppliers. A milestone the
couple wants private has to be genuinely absent from the payload, not hidden by
CSS. This is the control that makes a wedding workspace safe to publish at all,
and E12.07 has to describe it to a venue in one sentence.

## Intended outcome

Private and title-and-date are proven working and recorded as evidence. Image
and short-story have an agreed data model, a named blocker, and an explicit
founder decision, rather than a placeholder that looks built.

## Deliverables

- [x] Behavioural proof of **private** by both mechanisms that produce it.
- [x] Behavioural proof of **title-and-date**, on a serialised DTO.
- [x] The image and short-story content model, pre-agreed in
      `evidence/E06.01-public-artifact-content-model.md` §6.
- [x] A regression that goes red the moment either state is half-built.
- [ ] **Image publication. Not built.**
- [ ] **Short-story publication. Not built.**

## Scope

The two shipped states, audited, evidenced and regression-guarded. The written
model and named blocker for the two unbuilt states.

  and no receipt-backed runner (`scripts/db/migrate.mjs:465` reads

  writing none.
- Photograph upload, crop, EXIF stripping and alt text. That is E06.03.
- The date-concealment control on the wedding day itself. That is E06.06.

## Implementation approach

Split in two, and the two halves do not share a tick.

**Half one, shipped and evidenced.** Private has two independent mechanisms and
both were proved: a milestone omitted from `selectedSourceIds` is never copied
into `timeline_publication_items`, and a milestone whose overlay is `hidden` is
refused at selection with `"A selected milestone is not available in this
workspace"`. Title-and-date was proved on the serialised DTO: an undated
milestone emits no `date` key.

**Half two, not built.** Image and short-story need a new `ITEM_KEYS` entry,
a new validator branch and a new frozen column on `timeline_publication_items`.
The column has nowhere to go. Recorded as a founder decision with three options
and a recommendation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Private is proven twice**, because two mechanisms produce it: a milestone
   omitted from the selection is never copied, and a milestone with overlay
   `hidden = true` is refused at selection. Both proved on behaviour, and the
   refusal proved by a fail-before mutation.
2. **Title-and-date is proven on a serialised DTO**: an undated milestone
   carries no `date` key at all, while a dated one in the same payload carries
   exactly `date, publicId, state, title`.
3. **Freeze semantics are stated with their evidence**: publication items are a
   copy taken at create, so the published page does not follow the private
   source. Where this is proved and where it is only read is distinguished.
4. **Image and short-story are recorded as not built**, with the exact schema,
   validator and migration work each needs, and the migration blocker named.
5. **A regression guards the half-built case**: a test fails if `ITEM_KEYS`
   gains an image or story key, or `timeline_publication_items` gains an image
   or story column, without the content model being updated.
6. Nothing in the spec or the evidence describes image or short-story as
   available, and no user-visible string offers them (D-016).

## Risks

- **The Timeline database has no production migration path.** Any task that
  needs a Timeline column is blocked behind either building the runner or
  agreeing another route. This affects E06.02 and E06.03 and it was not on the
  register before this package.
- Deferring image and short-story changes what E06.04 can transition into and
  what E06.09/E06.10 can lay out. Sequencing risk, not a defect.

## Decisions required

1. Image and short-story need columns on the Timeline database, which has no
   receipt-backed migration runner. Do we build the runner, ship the columns by
   another agreed route, or defer both states past 1 September?

**Recommendation: defer both past 1 September.** The visible work is a state
picker; the real work is a public byte path, EXIF stripping and a migration
runner for a second database, inside 29 days. Private and title-and-date are the
two states that make the page safe to publish, and they work today.
