## Objective

Record a cleared annual prepaid Venue Edition charge correctly, once, at the
right price for the plan, with the VAT position stated honestly and every
prepayment individually identifiable.

## Business reason

Twenty-five founding venues are about to prepay EUR 1,000 each and the standard
agreement is EUR 1,500. Getting the recorded amount wrong is not a reporting
inconvenience: it is the cash ledger, it feeds HQ Traction, and under D-009's
price lock a founding venue recorded at the standard rate cannot be corrected by
raising the price later. That exact defect already shipped once (I-002).

## Intended outcome

A venue's annual charge is derivable from one place, matches the machine
contract, renders VAT-inclusive in the ratified wording everywhere, and lands as
an append-only term row that can be found again for retrospective VAT
correction. The recording path refuses the wrong plan's price rather than
trusting the operator.

record, the writer's refusals, the operator command, and the honest statement of
how payment is actually taken.

## Implementation approach

Gross is the only input to a money amount and net is always derived, so "net
plus VAT equals gross" is true by construction rather than by rounding luck. The
amount of record comes from the plan, never from the caller: the caller's figure
is checked against the plan and then discarded, so an operator typo cannot
become a price. Each term is its own append-only row rather than a mutable
column, enforced by SQLite triggers rather than by convention.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A venue's annual charge is derivable in code from one place: plan
   (founding|paid) to amount in cents, matching
   `contracts/commercial-terms.v2.json` (100000 / 150000), with a test that
   fails if the two drift.
2. Every venue-facing money string renders VAT-inclusive with the exact D-021
   wording "inclusive of VAT at the prevailing rate", never VAT-added and never
   a separate VAT line implying it is on top.
3. A prepaid annual term records `termStartsAt`, `termEndsAt`, `paidAt` and the
   amount actually received, and is individually identifiable for retrospective
   VAT correction (R-014, R-022).
4. The recording path refuses the standard price against a founding plan and the
   reverse, proven by test.
5. There is exactly one live writer that records a cleared payment, and it
   carries every guard. A second unguarded path does not exist.
6. The payment capture route is stated honestly: either a built path exists end
   to end, or the documented answer is that venues are invoiced out of band and
   recorded by CLI, with no surface claiming otherwise.
7. No surface produced states or implies legal or accounting approval, or that
   VAT treatment has been confirmed by Revenue (D-016, R-013).
8. D-022's couple-term rule is not reimplemented here; billing links to
   `venue-lifecycle.ts` as the single source.
9. Evidence records what was actually run, against a real SQLite engine, not
   what should work.
