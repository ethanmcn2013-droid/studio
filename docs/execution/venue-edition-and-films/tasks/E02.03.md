# E02.03 — Define precisely what the €1,000 founding-rate lock covers and state that it applies to the base annual Venue Edition agreement.

**Task ID:** E02.03 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
State the scope and the condition of the founding rate lock, in words that do not promise the service is eternal.

## Business reason
A price promise made in writing to 25 businesses, drafted without a solicitor (D-016), is the place where loose wording is most expensive.

## Intended outcome
The lock is defined as: the base annual Venue Edition agreement, held for as long as it renews continuously without lapse. Nothing more.

## Deliverables
- [x] Scope and condition in the HQ decision and the Benefits Charter, each with its boundary
- [x] `commercial-terms.v2.json` fields `lockScope`, `lockCondition`, `forbiddenWording`
- [x] A mechanical check that fails the build on permanence wording

## Scope
What the lock covers, what ends it, and the forbidden phrasings.

## Non-goals
Missed payment and reactivation mechanics (E02.04, founder). What sits outside the price (E02.06, founder).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The lock is stated as covering the base annual agreement only.
2. The condition is stated as continuous renewal without lapse.
3. "for life", "forever", "lifetime" and "in perpetuity" appear nowhere except in the documents that define the ban, and a check enforces this.
4. A lapse is stated to end the founding rate, before signature rather than at the lapse.

## Validation plan
`node scripts/check-venue-edition-contract.mjs`, plus `src/lib/commercial-terms.test.ts` "refuses permanence wording anywhere in the contract".

## Evidence required
Check output and the passing test.

## Risks
R-008. Now enforced by the gate rather than by care.

## Decisions required
None. D-009 point 3, restated in D-021.
