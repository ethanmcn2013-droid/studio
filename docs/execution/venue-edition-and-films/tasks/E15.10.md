# E15.10 — Collect venue branding, configure the account, invite venue members and approve the couple experience.

**Task ID:** E15.10

---

## Objective

Configure one venue account end to end and have the venue approve what its
couples will see, within the launch boundaries that already exist.

## Business reason

This is the first moment the venue sees the product carrying its own name. It is
also where two launch decisions become visible to the venue: branding is the
name only, and there is no venue login.

## Intended outcome

One venue is configured, its name renders correctly in a real couple workspace,
and the venue has said in writing that the couple experience is approved.

## Deliverables

- [ ] The configured venue record with its plan, ceiling and contacts.
- [ ] A rendered couple experience for that venue, shown to the venue.
- [ ] The venue's written approval.
- [ ] A written statement of what "invite venue members" means at launch.

## Scope

The venue record in the entitlements database, the fair-use ceiling, the venue
name as a couple sees it, the couple preview, and the invitation administration
Signal HQ performs on the venue's behalf.

## Non-goals

- **Venue logos and venue-written welcome messages.** D-027 point 3 fixes launch
  branding at the venue's name only. Do not collect a logo the product cannot
  use.
- **Building venue member invitation.** R-043: `members` is empty on the live
  snapshot and no venue-authenticated route exists. D-027 point 4 makes the
  portal at launch invitation administration only.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The venue record carries the correct plan and a fair-use ceiling computed
   from the annual wedding count, and no seat count is presented to the venue
   anywhere.
2. The venue's name renders in a real couple workspace exactly once, as one
   quiet line, with no logo and no venue-written message.
3. The couple preview shown to the venue is the real render, not a mockup, and
   the venue's approval is recorded in writing with a date.
4. "Invite venue members" is explained in the terms that are true: Signal HQ
   issues invitations on the venue's behalf and the venue does not log in. No
   surface offers the venue a member invitation that does nothing.
5. The HQ password is not given to the venue, in any circumstance. It opens
   Today, Money, Company, Vault, Atlas, Decks, Founders Circle and the Data Room.
6. The onboarding form does not silently apply a default allotment. Ten was
   never decided by anyone (R-016).
7. Nothing collected from the venue is data the product cannot use.
