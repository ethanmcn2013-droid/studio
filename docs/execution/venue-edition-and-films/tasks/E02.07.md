## Objective

Ratify the money mechanics of the annual Venue Edition agreement: when the term
starts, when the invoice goes out, when payment is due, when the renewal falls,
what happens if it is not paid, and what is refunded.

## Business reason

Twenty-five venues paying €1,000 up front is €25,000 of prepaid revenue in a
compressed window, taken by one person with no invoicing system, no venue
payment provider and no email sender. The terms of that exchange have never been
written down, and the first invoice cannot honestly be issued until they are.

## Intended outcome

An order form and an agreement can be drafted. A venue knows its renewal date on
the day it pays. Signal Studio knows what it owes a venue that cancels, and what
it does not.

## Implementation approach

Audit what is already ratified and what is already published, verify each in
code rather than in prose, then put three options for the term start and one
recommendation for each remaining mechanic. Defer the notice and grace numbers
to E02.04 rather than answering them twice.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Annual prepayment is confirmed as ratified, with its decision cited, and is
   not re-argued.
2. The term start basis is presented as at least two real options with the cost
   of each, and one recommendation.
3. Invoice timing and payment terms are proposed as specific numbers of days,
   and each is either consistent with something already published to venues or
   the inconsistency is stated.
4. The conflict between `src/app/terms/page.tsx` and the Venue Edition reality is
   recorded with file and line references, and its four separate claims are
   itemised rather than summarised.
5. The brief states, with the arithmetic shown, that no renewal falls due before
   roughly September 2027, and draws the conclusion that the wording is urgent
   and the machinery is not.
6. The refund position covers both directions: what a venue that leaves gets,
   and what a venue gets if Signal Studio stops.
7. The Revenue MyEnquiries submission is named as unfiled, with the consequence
   of filing it late stated, and no confirmed VAT position is claimed anywhere.
8. All proposed venue-facing wording passes brand voice: no em dashes, no
   exclamation marks, sentence case, plain English, no permanence terms.
9. Nothing states or implies legal approval, solicitor review or accountant
   verification (D-016).

## Risks

- **R-018.** If receipt of foreign B2B services already makes Signal Studio an
  accountable person, VAT applies from the first €1,000 rather than at a
  threshold. The submission that answers it is free and unfiled.
- **R-022, accepted.** VAT-inclusive pricing under a permanent lock. €1,000
  inclusive nets €813.01 at 23%.
- **R-014.** The tax point on an annual prepayment may be receipt, which would
  put the whole cohort's VAT into one period.
- **R-013.** Annual prepaid B2B terms drafted with no solicitor. The refund
  direction that protects the venue is the cheapest available mitigation while
  nothing has been sold.
