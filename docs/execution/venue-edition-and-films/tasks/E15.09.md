# E15.09 — Complete signed-venue handoff from sales into onboarding.

---

## Objective

Define and run the handoff that turns a signed agreement into a configured
account, so nothing said during the sale is lost and nothing promised is missed.

## Business reason

R-024 records the gap plainly: the six release gates certify that the product
works and that the sale can be made, and nothing certifies that what was sold can
be delivered. This is that certification, one venue at a time.

## Intended outcome

A written handoff record exists per venue carrying what was promised, what was
agreed outside the standard terms, the venue's own words about what it wants,
and the configuration decisions that follow.

## Deliverables

- [ ] A one-page handoff template, filled per venue.
- [ ] The first completed handoff, run as a rehearsal before any real venue.
- [ ] A named list of anything promised in the sale that the product cannot do.

## Scope

The point between signature and configuration: the agreement, the founding
schedule, the annual wedding count collected after signature, the venue's
contacts, and anything said in the room.

## Non-goals

- Building venue-specific product. D-014 point 2: requests are logged and shape
  the roadmap, nothing is built for one venue.
- Collecting the annual wedding count as a contract term. It is an onboarding
  field, it sets the issuance ceiling, it never sets the price and it never
  changes at renewal (D-020 point 4).

## Acceptance criteria

1. A handoff record exists per signed venue carrying the signature date, the
   cleared-payment date, the plan, the founding number if assigned, the named
   contacts, and the annual wedding count.
2. Anything promised during the sale outside the standard offer is written down
   verbatim and either honoured or corrected with the venue before configuration.
3. The venue is told, unprompted, the survival sentence: any workspace already
   redeemed keeps its full term plus Keepsake whatever happens to the licence,
   and only new issuance stops (D-020 point 2).
4. The release rule is stated: if a couple cancels or moves venue, the venue can
   release the workspace, the couple keeps their content, and the venue's name
   and branding come off within 24 hours (D-020 point 3).
5. The annual wedding count is recorded with the exact framing in D-020 point 4
   and used only to set the fair-use ceiling.
6. Nothing in the handoff creates a commitment that is not in the agreement.
7. The handoff is rehearsed once end to end before the first real venue (R-024).

## Risks

- **R-024** — an improvised first onboarding.
- **R-020** — the venue starts describing the product the day it signs.
- **R-021** — a very large venue is a permanent loss at the founding rate,
  accepted by the founder. No eligibility screen by volume will be built.

## Decisions required

1. Does the handoff happen on the call, or as a written form the venue returns?
   A form the venue never returns blocks configuration.
