## Objective

Produce the signed go/no-go decision for contacting Cohort 1, taken against the
six release gates as they actually stand, with the gap between "must be true"
and "is true" written down rather than assumed.

## Business reason

Every other E15 task depends on this one. Cohort 1 is the first and only chance
to make a first impression on 25 named Greater Limerick venues. A venue
contacted before the product can deliver what the proposal says is a venue lost
twice: once on the sale and once on the reference.

## Intended outcome

A dated go/no-go record exists that states, per gate, which exit criteria are
met, which are not, and which are waived by the founder in writing. The decision
is go, no-go, or conditional-go with the conditions named and dated. Nothing in
it says "should" or "expected to".

## Deliverables

- [ ] A gate-by-gate table covering all 75 exit criteria with a met / not met /
      waived verdict and an evidence path for every "met".
- [ ] A named list of the release-blocking gaps, each with an owner and a date.
- [ ] The founder's decision, recorded with a date and a note that says what was
      judged, not only that approval happened.
- [ ] Any waiver written as a waiver, naming the criterion it waives and the
      exposure it accepts.

## Scope

The six gates in `PROJECT_STATE.json` (`commercial`, `legal`, `product`, `data`,
`creative`, `sales_readiness`), their 75 exit criteria, the open RAID entries
that touch release, and the state of the deployed production surfaces.

## Non-goals

- Passing any gate. Gate passes are founder-only (`project-control.mjs gate`).
- Fixing what the review finds. Findings become tasks; this task reports.
- Reopening D-009, D-020, D-021, D-022 or D-026. Ratified positions are inputs.
- Any statement about legal approval or GDPR compliance (D-016).

## Implementation approach

Run the script in the evidence document top to bottom. It is ordered so the
cheap machine checks come first and the founder judgement calls come last, and
so a hard stop is reached before any time is spent on the later gates.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every one of the 75 gate exit criteria carries a verdict of met, not met, or
   waived. No criterion is left blank and none is marked met without a
   repository path or a command output that a second person could re-run.
2. The review names, explicitly, each of the following as its current state
   rather than leaving it implied: R-031 (`/p` indexing on wedding workspaces),
   R-043 (no venue-authenticated route), the absence of any Keepsake product
   surface, the Blob store having never been exercised in production, and the
   fact that no renewal falls due before 1 September.
3. The review distinguishes what is true in the integration branches from what
   is true on the deployed production surfaces, and states which of the two the
   decision is being taken against.
4. Every gate marked passed has a founder waiver recorded for each unmet
   criterion under it, and each waiver names the exposure it accepts.
5. The decision is recorded as go, no-go, or conditional-go, dated, with the
   conditions listed and owned if conditional.
6. No line of the review states or implies legal approval, solicitor review,
   accountant verification or unqualified GDPR compliance (D-016).
7. The review states the film position honestly against the creative gate rather
   than deferring it, given E13 is 4/18 and E14 is 1/18 with 28 days remaining.

## Validation plan

  review reports.
- Every evidence path cited in the review resolves to a file that exists.
- Every command quoted in the review can be re-run and produces the quoted
  result. The 2026-08-04 run is recorded in the evidence document.

## Evidence required

## Risks

- **R-024** — nothing in the six gates certifies that what was sold can be
  delivered. E15.09 to E15.11 are the delivery path and none is in a gate.
- **R-013 / D-016** — the legal gate cannot be satisfied as originally written.
  CR-001 already rewrote it; the review must use the rewritten criteria.
- The largest risk to this task is a gate passed on the strength of a green test
  suite. Tests being green is criterion evidence, never a gate pass.

## Decisions required

1. Is the decision taken against the integration branches or against deployed
   production? They differ materially today.
2. What is the creative gate's honest disposition with the films at 22% and 6%?
3. Do the four unresolved privacy findings (R-031, R-032, R-033, R-029) block
   Cohort 1, or are they waived with the exposure accepted in writing?
