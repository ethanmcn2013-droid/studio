# E04.09 — Define wedding-date metadata, event-date changes and which dates can appear in the Venue Portal.

**Task ID:** E04.09
**Epic:** E04 — Venue Edition product architecture and workspace lifecycle
**Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code

---

## Objective

Define where the wedding date lives, how a change to it behaves, and which dates a venue may see.

## Business reason

The wedding date now drives the access term after D-022, and D-011 point 1 governs what the venue sees. Those two facts meet on one field and nobody has written down how.

## Intended outcome

The date's storage, its projection into the entitlements store, its recompute behaviour and its portal visibility are one statement, and the unreconciled gate question is put to the founder.

## Deliverables

- [ ] studio/docs/architecture/ADR-008-venue-edition-lifecycle.md section 9
- [ ] studio/src/lib/entitlements-db/schema.ts entitlements.wedding_date, with its projection status documented on the column
- [ ] The D-011 versus consent-gate tension recorded as an open question with a recommendation

## Scope

Storage, projection, recompute-on-change behaviour, and the portal visibility rule.

## Non-goals

Building the portal date display. Building a date-change UI, which does not exist today and is E05 scope.

## Dependencies

| Dependency | State | Waived? |
|---|---|---|
| R-015 access-term fix | shipped in this package | no |

## Inputs

D-011 point 1, D-022 points 1 and 3, app/src/server/db/schema.ts workspaces.primary_date, studio/src/lib/entitlements-db/sponsorship-policy.ts SPONSOR_CONSENT_FIELDS.

## Implementation approach

The couple owns the date in their workspace; the entitlements store holds a projection so the term can be evaluated without reaching into product content. Record that D-011's per-venue redemption rule and the consent-grant model are two different gates and that only the founder can say which governs.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The source of truth for the wedding date is named, and the projection is named as a projection.
2. Date changes recompute the term and can only extend it, matching D-022 point 3.
3. D-011 point 1 is restated: dates visible only where the couple redeemed that venue's code, and changes never shown.
4. The tension between D-011's rule and the consent-grant model is recorded, unreconciled, with a recommendation.
5. There is no date-change UI today and the section says so.

## Risks

Projecting a personal date into a second store is a second place it can leak from. Mitigated by keeping it out of SPONSOR_DEFAULT_FIELDS.

## Decisions required

Which gate governs venue-visible wedding dates: D-011's per-venue redemption rule, the consent-grant model, or both.
