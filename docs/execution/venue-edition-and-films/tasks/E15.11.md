# E15.11 — Train the venue and support it through its first real couple invitation and redemption.

**Task ID:** E15.11

**Executor:** founder

---

## Objective

Take one venue through issuing its first real couple invitation, watch that
couple redeem it, and record what the venue found hard.

## Business reason

The project completion condition requires venues "configured, onboarded and
capable of issuing functioning couple invitations". This is where capability is
proven rather than assumed. It is also where a coordinator, not the owner,
starts describing the product to couples.

## Intended outcome

One venue has issued a real invitation, one real couple has redeemed it, and the
friction in between is written down in the venue's words.

## Deliverables

- [ ] The training material the venue actually receives.
- [ ] The record of the first real invitation and its redemption.
- [ ] A friction list in the venue's words.
- [ ] The approved copy pack and prohibited-claims sheet, handed over.

## Scope

How a venue asks for an invitation, how Signal HQ issues it, what the couple
receives, and what the venue is told to say.

## Non-goals

- Training the venue on a portal it cannot log into.
- Any promise about the term other than the ratified one.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The venue can request an invitation through a route it can actually use, and
   the request reaches Signal HQ reliably.
2. The invitation is issued and the couple redeems it, in production, for real.
3. The couple's access term written to the database matches D-022 for that
   couple's wedding date. Read the database, not the screen.
4. The venue receives the approved copy pack and a one-page prohibited-claims
   sheet before it speaks to a couple: no "forever", no "guaranteed", no
   permanence or storage promise, no "GDPR compliant", no claim that legal
   review was obtained (R-020).
5. The venue is told what to say when a couple asks how long they keep it, and
   that answer matches the agreement.
6. Every point of friction is recorded in the venue's words, including the ones
   that are the venue's own confusion rather than a product defect.
7. Support requests arising in the first week are logged with their resolution.

## Risks

- **R-020** — this is where the reseller exposure becomes real. A coordinator
  saying "yours forever" at a show-around is a commercial practice under the
  Consumer Protection Act 2007 and it folds into the venue's own contract with
  the couple.
- **R-015** — if the app integration branch is not deployed, the term the couple
  actually receives is the flat 548 days.

## Decisions required

1. Who at the venue is trained? The owner signs; the coordinator speaks to
   couples. Training the signer only is training the wrong person.
