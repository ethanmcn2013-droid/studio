## Objective

Produce the first drafts of the data protection instrument between Signal Studio
and a founding venue, with its security schedule and its recipient schedule, so a
venue can be sent the paperwork before it signs.

## Business reason

`studio/src/app/venues/questions/page.tsx` is live and already tells a venue: "The
paperwork is being written now and you will get all of it before you sign." Its
own source comment at lines 26 to 27 names the artifacts the full answer depends
on: **E03.04 (DPA, security schedule)** and E03.05 (couple privacy notice). So
this task sits on the critical path to first contact even though it is marked
non-release-blocking. A venue's own adviser reading a security schedule is one of
the few moments where a wrong sentence is expensive after the fact.

**A discrepancy worth recording once.** The live page and the backlog both put the
security schedule in E03.04. `evidence/E09.10-copy-hierarchy.md` Answer 1 and
Answer 4 put it in E03.05 and put the couple privacy notice in E03.04. Two
documents assign one deliverable to two owners. E03.04 is treated as the owner
here, on the strength of the backlog title and the live page, and E09.10 needs
correcting rather than being left to diverge.

## Intended outcome

Three documents exist that a venue could actually be sent, every claim in them is
traceable to a file in the repository, and every question they cannot answer is
written down as a question rather than answered quietly.

## Implementation approach

Read the role map in full first. Enumerate every recipient from evidence rather
than from memory: both dependency manifests, every `process.env` reference in both
source trees, then the call site for each candidate. Write only what a reader can
check. Where the role map records a question as open, take the safer reading, name
it, and give the reason.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each of the three documents opens with a visible DRAFT status block naming what
   it is, what it is not, and that no solicitor has reviewed it, carries the date,
   and reproduces the E09.10 Answer 2 sentence verbatim: "No solicitor has reviewed
   these documents, and no accountant has verified the financial or VAT position."
2. The instrument is named and justified on the record. Processor standard
   contractual clauses (2021/915) are ruled out with the role map §1 reasoning
   shown, and the chosen shape is an Article 26 arrangement covering invitation and
   redemption plus a controller-to-controller arrangement covering everything else.
3. The Article 26 boundary is drafted as the three clauses of role map §7 and never
   as the single sentence, and Article 26(3) is stated so that neither "the venue
   holds the relationship" nor "Signal Studio runs the software" is available as a
   defence.
4. Every recipient in Schedule 2 is traceable to a dependency-manifest entry, an
   environment-variable reference or a named call site, and no recipient is named
   that was not verified that way.
5. Vendor roles are recorded individually per role map §9, including Stripe as both
   processor and independent controller with an Article 13 disclosure named for the
   controller leg, and the word "sub-processor" appears nowhere in any of the three
   documents.
6. No region, country or transfer claim is made for any vendor unverified in this
   programme. The `fra1` attachment store is stated with its outstanding production
   redeploy and its orphaned empty United States store, and every other region,
   transfer mechanism and agreement execution date is marked unverified.
7. The security schedule describes only controls readable in the repository, each
   named by file, and carries a "what is not built" section naming at least: no
   scheduled backups, no provisioned error monitoring, no alerting, an audit log
   covering two event kinds, no malware scanning, no data-protection footer or
   removal route on any published surface, no `security.txt` and no `security@`
   address.
8. The absence of row-level security on Turso is stated plainly with the
   application-level control named and described as materially weaker, and no
   sentence in any of the three documents says otherwise.
9. Article 9 is addressed at the template-instruction mechanism established by role
   map §13 Finding 3, not at a dietary field, no Article 9(2) condition is claimed,
   and the documents do not design a structured dietary field.
10. Breach responsibilities cite `evidence/E08.12-incident-response-runbook.md`
    rather than restating it, and correct role map §10.5's statement that no
    procedure exists.
11. Every point the drafts cannot settle is a visibly marked decision point drafted
    to the safer reading, with the reading named and its reason given, and every
    point a solicitor must settle appears as a question.
