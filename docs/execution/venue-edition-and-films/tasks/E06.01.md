## Objective

Write, field by field, what may cross from a couple's private workspace onto a
public page, for both published products that exist, and make the never-publish
half an enforced allowlist rather than a paragraph.

## Business reason

Twenty-five venues are being asked for EUR 1,000 a year, and the thing being
sold is a trust layer around other people's weddings. E12.07 has to state
exactly what is public and what is not. It cannot state it until this document
exists, and it must not state anything the code does not enforce. Every other
E06 task inherits the answer: E06.02 adds states to this model, E06.03 adds a
byte to it, E06.06 removes fields from it.

## Intended outcome

There is one document naming every value that can reach a public surface, for
both `/p` and `/s`, each claim carrying the line that enforces it. The single
free-text identity string on the Timeline boundary, `ownerDisplayLabel`, has a
deliberate decision recorded and a rule in code. Adding a new field to either
product excludes it from publication by default.

## Implementation approach

Audit first. The Timeline boundary is an allowlist with a denylist layered
under it and it is the right design, so it was extended rather than replaced.
The Tasks boundary is a hand-written field-by-field projection with an exact-key
test, so it was recorded as passing rather than rewritten.

One gap was genuine: `ownerDisplayLabel` is owner-authored free text that
renders above the couple's names on the public page, and the only rule on it was
length 80 and no `@`. A name shape was added.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A written content model names, field by field, every value that may cross to
   a public surface, for **both** published products: the Timeline audience DTO
   (`AudienceTimelineDto`) and the Tasks published projection (`PublicTask`).
2. The never-publish list is enforced in code, not only in prose. Each claim
   cites the enforcing line — `FORBIDDEN_KEYS` and `exactKeys` for Timeline, the
   field-by-field allowlist in `toPublicTask` for Tasks — and the document
   states that `validateAudienceTimelineDto` rebuilds the DTO rather than
   spreading it.
3. `ownerDisplayLabel` is dealt with explicitly: what it may contain, whether
   owner identity belongs in the published projection at all with the reasoning
   recorded either way, and a rule that stops a couple publishing a home
   address, phone number, Eircode or email in it. The rule runs at the
   projection layer, not only at the form, and is proved by a test.
4. R-017 is addressed by mechanism, not by clause: the model states that
   dietary, allergy, accessibility and any Article 9 value can never reach a
   public surface, and names the enforcing mechanism on each product.
5. The model names which of the two products is the couple's artifact under
   D-033, or records the remaining ambiguity as an open founder question. It
   must not assume.
6. A new field added to either product is excluded from publication by default,
   and a test fails if that stops being true.
7. No sentence in the document states or implies legal approval, solicitor
   review or GDPR compliance (D-016), and every capability not built is stated
   as not built.
