# E02.05 — Define how the founding rate behaves after a venue sale, company acquisition, operator change, rebrand, merger or transfer.

**Task ID:** E02.05 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
State what happens to the founding rate when the venue changes hands.

## Business reason
Irish wedding venues change ownership. A rate promise with no change-of-control rule is either an argument or an accidental gift of the founding rate to a hotel group.

## Intended outcome
One rule: the rate follows the property, not the company. It survives a sale, rebrand and ownership change while the agreement runs continuously. It does not travel to other properties a new owner already holds.

## Deliverables
- [x] The rule in the HQ decision, the Benefits Charter and the mechanics document
- [x] `commercial-terms.v2.json`: `lockFollows: "property"`, `lockTransfersToNewProperties: false`
- [x] Edge cases decided in `FOUNDING_25_PROGRAMME_MECHANICS.md` §5

## Scope
Sale, acquisition, operator change, rebrand, merger, transfer, and multi-property owners.

## Non-goals
The agreement clause itself (WP-04 drafts it from this rule).

## Acceptance criteria
1. The rate is stated to follow the property, not the corporate entity.
2. It survives rebrand and ownership change while the agreement renews continuously.
3. It is stated not to extend to additional properties a new owner brings.
4. A group with several qualifying venues is covered: one place per property.
