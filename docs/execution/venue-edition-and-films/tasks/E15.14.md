# E15.14 — Secure permissioned venue evidence, couple evidence, referrals and public map/name approval.

**Task ID:** E15.14

**Status:** backlog
**Executor:** founder

---

## Objective

Obtain and record specific, revocable permission before any venue name, venue
quote, couple material or referral is used publicly.

## Business reason

The programme's whole trust story rests on asking rather than assuming. D-010
point 1 asks venues for logo and name usage in the founding programme and on the
map, and asks **couples for nothing by default**. A single unpermissioned use
undoes the position that makes the offer credible.

## Intended outcome

Every public use of a name, a quote or an image traces to a recorded, specific,
dated permission that the giver could revoke.

## Deliverables

- [ ] A permission register: who gave what, for what use, on what date, and how
      it is revoked.
- [ ] The venue name and map approvals for every published venue.
- [ ] Any couple permission, obtained separately and specifically.
- [ ] The referral approach, with the venue's agreement recorded.

## Scope

Venue names on the public map and in the founding programme, venue quotes,
couple case-study material, and referrals.

## Non-goals

- Any default couple permission. **Couples are asked for nothing by default**
  (D-010 point 1). Case-study use is a separate, specific, opt-in permission.
- Bundling permission into the agreement as a blanket grant.
- Face detection, auto-tagging or face grouping. A standing no.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A permission register exists with one row per permission: giver, exact use,
   date, expiry or revocation route, and the artefact where it appears.
2. No venue name appears on the public map or in any founding-programme surface
   without a row in the register.
3. No couple material of any kind is used publicly without a specific, separate,
   dated opt-in from that couple. A general consent in an account flow does not
   satisfy this.
4. Permission is specific to a use, not general. "Marketing" is not a use.
5. Every permission has a stated revocation route the giver can actually use,
   and revocation removes the material within a stated period.
6. A referral request is made only where the venue has agreed to be asked.
7. No published material implies a venue's logo or its own words appear in a
   couple's workspace (D-027 point 3).
8. Nothing published states or implies legal approval or GDPR compliance.

## Risks

- **R-017** — a couple's workspace foreseeably contains special-category data
  about guests who consented to nothing. Nothing from a workspace goes public
  without a specific decision about the third parties named in it.
- **R-031** — until the noindex fix is deployed, a published wedding workspace is
  offered to search engines regardless of any permission register.
- The permission most likely to be taken casually is the venue quote, because a
  venue says something generous on a call and it ends up on a page.

## Decisions required

1. Is any couple material used publicly at all in year one? The safest answer is
   no, and it costs the programme very little.
