**Task ID:** E02.12 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review

## Objective
Record the ratified entitlement model in the commercial contract, and reconcile the financial model to the founding rate.

## Business reason
The entitlement model drives the unit economics of €1,000. It was the last open baseline decision (FD-03), closed by D-020.

## Intended outcome
The machine contract states unlimited entitlement with no number anywhere, fair use that notifies rather than blocks, and the survival and release rules. The financial model prices founding at €1,000.

## Deliverables
- [x] `commercial-terms.v2.json`: `activationAllowance: "unlimited"`, `fairUseBehaviour: "notify_never_block"`, `redeemedWorkspaceSurvivesLicenceEnd: true`, 24-hour branding removal, wedding count collected after signature
- [x] `venue_activation_allowance` and `venue_calendar_month_semantics` moved out of `unresolved`, each with a stated basis
- [x] `src/lib/hq/financial-model.ts` prices founding at €1,000, with before and after model output captured
- [x] The prior decision evidence retained at `evidence/E02.12-entitlement-choice.md`

## Scope
The commercial record and the financial model.

## NOT IN SCOPE, and this task is not finished without it
The code representation of "unlimited" is **R-016, and it belongs to WP-01**: the mint refuses a null allotment, the HQ onboarding form defaults to 10, and the venue portal shows a headroom warning to a venue sold "no seats". Until that lands, the contract states an entitlement the product cannot issue. The commercial half is done here. The product half is tracked on WP-01 and should be confirmed before approval means anything.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The contract states unlimited entitlement with no numeric allowance anywhere in the commercial terms.
2. Fair use is recorded as notify-never-block.
3. The survival sentence and the release rule are both present in the contract.
4. The financial model prices founding at €1,000, and the revenue delta is measured rather than asserted.
5. The R-016 code dependency is stated on the face of this task rather than left implicit.

## Validation plan
`commercial-terms.test.ts` "states entitlement as unlimited and never as a count"; the financial model run with the founding price at €1,500 and at €1,000, with both outputs recorded.

## Evidence required
Test output, model output both ways, and the E02.12 decision evidence.

## Risks
R-021 (no volume screen) and R-022 (VAT-inclusive under a permanent lock) are accepted by founder decision. R-016 is open and blocks the product side.

## Decisions required
Whether the founding ramp in the financial model should schedule 25 venues rather than the 10 it currently schedules. Raised in the WP-10 packet and deliberately not changed, because the model's own contract says every figure in it is a founder-owned assumption.
