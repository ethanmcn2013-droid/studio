# E15.15 — Release Cohorts 2, 3, 4 and reserve accounts as necessary until 25 venues are signed and paid.

**Task ID:** E15.15
**Epic:** E15 — Release, venue onboarding and completion of the Founding 25
**Status:** backlog
**Executor:** founder
**Approver:** Ethan McNamara
**Spec written:** 2026-08-04

---

## Objective

Release further cohorts sequentially until twenty-five venues have signed and
paid, without padding a cohort from outside the ratified geographic ring.

## Business reason

M6, the project completion condition, is twenty-five signed and paid venues.
Getting there is arithmetic against a finite pool, and the pool is smaller than
the target implies.

## Intended outcome

Cohorts are released one at a time, each one built to the same three rules, and
the count of signed and paid venues is tracked honestly against 25.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Each released cohort, dated, frozen at release.
- [ ] The running count of signed and paid venues.
- [ ] An honest report whenever a cohort is short.
- [ ] A decision record if the pool runs out before 25.

## Scope

Cohorts 2 onward, the reserve accounts held on the unconfirmed-trading rule, and
the release cadence.

## Non-goals

- Inventing a cohort. `venue-universe/05-COHORTS.md` states the rule: a short
  cohort reports as short, and a cohort padded from outside the ring would
  quietly overturn D-012.
- Changing the geographic boundary. That is change control (`/venue-change`).

## Acceptance criteria

1. Each cohort is built by `venue-rank.mjs` under the same three rules: one
   property per operator per cohort, nothing unconfirmed in a cohort, and a short
   cohort reported as short.
2. No cohort contains an account from outside the ratified ring.
3. The running count of signed and paid venues is derived from the entitlements
   database, not from a spreadsheet, and matches `sponsors.founding_number`
   assignments.
4. Held reserve accounts are verified before release rather than assumed, and
   the verification result is recorded either way.
5. When the pool inside the ring is exhausted before 25 signatures, that is
   reported as a fact with the number reached, and the next move is a founder
   decision under change control rather than a quiet boundary extension.
6. No venue is contacted twice across cohorts.
7. Every cohort release is preceded by a check that the ratified position and
   the live surfaces still agree.

## Risks

- **The pool is the risk.** `venue-universe/counts-only.md` records 219 venues
  researched, 70 inside the 45-minute ring, **50 inside the ring and eligible**,
  and 28 near the boundary needing confirmation. `05-COHORTS.md` states that
  Cohort 1 fills to 25, Cohort 2 comes up short, and **Cohorts 3 and 4 do not
  exist inside the ratified ring.** Twenty-five signed venues from 50 eligible
  accounts requires a 50% conversion rate on cold founder-led outreach.
- **R-006** — capacity, compounded by onboarding running in parallel with sales.

## Decisions required

1. What happens when the ring is exhausted? Extending the boundary, lowering the
   count, or accepting a longer timeline are the three options and each is change
   control.
2. Is the 50% implied conversion rate acknowledged in the plan, or is the plan
   still written as though 25 is a scheduling question?
