# E01.10 — Define six formal release gates: commercial, legal, product, data, creative and sales-readiness.

**Task ID:** E01.10 · **Epic:** E01 · **Status:** in_progress
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03

---

## Objective

Six gates whose exit criteria could actually fail them, each criterion checkable
by someone who was not in the room.

## Business reason

The go/no-go cannot pass unless every gate passes. If a gate's criteria are
headlines, the go/no-go is a formality and 1 September becomes a date rather than
a decision. "Couple journey, shared Timeline and Venue Portal complete to the
agreed standard" is not a criterion. It is a wish with a checkbox.

## Intended outcome

Every gate carries eight or more testable criteria naming their evidence, and the
gaps in the gate system itself are on record.

## Deliverables

- [x] Twelve or thirteen exit criteria for each of commercial, product, data, creative and sales readiness
- [x] The legal gate untouched: CR-001's twelve stand
- [x] A `gate <id> criteria <file.json>` route so criteria enter through the tool
- [x] Tests: at least eight criteria per gate, each over 40 characters, every gate owned
- [x] `CR-002` for the two epics no gate covers

## Scope

The six gates, their exit criteria, their owners, and the coverage of the epic
set by the gate set.

## Non-goals

- **Not the legal gate's criteria.** CR-001 rewrote them, D-023 approved them, and reopening them is change control.
- Not passing, waiving or moving any gate. Every gate stays `not_started`.
- Not changing any gate's supporting epics. That is a launch-gate change under `PROJECT.md` §20 and is raised as CR-002.

## Dependencies

None recorded.

## Inputs

- `PROJECT.md` §13 — the six gates as chartered
- `evidence/change-requests/CR-001.md` — the twelve legal criteria, the model for the rest
- `DECISIONS.md` D-008 to D-024 — every ratified constraint the criteria must respect
- `RAID.md` — every risk that should be caught by a gate
- `REPORTING.md` §4 — the gate state model
- The real verification tooling in `studio`, `app` and `signal-motion`, so named evidence is checkable rather than aspirational

## Implementation approach

Write criteria against what can actually be verified, naming the script, test or
artifact that satisfies each. Respect the ratified constraints: zero budget, no
solicitor, generated imagery, one surface for Account and Portal, unlimited
entitlement, VAT-inclusive pricing, cohorts of 25, the access-term grace rule.

The creative gate is owned by the motion lane, so every one of its criteria must
be verifiable by the Claude lane from artifacts rather than from a report. That
constraint produced its two strongest criteria: re-hashing every render manifest,
and the Claude lane executing the rerender procedure once, unaided.

Then map the six gates against the fifteen epics and see what falls through.

## Acceptance criteria

1. All six gates have exit criteria, an owner and a defined state model.
2. Each of the five non-legal gates carries at least eight criteria, and each criterion is specific enough that someone who was not in the room could check it.
3. Every criterion names the evidence that would satisfy it, and where it maps to a task, names the task ID.
4. No criterion can be satisfied by assertion. "Complete to the agreed standard" does not appear.
5. Every criterion is consistent with the ratified constraints and none assumes spend, professional review, or capability the decisions removed.
6. The legal gate's twelve criteria from CR-001 are unchanged.
7. The creative gate's criteria are verifiable by the lane that did not produce the work.
8. A test asserts the minimum criteria count and length per gate, so a gate cannot quietly revert to headlines.
9. Every epic is either covered by a gate or declared as deliberately uncovered with a change request open against it.
10. Criteria are recorded through the tool, under the lock and the validator, not by hand-editing canonical state.
11. Criteria of a passed or waived gate cannot be rewritten.
12. Risks on the register that no gate criterion covered are identified, and each is either folded into a gate that already owns the epic, or raised as a change request.

## Risks

Twelve criteria per gate is a lot to satisfy in 29 days, and that is the point:
the criteria describe what "ready" means. If they cannot be met, the honest
outcome is a documented founder waiver against a named criterion, not a gate that
was easy to pass.

R-025 is now on record: all six gates could pass with release-blocking tasks
still in Backlog, because a gate asks whether a thing is true and not whether
every task meant to make it true is finished. Its fix is a precondition on
E15.01, which is yours.

## Decisions required

is that nothing certifies that what was sold can be delivered.
