## Objective

Keep the 1 September release milestone and the Founding 25 completion condition
as two separate, separately tracked milestones that cannot be reported as one.

## Business reason

R-001 is 120 critical-path tasks against a month. That risk is survivable only
because a slip in release scope is not a slip in the business outcome. The moment
the two milestones blur, either the date forces bad product decisions or a missed
date reads as a failed project. Both are avoidable by keeping them apart.

## Intended outcome

M5 and M6 exist independently, with their own exit criteria, and the meaning of
"release" is written down rather than assumed.

## Deliverables

- [x] M5 and M6 as distinct milestones with distinct exit criteria
- [x] A written definition of what release means, ratified
- [x] `BRIEF.md` carrying the distinction in one line a reader cannot miss
- [x] An assessment of whether any report blends them

## Scope

The milestone structure, the definition of release, and the reporting separation.

## Non-goals

- Not the go/no-go review itself. That is E15.01.
- Not the release gates. That is E01.10.
- Not the freeze dates. That is E01.11.

## Implementation approach

Assess. M5 and M6 were created at import and D-015 Q1 ratified the meaning of
release. Verify that both milestones exist with separate exit criteria, that
nothing computes a single blended completion figure across them, and that the
distinction is legible to a reader who only reads the brief.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two distinct milestones exist: M5, release 2026-09-01, and M6, Founding 25 complete, with separate exit criteria.
2. What "release" means is written down and ratified by a founder decision, not inferred.
3. M6 carries no target date, because the completion condition is outcome-driven and dating it would be fabricated precision.
4. No generated report presents a single figure that blends release readiness with Founding 25 completion.
5. The separation appears in `BRIEF.md` in plain English.
6. The dependency between this project's release date and the company launch gate is recorded, so the two 1 September dates are not treated as independent.

## Validation plan

- Read generated `STATUS.md` and confirm release readiness is reported gate by gate and the Founding 25 tracker is reported separately
- Confirm D-015 Q1 states the meaning of release, and D-007 records the shared date
