# E02.02 — Ratify the exact price-reduction wording: “€500 founding saving,” “one-third founding rate” or “33.3% founding discount.”

**Task ID:** E02.02 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
Fix one phrasing for the founding reduction and use it everywhere.

## Business reason
Three phrasings for one number is how a venue ends up quoting a different offer back at you. A venue owner does arithmetic in euro, not in percentages.

## Intended outcome
Every surface says the same sentence: **€500 a year less, for as long as you stay.**

## Deliverables
- [x] The wording recorded in the HQ decision and in `commercial-terms.v2.json` as `founding.publicWording`
- [x] Applied on `/venues`, in `comparison-pages.ts`, the outreach set and the asset library

## Scope
Wording only.

## Non-goals
The price itself (E02.01). Contract language (WP-04).

## Acceptance criteria
1. "€500 a year less, for as long as you stay" is the published phrasing, recorded in the machine contract.
2. No commercial surface expresses the reduction as a percentage or a fraction.
3. No surface pairs the reduction with permanence wording.

## Validation plan
`node scripts/check-venue-edition-contract.mjs`; grep for "one-third", "33.3", "33%" across commercial sources.

## Evidence required
The contract field, the check output, and the grep result.

## Risks
None beyond R-008, now mechanically enforced.

## Decisions required
None. D-009 point 2 chose the euro phrasing.
