## Objective

Establish what the shipped Notes product already gives a sponsored couple, close
the honest gaps in voice capture, and make Notes speak in wedding language when
the workspace is a wedding.

## Business reason

Notes is where a couple's private planning actually starts: the thought in the
car after a venue visit, the number a parent mentioned on the phone. It is also
the surface with the highest privacy exposure per word written, because it is
unstructured and holds other people's names. A venue is being sold "a private
planning workspace for every couple you book". If Notes reads like a generic
productivity tool, or if dictation quietly sends a couple's private
conversation to a third party without telling them, the promise the Founding 25
are buying is not the promise being delivered.

## Intended outcome

- Voice capture is documented as what it actually is, not assumed absent.
- A couple is told, before they speak, that dictation sends their words off the
  device, and what the alternative is.
- A browser with no speech engine explains itself instead of silently hiding a
  control.
- Notes knows when it is a wedding notebook and says so in its own words.
- None of this changes what a venue can see, which stays nothing.

## Implementation approach

Notes was entirely domain-blind: no file in `src/modules/notes/**` read
`activeDomain`, `contextType` or any wedding signal. The redemption path sets
`workspaces.active_domain = 'wedding'` (`src/server/actions/comp.ts:263`) and
nothing else, so `contextType` stays `'project'` and is not the signal.

The workspace-personalization route already returns `activeDomain`, and Notes
already has a client for it (`fetchTasksPersonalization`) with zero callers.
Declaring the field on the existing type and adding one narrow accessor is the
whole of the wiring. No new route, no new field, no migration.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The audit states whether voice capture exists in the shipped build, with
   file and line, and does not claim a capability that is absent.
2. Before a couple's first dictation, the surface states plainly that the
   spoken words leave the device, names what receives them, and says typing
   does not. The statement is standing, not a dismissible one-time dialog.
3. Each dictation failure (permission refused, nothing heard, engine failure,
   no engine at all) produces a distinct plain-English message.
4. A browser with no speech engine explains that in words, rather than the
   control silently disappearing.
5. Notes selects a wedding register from data already crossing the Tasks
   boundary, with no new API field, no new route and no migration.
6. In the wedding register the capture placeholder, the privacy line and the
   empty state read in wedding-planning language; every other account is
   unchanged.
7. The wedding signal is presentation only. It grants no access, hides no
   control and scopes no query.
8. Every string added or changed passes the brand-voice mechanical lint with
   zero HARD findings.
9. Nothing this task changes alters what a venue can see. The venue still sees
   no private planning content.
10. `pnpm typecheck` clean and `pnpm test` green, with the new guards inside
    the CI chain rather than beside it.
11. **NOT MET, and recorded rather than defined away.** The title also says
    "high-signal structured output". Notes produces free text plus the
    hand-picked wording that crosses to Tasks. There is no wedding-shaped
    structure (supplier, cost, date, decision) and this task did not invent
    one, because R-017 is open and the role map (E03.01) has not settled what
    an unstructured planning store may hold about third parties. Building a
    structured capture schema before that decision would be the exact mistake
    R-017 describes. Needs a founder call before it can be scoped.

## Risks

- **New, and it is the reason this task matters.** Dictation sends audio to the
  browser maker's speech service. That is a third-party processor of a couple's
  private planning speech, and until this task nothing in the product, the
  privacy page or the docs said so. Raised as a new RAID candidate below.
- The wedding register depends on a cross-product HTTP call. It fails to
  generic on any error, so the failure mode is bland copy, never a broken
  notebook.
- `src/modules/notes/app/notebook/hooks.ts` exports a broken, unused
  `useNoteSearch` whose helper always returns null. Zero callers today. Left
  alone deliberately; flagged rather than fixed inside this task.

## Decisions required

1. **Does dictation ship at all for the Venue Edition?** Three options are in
   the packet. This is a genuine founder call about a privacy boundary, not an
   engineering preference.
2. **Is the disclosure wording sufficient, or does the couple privacy notice
   need a matching clause?** E03 owns the notice; this task owns the surface.
