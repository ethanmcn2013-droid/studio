# E01.04 — Lock project scope and explicitly exclude internal venue operations, full-scale schools, full-scale students and unrelated product expansion.

**Epic:** E01 — Project governance and control

---

## Objective

Fix the scope boundary, record the exclusions the founder ratified, and make new
scope enterable only through a change request.

## Business reason

One founder, 211 tasks, 29 days. Scope arriving by accretion is the ordinary way
a programme like this misses its date, and it arrives disguised as a good idea.
The exclusion list is what lets a later session say no with a citation.

## Intended outcome

Anything not traceable to an epic is out until a change request puts it in, and
the five exclusions are written where a session will actually read them.

## Deliverables

- [x] The exclusions recorded in `PROJECT.md` §10 and ratified in D-008
- [x] The fifth exclusion the founder added: no bespoke development for any individual founding venue
- [x] A change-request route that is the only way new scope enters
- [x] An assessment of whether the boundary is enforced or merely stated

## Scope

The scope statement, the exclusion list, and the change-control route.

## Non-goals

- Not the backlog contents. The 211 tasks are the approved scope per D-019.
- Not the release definition. That is E01.03.
- Not deferral decisions on individual tasks.

## Implementation approach

Assess. The exclusions are written and ratified. Verify the fifth exclusion is
present everywhere the first four are, that the change-request template and
procedure exist and were actually used at least once, and that the boundary would
survive a reader who wanted to add something.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `PROJECT.md` §10 lists all five exclusions, including the founder's addition of no bespoke development for any individual founding venue.
2. D-008 records the founder's ratification of the exclusion list.
3. New scope has exactly one documented entry route, the change request, and that route is written in both `PROJECT.md` §20 and `WORKFLOWS.md` §5.
4. The change-request route has been exercised at least once in practice, not just documented.
5. The no-bespoke-development boundary is stated as a benefit in the Founding Venue Benefits Charter scope, per D-014, rather than surfacing later as a refusal.
6. The assessment names anything in the exclusion set that is stated in one place but missing from another.

## Validation plan

- Read `PROJECT.md` §9, §10, §20 and compare against D-008 point 1 and D-014 point 2 line by line
- Confirm `templates/CHANGE_REQUEST.md` exists and `evidence/change-requests/CR-001.md` is a real worked instance
- Grep the control root for the five exclusions to find any place stating only four

## Evidence required

- `PROJECT.md` §10 as it stands
- D-008 and D-014
- CR-001 as proof the change route works in practice

## Risks

The fifth exclusion was added after `PROJECT.md` §10 was written, so the risk is
drift between the ratified list and the charter. That is exactly what the
validation plan tests for.
