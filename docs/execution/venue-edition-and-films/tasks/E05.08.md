## Objective

Establish, with evidence and a regression test, what the authenticated Timeline
gives a sponsored couple today, where the private plan ends and the published
artifact begins, and what remains before a couple can reach either.

## Business reason

Timeline is the hero surface of both films. A venue's whole proposition is that
the couple's planning stays theirs. If an unpublished plan could ever be read by
the venue that paid for the workspace, the offer is over. This task turns that
promise from a stated intention into a test that fails loudly.

## Intended outcome

Three things are true that were not true before:

1. The private-to-published boundary is pinned by an executable test that fails
   when the publish gate is removed.
2. The reachability blocker (a couple cannot obtain a Timeline workspace at all)
   is recorded with independent evidence rather than inherited from an earlier
   audit.
3. The one decision that gates the fix is written down as options, not guessed.

## Implementation approach

Audit first (D-015 Q2). Nothing in the Timeline module was rewritten. One new
test file and one test-infrastructure pair were added. The boundary code is
correct and was left alone; what it lacked was a test, and the test was written
so that deleting the gate makes it fail.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A publication in `draft` state never resolves for a bearer holding a valid,
   active, unexpired share token.
2. Moving a publication to `published` is the only state change that opens the
   boundary, and moving it to `unpublished` closes it again for the same token.
3. A revoked share cannot read a published publication.
4. The published projection carries presentation fields only: no source
   workspace id, no source relation, no digest, no workspace slug, no owner
   identity, no sponsor reference.
5. The relationship between Tasks and Timeline is stated exactly: which tasks
   become Timeline milestones, under what condition, and what a sponsored couple
   actually gets today.
6. Every venue-facing read path out of this app is enumerated, and none of them
   reads any Timeline table.
7. The reason a sponsored couple cannot reach the authenticated Timeline is
   proven independently, not inherited, and the fix is stated as options.
