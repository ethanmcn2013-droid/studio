## Objective

Report what the code actually does about retention, deletion and export; draft the
policy to what is enforceable; and write the legitimate interests assessments the
role map says to write regardless of how the lawful-basis question resolves.

## Business reason

Five different retention statements are live or recorded, at most one can be true,
and three of them are on public pages a couple can read today. Twenty-five venues
are about to be told that couples get a one-click export they own. A retention
period that nothing enforces is a false statement, and this is the task that stops
one going into an agreement.

## Intended outcome

Every retention rule in the project carries an explicit enforcement state that was
verified in the repository. The conflicts are enumerated rather than reconciled by
guess. Three written assessments exist, so the Article 6(1)(f) reading has its
apparatus whichever way the basis question resolves.

## Implementation approach

Audit first, policy second, and keep them in separate sections so a reader can
never mistake one for the other. Mark every rule ENFORCED, BUILT BUT NOT RUNNING,
or NOT BUILT. Write the assessments in the structure a supervisory authority
expects, and mark each safeguard with whether it exists, because an assessment
whose safeguards are aspirational is worse than none if it does not say so.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Both documents open with a visible DRAFT status block naming what they are, what
   they are not, and that no solicitor has reviewed them, carry the date, and
   reproduce the E09.10 Answer 2 sentence verbatim.
2. Every retention rule carries an explicit enforcement state of ENFORCED, BUILT BUT
   NOT RUNNING, or NOT BUILT, verified in the repository, and no period appears as a
   control that no code enforces.
3. The 24-month sweep is reported accurately: implemented in
   `studio/src/lib/entitlements-db/gdpr.ts`, called only from
   `/api/cron/access-reconcile`, and absent from `studio/vercel.json`, so it does
   not run.
4. The five conflicting retention statements are enumerated with their source and
   their enforcement state, a sixth version is not created, and the choice between
   them is carried as an open founder decision.
5. Deletion is described only as far as it is built, and names the verified defect
   that attachment bytes are not removed from object storage because
   `account-erasure.ts` unlinks a local filesystem path while production stores a
   blob URL, together with R-023 and the absent third-party removal route.
6. The role map §12 item 3 rule (special-category data, guest contact details and
   children's data purged at term end whichever lawful basis wins) is drafted
   firmly, with its NOT BUILT state stated in the same place.
7. Service-discontinuation commitments are proposed rather than asserted, and the
   Keepsake export is described accurately: the rules and the artifact exist, and
   there is no route, no download button and no read-only enforcement.
8. Three legitimate interests assessments exist in purpose, necessity, balancing,
   safeguards and outcome structure; every safeguard is marked built or not built;
   and none of them claims to supply an Article 9(2) condition.
9. Opt-out and suppression requirements are stated, and the suppression list is
   recorded as manual with no store anywhere in the repository.
10. Every proposal that needs a founder answer is marked PROPOSED and listed, and
    every point a solicitor must settle appears as a question.
