## Objective

Make the wedding template able to express restrained milestones and real
wedding-relative dates, and put the template's content questions, including
R-017, to the founder rather than answering them here.

## Business reason

The template is the first thing a sponsored couple sees. It is also the only
thing that can fill the couple's Timeline, which is the hero surface of the
"Before the Day" film. A template that cannot declare a milestone produces an
empty Timeline and a Signal briefing with no dated item, which is two of the
four products silent on day one.

## Intended outcome

A template task can be declared a milestone and given a due date measured from
the wedding day, and applying the template writes both to the database. The
shipped template's actual content is documented, honestly assessed against the
restraint bar, and its one Article 9 question is guarded by a machine check and
put to the founder.

## Implementation approach

Audit first. Establish that the generated file is a build artifact and locate
its generator and source. Assess the shipped content against a stated restraint
criterion. Then build only what the app repo owns: the ability of a template to
declare a milestone and a wedding-relative date, and a machine check that makes
any future special-category addition visible.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A written audit states how many tasks, milestones, decisions and example
   Notes the shipped template produces, each with a file and line.
2. The audit answers plainly whether the current template is restrained,
   against a stated criterion.
3. The canonical source and the build artifact are identified, the generator's
   resolved source path is recorded, and content changes are shown to belong in
   the canonical source.
4. A template task can declare a Timeline milestone, and applying the template
   writes `tasks.is_milestone` accordingly, proven by a test.
5. A template task can declare a due date relative to the wedding date, and
   applying the template writes a real `tasks.due_at` when the workspace has an
   anchor date, proven by a test.
6. When no anchor date exists, no `due_at` is written and no date is invented,
   proven by a test.
7. A machine check fails when a new task instructing collection of
   special-category guest data is added to the wedding template, proven by a
   fail-before run.
8. R-017 is neither silently removed nor redesigned; handling options with
   trade-offs are put to the founder.
9. The template seeds example Notes for the couple.
10. The template seeds decisions.

**Criteria 9 and 10 are UNMET.** Reasons in the evidence document, section 5.

## Decisions required

   moves earlier. Task dates are a separate question with the opposite intuition.
