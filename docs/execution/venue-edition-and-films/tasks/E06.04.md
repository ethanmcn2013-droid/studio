## Objective

Prove the shipped milestone navigation meets a written bar, fix the copy defects
found doing it, and state plainly that the photograph-and-story half cannot be
built yet and why.

## Business reason

This is the interaction a guest performs on the couple's page and the one the
film shows. It is also the only part of E06 where a large amount of good work
already exists, so the value here is converting it into evidence rather than
rebuilding it. D-015 Q2 made that the largest single lever on the critical path.

## Intended outcome

- The keyboard and screen-reader contract is written down and asserted.
- Two front-facing sentences that broke the brand voice are gone from a couple's
  wedding page.
- The dependency on E06.03 and E06.02 is stated as a dependency rather than
  worked around with a placeholder frame.

## Implementation approach

Audit first, and build only what the audit exposes. The navigation half is built,
complete and covered; rewriting it would be exactly the "do not rewrite working
code to look like your idea of the task" failure D-015 Q2 warns about. The audit
found one real defect, in copy rather than behaviour, and that is what was fixed.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The dependency is stated rather than worked around: there is no photograph and
   no story to transition into until E06.03 and E06.02 land, and no placeholder
   frame ships.
2. The existing keyboard contract survives unchanged: Left and Right between
   milestones, Home and End to jump, Enter or Space to select, Escape to close,
   with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` on the selected point and a polite
   live region announcing open and closed.
3. `prefers-reduced-motion` is honoured, and the component reads it through a
   hydration-safe hook so server and first client render agree.
4. A milestone with no photograph and no story degrades to today's detail panel
   with no empty frame and no layout shift.
5. The axe gate stays clean: zero critical and zero serious violations at 390,
   768, 1280 and 1440.
6. `detailNote`'s three generated sentences are either replaced or kept
   deliberately, and they pass the brand voice. They are front-facing copy on a
   couple's wedding page.
