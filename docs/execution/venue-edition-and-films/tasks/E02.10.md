# E02.10 — Select legally safe programme terminology, establish Founding Venue numbers 01/25 through 25/25, and define when numbers are assigned.

**Task ID:** E02.10 · **Epic:** E02 · **Status:** Founder Review
**Executor:** claude_code · **Approver:** Ethan McNamara · **Spec written:** 2026-08-03 (WP-10)

## Objective
Fix the programme's vocabulary and its numbering rule.

## Business reason
Words used with 25 businesses become the offer. "Partner" implies shared liability. "Member" implies a scheme with rights of continuation. "Exclusive" implies a protected patch that 25 venues in one region cannot all have.

## Intended outcome
`FOUNDING_25_PROGRAMME_MECHANICS.md` sections 1 and 2: a say/never-say table with the reason for each ban, and numbering defined as `NN/25` assigned on cleared payment.

## Deliverables
- [x] Terminology table with the reason each banned word is banned
- [x] Numbering format `01/25` to `25/25`, zero-padded
- [x] Assignment on cleared payment, ordered by clearing time, with tie-breaks
- [x] Numbers never reused, and a withdrawal rule when a payment reverses
- [x] The scarcity-claims rule: every published statement about places must be true when it is sent

## Scope
Terminology and numbering.

## Non-goals
Certificate design (E12.11). Building the register.

## Acceptance criteria
1. "Founding 25" is the programme name, and the retired fifteen-venue forms are banned and mechanically checked.
2. Each banned term carries a stated reason, including the explicit position that no territorial exclusivity is granted.
3. Numbers run 01/25 to 25/25 and are assigned on payment, never on signature, recorded in the machine contract as `numberAssignedOn: "payment"`.
4. Tie-breaking, non-reuse and payment reversal all have decided answers.
5. No permanence wording outside the document that defines the ban.

## Validation plan
`node scripts/check-venue-edition-contract.mjs`, and the `commercial-terms.test.ts` assertion on `numberAssignedOn`.

## Evidence required
The mechanics document, the check output, the passing test.

## Risks
The terminology bans are drafted without a solicitor (D-016, R-013). They are framed as plain-language risk avoidance and state no legal conclusion.

## Decisions required
None. D-009 point 6 set the name and the assignment event.
