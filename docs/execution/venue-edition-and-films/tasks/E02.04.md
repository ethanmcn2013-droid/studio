# E02.04 — Define continuous-renewal requirements and what happens after a missed payment, cancellation, lapse or later reactivation.

## Objective

Put a recommendation to the founder that defines, in words that can go into the
annual agreement, what "renews continuously" requires, what happens when a
payment is missed, what a lapse costs, and what a returning venue is offered.

## Business reason

D-009 point 3 holds the €1,000 founding rate "for as long as the agreement
renews continuously without lapse". That sentence is the entire founding offer.
Until "continuously" has a definition, nobody can say when a venue loses €500 a
year, and the agreement that carries the promise cannot be drafted. This is the
most consequential unwritten term in the programme.

## Intended outcome

The renewal notice period, the grace window, what changes during grace, what a
lapse does, and what a returning venue is offered are all decided and recorded.
`VENUE_RENEWAL_POLICY.ratified` can move to `true` and
`foundingRateOnReturn()` can stop returning an unsettled marker.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every element of the renewal path already ratified is listed with the
   decision that ratified it, and nothing ratified is reopened.
2. The genuinely open questions are stated as questions with no invented answer,
   and each is answerable by the founder in one line.
3. At least two materially different options are presented for the grace regime,
   with the cost of each stated in euro or in relationship terms, and one clear
   recommendation with its reasoning.
4. The conflict between `foundingRateOnReturn()` (unsettled) and the two
   strategy documents (standard rate on return) is recorded as a conflict, with
   file and line references, and is not silently reconciled.
5. The brief states, with a verified code reference, that no production code path
   enforces "new issuance stops on lapse" today.
6. The recommended agreement wording contains no permanence term
   ("for life", "lifetime", "in perpetuity") and passes brand voice: no em
   dashes, no exclamation marks, plain English.
7. Nothing in the brief states or implies legal approval, solicitor review or
   accountant verification (D-016).
8. The brief states plainly that no renewal falls due before roughly September
   2027, with the arithmetic shown, and draws the right conclusion from it: fix
   the wording now, do not build the machinery now.

## Risks

- **R-021 / R-013.** A grace window written into 25 agreements is binding. Too
  short loses a founding venue to a slow accounts department. Too long gives
  service away and weakens the condition the lock depends on.
- **R-008.** A lock with no enforceable condition reads as a permanent
  unconditional promise, which D-001 point 16 forbids.
- Renewal notices are sent by hand and there is no sender. Signal Studio can
  fail to send the notice that triggers the venue's payment. Any rule that
  penalises the venue for that failure is indefensible.
