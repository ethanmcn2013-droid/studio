# E15.07 — Release Cohort 1 and record every personalised touch and response.

---

## Objective

Contact the twenty-five venues in Cohort 1, one at a time and personally, and
record every touch and every response in one place that survives the session.

## Business reason

Cohort 1 is the only first impression available. D-015 Q1 defines "release on
1 September" as ready to contact Cohort 1, so this task is the release. Twenty-
five personalised approaches is also the largest single demand on founder
capacity in the programme (R-006), and an unrecorded send is a send that gets
repeated or contradicted.

## Intended outcome

Twenty-five venues contacted, every touch dated and attributed, every response
recorded with its next action, and no venue contacted twice by accident or
handled with a different story than another.

## Deliverables

- [ ] The released Cohort 1 send list, frozen at release.
- [ ] A touch log with a row per contact, per venue.
- [ ] A response log with the venue's words, not a summary.
- [ ] The pipeline state after the cohort has run.

## Scope

Cohort 1 as built in `venue-universe/05-COHORTS.md`, the outreach email, the
personalised film link, the proposal page, and the CRM stages from E11.

## Non-goals

- Contacting anyone outside the ratified ring. A short cohort reports as short.
- Contacting two properties of one operator in the same cohort.
- Any promise outside the ratified position: EUR 1,000 inclusive of VAT at the
  prevailing rate, held while the agreement renews continuously, number assigned
  on cleared payment, fourteen-day hold.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every venue in the released cohort is contacted once, with the send dated and
   the channel recorded. No venue is contacted twice by accident.
2. No two properties of the same operator appear in the same released cohort.
3. Every response is recorded in the venue's own words with a date and a next
   action, including the refusals and the silences.
4. Every outbound asset used carries the ratified position and none implies a
   venue logo or venue-written words appear in the couple's workspace
   (D-027 point 3).
5. No asset uses "partner", "member", "investor", "exclusive", "guaranteed" or
   "certified", and no scarcity claim can go stale.
6. Any founding place held is held for fourteen days from the proposal, once,
   and the hold is recorded with its expiry date.
7. The go/no-go decision (E15.01) is recorded before the first send.
8. Nothing sent states or implies legal approval or GDPR compliance (D-016).

## Risks

- **R-006** — founder capacity. Twenty-five personalised approaches plus the
  onboarding that follows the first yes.
- **R-020** — the moment a venue says yes, an untrained reseller starts
  describing the product. The marketing-controls clause has to be in the
  agreement before the first signature, not after.
- **R-024** — the first onboarding is improvised unless it is rehearsed first.
- E13.17 is a dependency and E13 is 4/18 with 28 days to release.

## Decisions required

1. Does Cohort 1 go without the personalised film if E13 does not land?
2. Is the send sequenced over days, or released at once? Twenty-five replies in
   one week against one founder is the capacity question in practice.
