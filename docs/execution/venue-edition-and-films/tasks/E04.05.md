# E04.05 — Define how venue name, logo, welcome message and “compliments of” attribution inherit into each sponsored workspace.

**Task ID:** E04.05
**Epic:** E04 — Venue Edition product architecture and workspace lifecycle
**Status:** Founder Review

---

## Objective

Define the branding inheritance path and record that only the name half of it ships.

## Business reason

The venue is buying a gift with its name on it. If the logo and welcome message have nowhere to live, the thing being sold does not exist yet.

## Intended outcome

Both sponsor-identity paths are documented, the unschema'd brandMeta blob is named as the blocker, and the attribution rule from D-011 is written down.

## Deliverables

- [ ] studio/docs/architecture/ADR-008-venue-edition-lifecycle.md section 5, the inheritance path and the two parallel identity sources
- [ ] The D-011 point 2 attribution rule stated as an architectural constraint

## Scope

Documenting the flow from sponsors.brandMeta and comp_codes.notes through to the welcome card, and the public-artifact attribution rule.

## Non-goals

Designing a brandMeta schema. Building logo upload. Writing venue welcome copy. Those are E05 and E07.

## Dependencies

| Dependency | State | Waived? |
|---|---|---|
| E04.01 | in this package | no |

## Inputs

studio/src/lib/entitlements-db/schema.ts brandMeta, app/src/server/db/venue-welcome.ts, app/src/components/welcome/venue-welcome-card.tsx, D-011 point 2.

## Implementation approach

Audit both identity paths and state the reconciliation question rather than picking silently.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The two parallel sponsor-identity paths are both described, with paths.
2. brandMeta is stated to have no schema, no type and no consumer.
3. The D-011 attribution rule appears as a constraint on the public artifact: one footer line, no logo, no badge in the viewport.
4. The logo and welcome message are stated as having no storage today.

## Validation plan

Reviewer greps brandMeta across both repos and finds only the cited sites.

## Evidence required

- studio/docs/architecture/ADR-008-venue-edition-lifecycle.md

## Risks

R-007 and R-020. A venue that supplies a logo which never appears has been sold something the product does not do.

## Decisions required

Which identity path becomes canonical is a design choice; options are in the packet.
