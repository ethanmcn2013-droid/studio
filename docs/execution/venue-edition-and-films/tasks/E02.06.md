## Objective

State what the €1,000 and €1,500 prices do not include, in words that can go
into the annual agreement, and record that the first word of this task's own
title has been superseded by a later decision.

## Business reason

A venue paying annually in advance forms a view of what it has bought at the
moment it signs. Every gap in that view is an argument in year two, and with 25
venues the same gap becomes 25 arguments. Signal Studio has written down what a
venue gets in detail and has never written down where it stops.

## Intended outcome

Two different statements exist and are not confused with each other: what the
price buys, and what the held rate covers. The second is already written. The
first is not, and this task produces it.

## Scope

The scope of supply for the annual Venue Edition agreement: what €1,000 and
€1,500 buy, and what they do not.

## Non-goals

- Restating the VAT basis. D-021 settled it and this task does not reopen it.
- Deciding the VAT treatment. That is a Revenue question, unfiled, and it is
  E02.07's item.
- Changing the task title. Imported titles are never rewritten. The conflict is

- Inventing add-on products. Nothing that does not exist is named as if it does.

## Implementation approach

Separate the two statements the task title runs together. Audit what is already
promised as included so no exclusion contradicts it. Propose a short list rather
than a schedule, because a long exclusion list at €1,000 reads as a product with
hidden costs. Surface the one edge nobody has decided, rather than deciding it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The brief states that D-021 supersedes the "exclude VAT" instruction in the
   task title, names the decision, and does not write a VAT-exclusive term
   anywhere.
2. No proposed wording says or implies that VAT is added to the published price.
3. The distinction between scope of supply and scope of the lock is stated
   explicitly, with the existing charter sentence identified as the second.
4. Every proposed exclusion is checked against the published inclusion list and
   none contradicts something already promised, with the check shown.
5. At least two materially different options are presented, with costs, and one
   clear recommendation.
6. At least one genuinely undecided edge is raised as a question rather than
   answered.
7. All proposed wording passes brand voice: no em dashes, no exclamation marks,
   no banned programme terms, sentence case, plain English.
8. Nothing states or implies legal approval, solicitor review or accountant
   verification (D-016), and nothing claims a confirmed VAT position.

## Risks

- **R-022, accepted.** VAT-inclusive pricing under a permanent lock. A rate rise
  is absorbed by Signal Studio. Any exclusion wording that appears to pass VAT on
  would contradict D-021 and is refused here.
- **R-020.** Twenty-five untrained venues describing the product. An exclusion
  the venue never read is one it will describe wrongly to a couple.
- A list written too long makes a €1,000 product read as a product with hidden
  costs. That is a commercial risk, not a legal one, and it drives the
  recommendation.
