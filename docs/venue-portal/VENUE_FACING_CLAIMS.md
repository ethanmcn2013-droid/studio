# Signal Studio Account · venue-facing claims (filed as "Venue Portal")

Naming: the **Signal Studio Account IS the Venue Portal** (**D-015 Q4**). One
surface, two names.

The Account may describe what Signal Studio can prove. It must not turn product
activity into a surveillance score or an invented business outcome.

**Corrected 2026-08-03.** The permitted sentences in this document were written
against a finite issuance budget. **D-020** abolished that budget and **E09.02
§8** states in terms that these examples are replaced. The old set is kept below,
struck through, because nothing in this programme is superseded silently.

## Prohibited

Unchanged:

- “Your most active couple is…”
- “Alex and Sam used Tasks 42 times.”
- “This couple is disengaged.”
- “Signal Studio saved 120 planning hours.”
- “Your Venue Edition generated €14,000 in value.”
- “Every couple is using the platform.”
- “Signal Studio improved wedding outcomes.”
- “Usage is down” when coverage or denominators changed.
- Any note, task, project, briefing, comment, attachment, collaborator, or
  private Timeline content.

**Now also prohibited under D-020 and E09.02 §8**, by name:

- “codes remaining”
- “licences allotted”
- “low allotment”
- any sentence containing “X of your 60”
- any seat count, cap, or numeric pause shown beside the word unlimited

**Prohibited under D-027 point 3** (venue branding at launch is the venue's name
only). No sentence anywhere may state or imply that a venue's logo or a venue's
own written words appear in the couple's workspace. What appears is the venue's
name.

**Prohibited under D-016.** No sentence may state or imply that any part of this
project has been legally approved, reviewed by a solicitor, or verified by an
accountant.

**Prohibited under D-009 point 3, D-001 point 16 and R-008.** Never “for life”,
never “forever”, never “lifetime”, never “guaranteed”. The founding rate holds
“for as long as your agreement renews without lapse”.

**Prohibited by construction.** No number that resolves to a single couple at
any cohort size, and no denominator drawn from bookings.

## Report footer

Every PDF and CSV report includes:

> Usage means committed actions in Signal Notes, Signal Tasks, Signal Timeline,
> or Signal. Page visits do not count. Private work is never included. Small
> groups are withheld. Coverage and definitions appear with each reporting
> period.

## Sales boundary

Sales and renewal conversations may say:

- ~~how many licences were available and redeemed~~ `[SUPERSEDED D-020 ·
  2026-08-03]` how many invitations were sent and how many were redeemed;
- how many sponsored workspaces crossed the first-useful-action definition;
- how often aggregate meaningful use occurred;
- whether observed continuation crossed the versioned band definition.

They may not convert those facts into ROI, time saved, satisfaction, or causal
claims without a separate, consented research method. They may not compare one
venue with another: cohort composition differs, so there is no league table, no
benchmark, and no “above average for your size”.

**One thing a venue is told once, plainly.** A low percentage can mean couples
are not using it, or it can mean the venue invited couples who were not ready.
Signal Studio cannot tell those apart and will not pretend to.
