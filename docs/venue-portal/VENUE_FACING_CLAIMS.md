# Venue-facing claims

The portal may describe what Signal Studio can prove. It must not turn product
activity into a surveillance score or an invented business outcome.

## Permitted when coverage is complete

- "18 of 24 issued codes were redeemed this term."
- "12 sponsored workspaces used Signal Studio on 21 distinct days in the last
  30 days."
- "Tasks was used in 10 sponsored workspaces. Notes in 8. Timeline in 6.
  Signal in 5."
- "9 of 12 eligible sponsored workspaces returned around day 30."
- "The latest complete usage data is through 24 July 2026."

Each claim includes its denominator, reporting window, coverage state, and
metric dictionary version in the report notes.

## Permitted with partial coverage

- "We observed at least 8 active sponsored workspaces. Usage coverage is
  partial for this period."
- "Access and redemption data is complete. Product use is unavailable for 3
  of 30 days."

Rates, changes, comparisons, and module rankings are withheld when coverage is
partial.

## Permitted when a group is too small

- "Not enough sponsored workspaces to report usage safely yet."
- "Redemptions are visible. Behavioural usage appears after at least three
  sponsored workspaces are eligible."

The hidden number does not appear in tooltips, exports, email, support view-as,
or accessibility text.

## Prohibited

- "Your most active couple is..."
- "Alex and Sam used Tasks 42 times."
- "This couple is disengaged."
- "Signal Studio saved 120 planning hours."
- "Your Venue Edition generated EUR 14,000 in value."
- "Every couple is using the platform."
- "Signal Studio improved wedding outcomes."
- "Usage is down" when coverage or denominators changed.
- Any note, task, project, briefing, comment, attachment, collaborator, or
  private Timeline content.

## Report footer

Every PDF and CSV report includes:

> Usage means committed actions in Signal Notes, Signal Tasks, Signal Timeline,
> or Signal. Page visits do not count. Private work is never included. Small
> groups are withheld. Coverage and definitions appear with each reporting
> period.

## Sales boundary

Sales and renewal conversations may say:

- how many licences were available and redeemed;
- how many sponsored workspaces crossed the activation definition;
- how often aggregate meaningful use occurred;
- whether observed retention crossed the versioned day-7/30/90 definitions.

They may not convert those facts into ROI, time saved, satisfaction, or causal
claims without a separate, consented research method.
