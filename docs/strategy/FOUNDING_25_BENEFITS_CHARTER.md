This is what a founding venue gets, and where each benefit stops. Every benefit
here carries its boundary in the same paragraph, on purpose. A benefit with no
edge becomes an argument in year two.

Written to be read by a venue owner. It is not a contract. The annual Venue
Edition agreement and the founding schedule are the binding documents, and where
this charter and an agreement disagree, the agreement wins.

---

## The six benefits

### 1 · The rate

**€1,000 a year, prepaid. €500 a year less, for as long as you stay.**

Both the founding rate and the standard €1,500 are inclusive of VAT at the
prevailing rate. The number you see is the number you pay.

The rate covers the base annual Venue Edition agreement. It holds for as long as
that agreement renews continuously, without a lapse. There is no clause that
expires it into a higher number and no review date that quietly resets it.

It follows the property, not the company. If the venue is sold, rebranded, or
changes hands, the rate stays with the venue as long as the agreement keeps
running. It does not travel to other properties a new owner already holds.

**Where it stops.** A lapse ends it. If the agreement is cancelled or a renewal
is missed and the account closes, coming back means coming back at the standard
rate. Products that are materially separate from Venue Edition are not covered
by it.

### 2 · Your number

**A place in the Founding 25, numbered 01/25 through 25/25.**

Your number is assigned when your first payment clears, not when you sign.
Payment is the only step that cannot be walked back, which is what makes the
number worth having. Whoever pays first takes the lower number.

A place is held for 14 days from the date you receive a proposal. One hold, and
Ethan can extend it if you ask him.

**Where it stops.** The programme closes at 25 paid venues. There is no 26th
place, no second founding round, and no later reopening at the founding rate.

### 3 · Founder access

**One 30-minute call a year with Ethan, plus an email route that reaches him
directly.**

The call is yours to use on whatever is useful: what the planning year looks like
from your side, what your coordinator keeps repeating, what the product should do
next. Email gets a reply within one working day.

**Where it stops.** This is one call a year and an email address, not a standing
arrangement, not a retainer, and not an account manager. Signal Studio is one
person. The boundary is what keeps the reply times honest across 25 venues.

### 4 · First look

**You see new work before it ships, and you can say what should change while it
still can be changed.**

Founding venues get the work in progress, not a launch announcement. That is the
window where an opinion actually moves something.

**Where it stops.** First look is not a veto. Twenty-five venues will not agree,
and a product built by committee is worse for all of them.

### 5 · Your requests, on the record

**Every founding request is written down and shapes what gets built.**

Requests from founding venues are logged and go into the roadmap ahead of
requests from anywhere else. You can ask what happened to yours and get a
straight answer, including no.

**Where it stops, and this is stated plainly rather than discovered later:
nothing is built for one venue.** A request that only helps you does not get
built. A request that helps you and would help the next twenty-four does, and
that is the test. Bespoke work for a single venue is the fastest way to a product
that serves nobody well, and it is outside the agreement.

### 6 · Recognition

**Your name on the founding page and the founding map, and a numbered certificate
for the wall.**

Recognition is a choice, not a condition. Consent to use your name and logo is
asked for separately, in writing, and you can withdraw it. Nothing about the rate
or the place depends on saying yes.

**Where it stops.** Recognition is your venue's name and logo in the founding
programme. It is not a joint marketing commitment, a case study, or an
endorsement, and none of those are assumed from a founding place.

## What every venue gets, founding or not

These are not founding benefits. They are the product, and they are here so the
charter is not read as the whole offer.

- **Every couple who books their wedding with you gets a workspace**, for as long
  as your licence is current. No seat count, no per-couple arithmetic, nothing
  for your coordinator to track.
- **Each couple keeps their workspace for 18 months from the day they open it, or
  3 months past the wedding, whichever is later.** A long engagement does not run
  out before the day.
- **A workspace a couple has already opened keeps its full term whatever happens
  to your licence.** If you stop, only new invitations stop. Nobody planning a
  wedding loses anything.
- **Your venue's name sits quietly at the top of the workspace.** The couple's
  plan stays the thing in focus.
- **You never see inside a couple's planning.** You see invitations, activation
  and entitlement state. Notes, tasks and an unpublished timeline are theirs.
- **Nothing for your team to run.** No admin panel, no accounts to manage, no
  support queue. A couple with a problem contacts Signal Studio.

## What we ask of you

Two things, both small.

**Describe it the way it is.** The venue pack carries approved wording for your
website, your brochure and your coordinator. Please use it. Signal Studio takes
care with what it promises couples, and a claim it never made is still a claim
someone has to keep. In particular, nothing about the product is permanent or
guaranteed to last forever, and it should never be described that way.

**Tell us when it is wrong.** A founding venue that stays quiet about a problem
is worth less to the programme than one that says so on the call.

---

## What this charter is not

It does not create a partnership, an agency, or any shared ownership. It does not
give a founding venue a stake in Signal Studio. It does not promise the service
runs indefinitely, and no part of it should be read as that promise.

Nothing in this document has been reviewed by a solicitor or an accountant, and
nothing in it states or implies otherwise.
